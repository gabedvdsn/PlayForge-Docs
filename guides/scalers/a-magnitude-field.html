A magnitude field

A magnitude field, such as an effect's Magnitude or Duration, is a number field (ScalableFloat, or ScalableInt for a whole number). It shows its name, then its kind, then what it reads, then its field menu, which opens as wide as its items. The kind menu lists Constant, Table, By Level, Shared, × Attribute and Set By Caller at its top, then the kinds that read the game (below): Asset Part, Group, Group Impact, Local Data, Relative to a Field and Worker. It files the rest by category: the scalers of every kind under Scaler, Effect Stacks and Tracked Impact under Effect, Item Stacks under Item, and a kind of a project's own under the category it names. A field whose code declares what its context provides, with [NumberContext], offers only the kinds that can be worked out there: a targeting task's range, which has no target yet, offers no kind that reads one.

Every number an asset authors is a number field, read at the level of what holds it: an ability's, as its Buffer Window and its tasks', at its level; an effect's, as its Stack Bounds' Min and Max, its Starting Stacks and a Stacks change's, at the effect's; a cue's Strength, Duration, Lifetime and Volume, for its effect as it lands; an item's Max Stack Size at its own level, and a set bonus's Pieces at its holder's; and an entity's own, as a barrier's Decay Per Second, an attribute set's Snap Interval, an aura's Interval and a worker's Threshold, Floor, Share, Every Ticks, Tick Pause or Required Weight, at the entity's or the change's. A level, a pointer's button, an order, a priority among configs and a log's size stay whole numbers, as do a kind's and a scaler's own numbers. A folded field's line shows a number field by what it reads, as "Uses 3".

  • Constant: the field reads its number, with its unit beside it, as Tick Every's "1 s". A field that used to read its scaler, and has none, reads 0, and a warning says so.

  • Table: the field reads one value for each level, exactly: level 1 the first, level 4 the 4th, and a level past the last the last, as Laguna Blade's 450, 600 and 750. The range it gives shows beside the kind, as "6–8 s", or "-25 to -10" where it goes below 0. Its values sit in a row under it, with no curve.

  • By Level: the field reads its values spread over a level range, as points on a curve, a level between two reading between them (below). It's the same number by level as a Table, read another way, so choosing one for the other keeps its values; one saved reading exactly shows as a Table, and nothing saved changes. Spreadsheets writes a project's Tables and By Levels to a spreadsheet and reads them back.

  • Shared: the field reads a Scaler asset (below).

  • The other kinds work out a value from the values or from the entity: × Attribute reads an attribute of the caster or the target, its current, its base, what's missing of it (base − current) or its share of its base (current ÷ base), which the menu starts as the field's number times it, all of it or only some of its retention groups (below), and reads it as its attribute's role says, a Bonus Share as 1 + x and a Resistance as 1 − x, or as its Read As says, Clamped keeps a number within bounds, Conditional reads one number where a condition holds and another where it doesn't, Randomized rolls a random value, Threshold steps at thresholds, and Fixed Value is one number. Stacking Curve turns a Count, as an item's stacks or an attribute, into its number by a Curve and its Coefficient, a: Linear, a × n, as 15% a stack; Hyperbolic, 1 − 1 / (1 + a × n), a chance that nears 1 and never reaches it, 13% with one stack and 60% with ten at 0.15; Reciprocal, 1 / (1 + a × n), as what Dota's armor leaves of a hit at 0.06; Inverse, a / n, as a wind-up an attack speed shortens; and Exponential, a to the n. Its Plus is added after, 1 making a share a multiplier. Effect Stacks reads the stack count of the effect's own container, and Item Stacks that of the item that applied it (Items); each reads 1 where there's nothing stacked. An attribute set's values use the cached kinds, which read other attributes as the entity's values change.

  • Set By Caller reads a value the code passes in. It checks one thing: whether what the number is read in holds a value under exactly its Key. Code sets one on an effect's spec before applying it (spec.SetByCaller(tag, value)), on an ability activation's data (data.SetByCaller(tag, value)), which each effect the ability makes takes and the ability's tasks read, or on the data that spawns a process (Effects). Only the key itself matches, not a tag above or below it in the tag tree, and a value set to 0 is a value. Where none is set, the field reads the kind's Default; its summary says both, as "the caller's Damage, else 0".

  • A Threshold lists its thresholds, each a Level and an optional Tier name, with the Value it reads from that level up under them, a number of any kind; below every threshold it reads its Default Value.

  • A Conditional has a condition, When, and two numbers, True Value and False Value: it reads True where the condition holds, and False where it doesn't, or where it has none. Its summary says all three, as "−0.1 × Target's Health (base) if Target's Health < 0.3 × Target's Health (base), else 0". A condition picks its kind from a menu, as a number does, and says itself in a line:

    • Tag: whether the caster or the target holds a tag, counted by its weight, so a tag granted twice counts twice;
    • Attribute: an attribute of the caster or the target, its current, its base, what's missing of it or its share of its base, compared with a number, as 0.3 × the target's base Health, or a share above 0.9 for "above 90%";
    • Level: the level, or how far up its range it is, from 0 to 1, compared with a number;
    • Compare Numbers: one number compared with another;
    • Always and Never;
    • and under Logic, All Of, Any Of and Not, which join others. All Of none holds; Any Of none doesn't.

    Where what the number is read in lacks what a condition reads, as a target's tag where there's no target, the condition doesn't hold, and neither does Not it.

  • A Clamped keeps its Value between its Min Value and Max Value, three numbers of any kind; one made in the inspector clamps a value by level.

  • A Randomized scaler's Variance is a kind of its own: Percentage, up or down by a Share of its value; Flat, by an Amount; Multiplier, times one between its Min and its Max; or one of your own, as two dice, a [Serializable] class made from AbstractVariance that overrides Vary, Lowest and Highest. Its numbers read the variance as their object, so a Share can widen by level and a Max can be 3 × its Min.

  • A scaler's own numbers read the scaler as their object: a Clamped's Max Value can be Relative to a Field, 2 × its Min Value, whatever field the Clamped is in.

  • A Group joins several numbers as one. It lists its members, each an Operation then its Scaler, which can be any kind. The members that Add are added up, that sum is multiplied by each member that Multiplies and divided by each that Divides, one that reads 0 dividing nothing, then each Flat Bonus is added on, in whatever order they're listed, so (5 + Caster's Strength) × 2 + 3 is one number, and 1.7 ÷ Caster's Attack Speed another, and a first member needn't add. Members that multiply or divide with none that adds work on 0, and the Group says so. A member that Overrides gives the group's value alone; where several do, the group's policy picks the highest, the lowest, their average, the first or the last, and Ignore sets them aside. A Group has no values per level. With no members, or none that holds a kind, it's no kind at all, so its field reads its number, as a Constant does.

Kinds that read the game, each with a summary that says what it reads:

  • Local Data reads the number under a Key in Local Data: Its Own, what the number belongs to, as its ability's or its item's asset, or the Caster's or the Target's as the entity holds it. Where there's no number under the key it reads its Default. A hero's attack range kept in its Local Data is one field read by every ability. The entry's number can scale too, read where the kind reads it: Its Own in the number's context, the Caster's or the Target's at that entity's own level. One that reads its own key stops at the Default.
  • Relative to a Field reads another number field of the same object, picked from a menu of them, and the field's number joins it as Combine says: a Clamped scaler's Max can be 2 × its Min, and a stack bound's Max its Min + 3. The other field is read in the same context, so a relative Max follows a Min that scales by level. A field offers this kind where its code hands its object over as it reads (below).
  • Tracked Impact, under Effect, reads what an effect has dealt: its Last impact, its Total, or its impact Count. Whose: This Effect, as a burn that grows by its own tick count; An Effect on the caster or the target, by its asset; or Effects Tagged there, added up. An impact is as it landed, after the target's workers, and signed, so damage is negative; By Size reads it positive, as a heal of half a blow wants. Before an effect's first impact, and after a load, it reads 0.
  • Group Impact reads one retention group's part of an attribute on the caster or the target, as "Caster's Strength (Bonus)": the Strength items add, and none of the hero's own.
  • Retention groups in any attribute read: × Attribute, the Attribute condition, a relative attribute worker's Relative To and the Threshold worker each have Groups: All, the whole value, as before; Only These, as a bonus of the items' Strength reads their group; or All But These, as a bonus of a hero's own damage leaves its items' out. A group names the groups under it too, so Items takes Items.Ring. The read says which: "Caster's Strength (Items)", "Caster's Attack Damage (all but Items)".
  • Asset Part finds effects, abilities or items on the caster or the target by a tag, and reads a part of them. An effect matches by its asset tag, a context tag or a tag it grants, and an ability or an item by its asset tag or a context tag; a tag under the one given matches too.
    • Of an Effect: its Count, Stacks, Level, Last Impact, Total Impact, Impact Count, Duration Remaining, Duration, Time Until Tick, or a Worker Value, what that effect's workers answer under a key.
    • Of an Ability: its Count, Level, Is Active, Is on Cooldown, or Cooldown Remaining.
    • Of an Item: its Count, Stacks, Level, or Is Equipped.
    • Among says how several that match read as one: their Sum, the First, the Highest or the Lowest. A true-or-false part reads 1 or 0, so its sum is how many are so. Where none matches, a count reads 0 and any other part the Default.
  • Worker asks workers for a number under a Key: This Effect's own workers, or the Caster's or the Target's. Where none answers it reads its Default. See Asking a worker, below.

Choosing a kind is one Undo step. A constant made a Table or By Level keeps its number as its first level's value.

With more than one asset selected, the field edits each asset's own field. Kinds that differ show as mixed, and choosing one gives it to each field, from that field's own value; a field of that kind keeps its scaler. Levels and Make Constant work on each field's own values, and a count, range or curve the fields disagree on shows as mixed. Save as Scaler Asset waits for one asset.

A kind other than Constant shows a one-line summary of what the field reads under its row, as "0.3 × Target's Health (base)", then Combine, how the field joins its number with the scaler: Scaler reads the scaler's value, Scaler + Number and Scaler × Number add or multiply the field's number, and Number Only reads the number. A whole-number field, such as Ticks, also shows its Rounding: Floor, Ceil, or Round, to the nearest, a half away from zero. A field whose unit is "%" stores 0.3 and shows and takes it as 30%. A kind its field can't work out, as one copied from a field that has a target into one that hasn't, says so under the field, and the Problems window lists it; it reads the field's number.

The field menu holds the items below. A scaler field of its own, as a Group member's, has the same menu, and its kind menu files its kinds by category, as a number field's does.

  • Save as Scaler Asset…: a name and a folder, then Save and Use, which saves the field's scaler as a Scaler asset and makes the field share it.
  • Scaler Assets: a submenu of the project's Scaler assets, the one the field shares checked. The field shares the one you pick.
  • Import Values…: a window that lists the Scaler assets and the other assets' fields with values per level. Pick one to compare its values with the field's, level by level, with both curves, then Copy Values. A shorter table's last value holds for the levels past its end, and the window says which levels those are. One Undo takes it back.
  • Quick Fill…: fills the table from a pattern: Same Value, Straight Line, Slow Then Fast and Fast Then Slow with a Strength, Steps, Add Each Level, Multiply Each Level, Ease In and Out, or a Custom Curve you draw, from one value to another. Rounding makes the values whole numbers or rounds them to a Step. A preview shows the values as the table will, with their curve, and one Undo takes them back. On a scaler spread over levels it fills the points, as many as Points says, and the preview shows what each level reads between them.
  • Make Constant: the field reads a number again, the value its first level read. A scaler field of its own, with no number, becomes a Fixed Value of that value.
