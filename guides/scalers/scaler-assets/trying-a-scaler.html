Trying a scaler

The asset's Try section reads it at each level of a range, Levels 1 to 5 to start, as an effect's Levels does. It shows a row of what it gives at each level, and under it the parts behind the level picked.

It reads against a stand-in Caster and Target, and a Caller. Each has a row for what the scaler asks of it, found as it reads.

Stand-in Its rows
Caster and Target An attribute on its side: whether the side Has it, its current and its base.
Caller A key the caller sets: whether it's Set, and its value. Unset reads the scaler's default.
  • A side's Entity fills its attributes from what that entity's attribute set starts each at, at its starting level. One the set doesn't give, it lacks.
  • A stand-in holds attributes alone. So a number that reads an effect, an item, a tag, a retention group's part or Local Data reads as on an entity with none of them.
  • What's typed is kept for the session, never in the asset.
  • ScalerTry does the same in code: new ScalerTry { From = 1, To = 5 }.Read(scaler).
