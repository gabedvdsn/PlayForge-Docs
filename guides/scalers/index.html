Scalers

A scaler is a value source that a magnitude field reads at a level, such as a table of damage per level. The level is the origin's, such as the level of the ability that applies an effect. Each section below matches what the inspector shows.

In this guide

  • A magnitude field: A magnitude field, such as an effect's Magnitude or Duration, is a number field (ScalableFloat, or ScalableInt for a whole number).
  • Values per level: A scaler's settings sit on a shaded block under its field, so they read as the scaler's among the fields around them.
  • Writing your own scaler: A scaler of your own derives from AbstractScaler, or from AbstractCachedScaler for an attribute set's values, and has a public constructor with no parameters.
  • A number field in your own code: A field of your own is one line, and a float converts to it
  • Asking a worker: A number can't count or remember; a worker can.
  • Scaler assets: A Scaler asset (Create → PlayForge → Scaler) holds one scaler that many fields can use.
  • Built-in kinds: The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says.
