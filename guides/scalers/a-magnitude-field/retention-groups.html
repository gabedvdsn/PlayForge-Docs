Retention groups

An attribute's value is the sum of its retention groups' parts: the base its set gives, what items add in their group, and what a buff adds in its.

Retention groups in any attribute read work alike. × Attribute, the Attribute condition, a relative attribute worker's Relative To and the Threshold worker each have Groups, which say which parts the read takes:

Groups What the read takes
All The whole value, as before.
Only These Only the groups named, as a bonus of the items' Strength reads their group.
All But These Every group but those named, as a bonus of a hero's own damage leaves its items' out.
  • A group names the groups under it too, so Items takes Items.Ring.
  • The read says which: "Caster's Strength (Items)", "Caster's Attack Damage (all but Items)".
  • Group Impact reads one group's part alone.
