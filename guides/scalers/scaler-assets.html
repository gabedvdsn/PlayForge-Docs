Scaler assets

A Scaler asset (Create → PlayForge → Scaler) holds one scaler that many fields can use.

A field of the Shared kind reads its Asset's scaler. An edit to the asset changes every field that uses it, with nothing to copy or propagate.

Open shows the asset in a window of its own. Under the field, the asset's curve shows beside its range, as "6–8, levels 1–5", and how many fields share it.

A field adjusts the shared values only through its own number and Combine. Scaler × Number with a number of 1.5 reads the asset's values times 1.5, as in Fire Duration × 1.5.

Note

The Scaler asset was the Scaler Template, whose link and sync settings nothing read, so those settings are gone. So are the old scaler window's curve editor and import window: a scaler's values are its table, and Import Values compares them before it copies.

In this section

  • The asset's inspector: The asset's own inspector shows its name, its kind and how many fields use it.
  • Trying a scaler: The asset's Try section reads it at each level of a range, Levels 1 to 5 to start, as an effect's Levels does.
  • What can share one: Any magnitude field can share a Scaler asset, within a few rules.
