Impact workers

An impact worker runs as a change its entity dealt lands on another's attribute, for the attribute the worker subscribes to.

Its hook validates the hit, then makes its actions. It runs for the dealer, not the one hit (Traps). Its context, ImpactWorkerContext, holds:

Member What it holds
System The dealer. The one hit is ImpactData.Target.
ImpactData The hit: its Target, its Attribute, the sourced change, its RealImpact and its OldValue.
FrameSummary The frame's summary.
ActionQueue The action queue (The action queue).
Property Description
Accept Unworkable Impact Also takes unworkable changes (Workable and unworkable).
