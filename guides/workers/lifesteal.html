Lifesteal

The Lifesteal worker, an impact worker, heals its holder a share of each workable loss it deals another: the value of its holder's Share, the attribute it names, as 0.15 for 15%; with none it heals nothing. The losses that count are to its Receives attribute, and it heals its Heals attribute, each the Health role's where it names none, so a mana burn can heal Health. An effect raises the share while it should, as an effect has no lifesteal of its own. Only hits of the Impact Types it names count, as attacks; with none, any; so a second worker on another attribute, of spells' types, makes a spell vamp. A worker that runs once for a hit, as this one, runs once however many of it its holder has, its project's and its own: the first of the kind to take the hit. It allocates nothing, healing or not.
