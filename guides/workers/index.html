Workers

Workers are the small rules that react to what happens to an entity: a floor that keeps Health above 1, a share of each blow sent back, a rage at half Health, a burst as a tag comes off. This page maps each kind: when it runs, on whose system and for whom, and what its context holds. Recipes builds common rules from them.

In this guide

  • Where workers come from: An attribute set's Worker Group: the entity built from the set holds its workers as it lives.
  • Lifesteal: The Lifesteal worker, an impact worker, heals its holder a share of each workable loss it deals another: the value of its holder's Share, the attribute it names, as 0.15 for 15%; with none it heals nothing.
  • The map: Inline or deferred. An attribute worker's Execution says how it acts.
  • The action queue: What a deferred worker returns, and whatever else is queued to an entity (GetActionQueue().Enqueue), waits in the entity's action queue for its end of frame.
  • Hearing workers run: An entity tells its workers' runs to whoever listens to its callbacks, and to no one else, so a worker no one hears costs nothing more
  • Workable and unworkable: A change is workable unless it says it isn't (SourcedModifiedAttributeValue.ImpactIsWorkable).
  • Traps: An impact worker runs for the dealer. Its context's System is the dealer, and the one hit is ImpactData.Target.
  • Built-in kinds: The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says.
