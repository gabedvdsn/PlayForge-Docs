Workers

Workers are the small rules that react to what happens to an entity: a floor that keeps Health above 1, a share of each blow sent back, a rage at half Health, a burst as a tag comes off.

Reactive rules in five families. A StandardWorkerGroup routes each family to the cache that runs it; effect workers run per application of the effect that lists them.

This page maps each kind: when it runs, on whose system and for whom, and what its context holds. Recipes builds common rules from them.

In this guide

  • Where workers come from: An entity holds workers from each of these sources
  • Lifesteal: The Lifesteal worker, an impact worker, heals its holder a share of each workable loss it deals another.
  • The map: The map shows, for each kind of worker, when it runs, whose system it runs on, and what its context holds.
  • The action queue: What a deferred worker returns, and whatever else is queued to an entity (GetActionQueue().Enqueue), waits in the entity's action queue for its end of frame.
  • Hearing workers run: An entity tells its workers' runs to whoever listens to its callbacks, and to no one else.
  • Workable and unworkable: A change is workable unless it says it isn't (SourcedModifiedAttributeValue.ImpactIsWorkable).
  • Traps: These are the mistakes workers invite most often.
  • Built-in kinds: The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says.
