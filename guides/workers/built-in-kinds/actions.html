Actions

Kind Menu What it does
ApplyEffectAction — Deferred action to apply an effect.
DeathAction — The deferred action that kills its entity as its turn comes, at Critical priority: the entity is marked dead, which its callbacks, its components, its effects and its killer hear, and it ends, or stays dead, at its frame's end, as its Death says.
LambdaAction — Simple action that executes a lambda.
LogAction — A deferred action that writes its text through ForgeLog, at Info, as its turn comes: at Cleanup priority unless it's given another, and only where its validity check, if it's given one, holds.
ModifyAttributeAction — Deferred action to modify an attribute.
RemoveEffectAction — Deferred action to remove an effect.
TagWorkerActivateAction — Deferred action to activate a tag worker.
TagWorkerResolveAction — Deferred action to resolve (deactivate) a tag worker.
