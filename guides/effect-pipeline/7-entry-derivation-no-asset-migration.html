7. Entry derivation (no asset migration)

The first entry is derived, never serialized. The effect's impact quad + duration flags are its authored surface.

GameplayEffectImpact.RebuildEntries re-derives that entry on every Generate(). So inspector edits (magnitude, attribute target, duration policy, tick flags) always take effect on the next application.

Magnitude on the derived entry references the live ScalableFloat, so even already-registered modifiers re-evaluate new magnitudes on their next recompute.

Note

Serializing the derived entry was tried and reverted: OnValidate froze half-authored state into assets (null targets, stale magnitudes, deliveries minted while the policy was still Instant) and never re-synced.

Additional impacts are authored entries. GameplayEffectImpact.AdditionalImpacts holds AttributeImpactEntry values that the impact drawer edits, each naming its own delivery.

RebuildEntries appends them, live, after the derived entry. It skips one with no attribute, and one whose delivery the duration can't make (IsDeliveryValidFor), which the drawer flags in place.

ResolveDelivery derives the first entry's delivery from the authored shape:

Authored shape Derived delivery
Instant OnApply
Durational/Infinite, no ticks Continuous
+ periodic ticks Periodic (ReverseImpactOnRemoval flagged: rollback no longer exists)
TickOnApplication-only trap Continuous for Multiply/Override (flagged), else OnApply

Window → PlayForge → Problems lists each effect that has one of the shapes above, among the validator's other problems. It's read-only: nothing is written.

Deleted outright:

  • IsSustainedModifier();
  • the Mode A/B inference;
  • TickAttribute;
  • RollbackCommittedDelta;
  • tick-rollback via TrackedImpact, which is analytics-only now.
