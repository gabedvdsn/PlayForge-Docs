A counter

Each loss another entity deals its holder sets it off, and it strikes back at that one for 10.

  • Retaliate (an ability): one stage, Apply Effects with Retaliation. Under Activation's Triggers, one trigger: Gameplay Event, the core's Impact Taken, Attribute Health, Sign Loss, Target Instigator.
  • Retaliation (an effect): Instant, -10 Health.

Its own hit is a loss to the attacker, not to its holder, so it doesn't set itself off. For a chance, as Counter Helix's 20%, set the trigger's Chance; to strike everyone near, give the ability Auto Target and leave the trigger's Target at Own.
