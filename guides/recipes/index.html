Recipes

These ten recipes build common rules from PlayForge's own assets and fields, with no code.

  • Each is in the Additional Content sample (Package Manager → PlayForge → Samples), in a folder of its own under Recipes.
  • Each reads the sample's Health attribute, in Recipes/Attributes. Point its fields at your own.
  • Workers maps the workers some of them use.

In this guide

  • A curse that bursts: This curse deals 5 each second for 3 seconds, then 30 as it ends, whether it runs out or comes off.
  • A counter: A counter answers each loss another entity deals to its holder: it strikes back at that entity for 10.
  • A crit: A crit doubles a hit on a 25% chance.
  • An execute: With an execute, a hit that would leave its target under a fifth of its Health takes the rest.
  • Lifesteal: While Vampirism lasts, its holder heals half of each loss it deals another.
  • Damage return: While Thorns lasts, half of each loss its holder takes goes back to the one who dealt it.
  • Can't die: While Undying lasts, no blow takes its target below 1, as Shallow Grave does.
  • On hit: While Frost Blade is equipped, each hit its holder deals bites for 5 more.
  • On kill: While Reaper's Mark is equipped, the hit that takes a target's Health to 0 feeds its holder 20.
  • A phase: As the boss's Health falls to half, it enrages, once.
  • Formulas: The sample holds Scaler assets for a genre's formulas, under Formulas, each named for what it computes.
  • A starter set: The sample holds plain assets named for what they are, under Starter Set.
