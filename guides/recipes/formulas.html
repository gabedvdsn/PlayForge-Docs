Formulas

Scaler assets for a genre's formulas, under Formulas, each named for what it computes. A field reads one with its Shared kind (Scalers); each is a Stacking Curve of a count, with an a.

  • 1 ÷ (1 + 0.06 × Armor): Reciprocal, a 0.06, of the hit's target's Armor. In a Value Pipeline's Multiply stage, a hit leaves all of itself at 0 Armor, a half at about 17, never none.
  • 100 ÷ (100 + Armor): Reciprocal, a 0.01: a half at 100 Armor, a third at 200.
  • −Armor: Linear, a −1. In an Add stage, a blow of 100 against 20 Armor lands as 80.
  • 0.15 × Stacks: Linear, a 0.15, of an item's stacks, a bonus that grows by as much with each.
  • 1 − 1 ÷ (1 + 0.15 × Stacks): Hyperbolic, a chance that nears 1 and never reaches it: 13% with one stack, 60% with ten.
  • 0.9 ^ Stacks: Exponential, a share that falls by a tenth with each stack.

The Armor ones read the Armor attribute in Formulas/Attributes, as the hit lands; point them at your own.
