A starter set

Plain assets named for what they are, under Starter Set; the recipes above hold the rest of a starter set, a damage over time, an on hit, an on kill and a boss's phase. Each hit reads the recipes' Health.

  • Melee Attack (an ability): Apply Effects, Melee Hit, on what it's cast at; Cooldown Melee Cooldown. Melee Hit: Instant, -10 Health. Melee Cooldown: Durational, 1 s.
  • Bolt (an ability): the same, its Bolt Hit -25 Health, its Bolt Cooldown 3 s.
  • Slow (an effect): Durational, 3 s, Multiply Movement Speed by 0.7, back as it ends.
  • Shield (an effect): Durational, 5 s, +50 to Shield's current and base while it lasts; its Modifiers, one Incoming rule whose Change Impact on Health adds an Absorb stage into Shield, so each hit on its holder's Health comes off its Shield first. Give Shield's element a base of 0, between 0 and its base, so it's 0 without one.

They read the Movement Speed and Shield attributes in Starter Set/Attributes.
