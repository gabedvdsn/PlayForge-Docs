Matching effects and abilities

Some fields match effects by their tags, not entities:

  • an effect's Removes Effects Tagged and Blocks Effects Tagged;
  • the Has Effect check's Tagged;
  • the Asset Part number's Effects Tagged.

An effect answers for a tag by its asset tag, a context tag or a tag it grants, each the tag or under it (Effects: Tags). GameplayEffectTags.Names(tags, effect) asks this in code.

AbilityTags.Names(tags, ability) asks the same of an ability: whether its asset tag or a context tag is one of the tags, or under one. Cancelled By and Blocks match abilities this way.
