How an entity holds tags

An entity carries the tags its sources grant it, each with a weight: the number of grants of it the entity holds now. Its sources, as its tags are compiled:

  • its identity: its asset tag, its sides and its Granted Tags. Its sides are its Affiliation, or those its registration or SetAffiliation gave it, or a Side Change's while one lasts (Entities: Tags);
  • its attribute set's asset tag, and each sub-set's;
  • each effect on its shelf: its asset tag, its Granted Tags, and those its modifiers add (Effects: Tags). An Instant effect grants its Granted Tags only as it applies;
  • each ability it knows: its asset tag and its Passive Tags, and its Active Tags while an activation of it runs (Abilities: Tags);
  • each item it holds: its asset tag, and its Granted Tags while it's equipped (Items: Tags);
  • its loose tags.

An asset keeps its tags in its Tags section, an ability's an AbilityTags, an effect's a GameplayEffectTags and an item's an ItemTags, and an entity's are fields of its EntityIdentity. Context Tags describe an asset, and are granted to no one. A tag two sources grant weighs 2, as two burns that each grant Status.Burning, and stays until both are gone. An effect grants its tags once for each container on its shelf, not once a stack: a burn stacked five times in one container grants Status.Burning once.

The entity compiles its tags as a source comes, goes or changes: as an ability is learned, starts or ends, an item is given, equipped or taken, a loose tag comes or goes, its sides change, and an effect lands or comes off. A compile rebuilds the weights from the sources and tells, as it ends, only what it changed (Hearing tags change).

Loose tags

A loose tag is one granted to the entity directly, with nothing behind it, as Unreal's loose gameplay tags are. gas.AddLooseTag(tag) grants one, each grant counted, and gas.RemoveLooseTag(tag) takes one grant back, false where the entity has none; gas.GetLooseTags() lists them, a tag once for each grant. A save keeps them (Saving and Loading: What a save holds). An ability's Grant Tags task grants loose tags while its stage runs (Abilities: Stages), and the Debugger's Entities view adds them, and takes them back, by hand.

Reading them

gas.GetTagCache() gives the entity's TagCache, its tags and their weights; a GameplayAbilitySystem component's is null until it initializes.

  • GetWeight(tag): the weight of that very tag, −1 where the entity hasn't it, not 0. TryGetWeight(tag, out weight) says whether it has it, and gas.GetTagWeight(tag) is GetWeight.
  • HasTagExact(tag), and HasTag(tag, mode) by a match mode (Querying tags); HasAnyTag, HasAllTags and HasNoneOfTags ask it of several, Exact unless told.
  • GetMatchingWeight(tag, mode): the weights of the tags that match, added up, 0 where none does; in Exact mode it is GetWeight, −1 for an absent tag. GetMatchingTags(tag, mode) gives the tags held that match.
  • GetAppliedTags(): each tag held, once. The list is the cache's own, reused until a tag comes or goes: read it, and copy it to keep it. gas.GetAppliedTags() is the same list.

AddTag, AddTags, RemoveTag, RemoveTags, RemoveTagCompletely and RemoveMatchingTags change the weights directly, as an Instant effect's tags are granted for the moment it applies, and are told as they happen; but the next compile rebuilds the weights from their sources and drops them, so a tag meant to last is a loose tag. ResetWeights() empties the weights, telling each tag's going, and ResetCache() resolves the active tag workers as well; BeginRebuild() and EndRebuild() frame a compile. LogWeights(), LogActiveWorkers() and GetHierarchyDebugString() write the cache out, for debugging.

What holds and what grants

  • ITagHandler is what carries tags, which every entity is: GetAppliedTags(), GetTagWeight(tag), QueryTags(query), against its weights, and CompileGrantedTags(), which compiles them now. A tag worker reads its entity through it.
  • ITagSource is what grants tags: GetGrantedTags(). An asset gives what it grants of itself, as an ability its asset tag and its Passive Tags; what holds it on an entity gives what it grants there, as a known ability its Active Tags while it runs, and an effect on the shelf its asset tag with the rest.

The Debugger's Entities view shows an entity's tags with what grants each, and its Tags view every tag the entities hold, as a tree, with who holds the one selected (Getting Started: Tooling tour).
