How an entity holds tags

An entity carries the tags its sources grant it, each with a weight. A tag's weight is the number of grants of it that the entity holds now.

In this section

  • Where its tags come from: An entity's tags come from its sources, which it reads as its tags are compiled
  • Weights and compiles: A tag's weight counts its grants, and a compile rebuilds the weights from the entity's sources.
  • Loose tags: A loose tag is one granted to the entity directly, with nothing behind it, as Unreal's loose gameplay tags are.
  • Reading them: An entity's TagCache holds its tags and their weights, and gas.GetTagCache() gives it.
  • What holds and what grants: Two interfaces describe what carries tags and what grants them: ITagHandler and ITagSource.
