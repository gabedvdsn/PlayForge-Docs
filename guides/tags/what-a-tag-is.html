What a tag is

A tag, a Tag, is a path of names joined by dots, parents first: Status.Debuff.Burn is under Status.Debuff, which is under Status, its root. It's a struct an asset saves as two strings:

  • Name, which tells it apart: two tags are equal, by == or Equals, exactly when their names are.
  • DisplayName, how it reads. ToString() gives it, or the name where it has none, and PlayForge's windows show a tag so.

A tag is of one of three makes:

  • Unique, as New Tag makes one (New Tag): its name is its path padded to 64 characters by a hash of it, as Status.Debuff.BurnQx3…, and its display name is the path. One path always gives one name, so the padding only keeps it apart from the plain tag of its path. PlayForge's own tags are unique (PlayForge's own tags).
  • An asset's own tag, which its Name gives, as Ability.FireBall for an ability named Fire Ball (Abilities: Identity): its name is its whole path, and its display name its leaf, FireBall.
  • Plain, as code makes one of a text, Tag.Generate("Status.Debuff.Burn"): its name and its display name are both the path.

IsValid says whether a tag has a name; an empty tag field holds default(Tag), which has none.

Its place in the tree

A tag's Path is what its family goes by: a unique tag's display name, and any other tag's name. From it come Depth, 1 for a root, and IsRoot; GetLeafName(), Burn; GetParentPath(), Status.Debuff; GetAncestorPaths(), from its parent to its root; and GetSegments(). IsChildOf(tag) asks whether its path is under another tag's, at any depth, and IsDirectChildOf(tag) one level under; IsParentOf(tag) asks the reverse; MatchesOrIsChildOf and MatchesOrIsParentOf take the tag itself too; and SharesAncestorWith asks whether two tags have one root.

A family goes by paths, and equality by names. Status.Debuff.Burn is under Status.Debuff whichever makes the two are, but the plain Status.Debuff and the unique one are two tags, and neither is under the other.

Showing a tag

Show a tag by its display name, with ToString(), GetName() or an interpolated string, $"{tag}". Its Name is padded where it's unique, and a tag converts to its Name where a string is asked for, as in string text = tag; or "Stunned by " + tag.

The registry

TagRegistry keeps the tags made as the game runs, by name: making a tag registers it, and a Bootstrapper registers PlayForge's own as it starts, TagRegistry.Initialize(). TagRegistry.Resolve(name) gives the registered tag of a name, with its display name, or else the plain tag of the name, as a saved name is read back. TagRegistry.IsValidPath(path) says whether a text can be a tag's path: names of letters, digits, underscores and spaces, joined by single dots, with no dot at either end. Its lookups of a family, GetDirectChildren, GetAllDescendants, GetParent and GetAncestors, go by the registered names, and so miss a unique tag's family: read that from the tag's own Path, with IsChildOf.
