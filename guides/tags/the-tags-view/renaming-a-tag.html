Renaming a tag

The Tags view renames a tag, and every tag under its path, in the assets that store them.

To rename a tag:

  1. Choose Rename… in its row's right-click menu, or press F2.
  2. Enter the tag's new Path.

The rename is one Undo step. It reaches every ability, effect, item, entity, attribute set, attribute, Scaler asset, Value Pipeline and Requirement Template that stores one of the tags, and the Tag Library entries that declare them.

A unique tag stays unique, and a plain one stays plain.

Nothing else follows. These keep the old name:

  • code;
  • scenes and prefabs;
  • a Forge Project's settings;
  • Crowd Control Rules and Effect Modifiers;
  • a Cue Library;
  • the Creator Lab's rules.

A rename refuses a path a tag has already, and one of PlayForge's own tags' text. It renames no tag that is, or has under it, one of PlayForge's own or an asset's own tag.
