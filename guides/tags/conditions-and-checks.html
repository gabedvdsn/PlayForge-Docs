Conditions and checks

Besides requirements, conditions, ability checks, tasks and triggers read tags: an entity's, or an asset's.

  • The Tag condition, TagCondition, holds where the caster or the target, as its On says, answers its Query, a tag query counted by weight, so a tag granted twice counts twice. Where the number's context lacks that entity, as a target's tag with no target, it doesn't hold. A Conditional number, an effect's Apply, Ongoing and Removal Conditions, an On Hit and an ability's Condition check read conditions (Scalers: A magnitude field).
  • The Tag Weight check, TagWeightValidation, among an ability's Caster or Target Checks, compares the checked entity's weight of its Tag with its Weight, by its Comparison, an entity without the tag holding it at 0. A check of your own that reads a tag derives from AbstractTagValidationRule, whose GetValue gives its tag's weight on the checked entity, or, where that entity holds none of it, the ability's Local Data under the tag (Abilities: Requirements).
  • A Tag Added trigger activates its ability as the entity gains a tag, or one under it, that it didn't have; a Wait For Tag task waits for its caster or its target to gain a tag, that very tag, or to lose it; and a stage's Condition runs the stage by a tag, in the activation's data or on the caster or the target (Abilities: Stages).
  • An effect's Removes Effects Tagged and Blocks Effects Tagged, the Has Effect check's Tagged and the Asset Part number's Effects Tagged match effects, not entities: an effect answers for a tag by its asset tag, a context tag or a tag it grants, each the tag or under it (Effects: Tags). GameplayEffectTags.Names(tags, effect) asks it in code, as AbilityTags.Names(tags, ability) asks whether an ability's asset tag or a context tag is one of the tags or under one, as Cancelled By and Blocks do.
