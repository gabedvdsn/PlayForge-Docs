The picker

Pressing a tag field opens its picker, which lists, in order:

  1. None;
  2. New Tag…, where the field may make tags;
  3. the tags the field offers, by their paths, in natural order.

While Group Tags by Root is on, each tag sits under a folder of its root, where the root has more than one tag. The field's tag is checked.

The search finds a tag by any part of its path. While a tag is highlighted, the picker's Context says the contexts it's for, or that it's for none yet.

A pick writes the tag, its name and its display name, into each selected asset, as one Undo step.

What a field offers

A field offers the tags of its contexts (Contexts), and the Universal ones, of the active Forge Project:

  • PlayForge's own, and those code exposes;
  • those the project's assets use;
  • those a Tag Library in its folder declares.

It leaves out a tag that only another project uses.
