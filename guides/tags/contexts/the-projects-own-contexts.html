The project's own contexts

A project can make contexts of its own, through any contexts field. A contexts field, such as New Tag's Contexts or the Tags view's Declared For, shows the contexts chosen, or None.

Pressing the field opens every context: PlayForge's, those the project's fields name, and the project's own. Universal comes first and the rest by name, with those chosen checked. Choosing one adds it, or takes it away.

To make a context of the project's own:

  1. Press a contexts field.
  2. Choose New Context…, last in the list.
  3. Name the context.

The field chooses the new context, and the first Tag Library keeps it. Every contexts field offers it from then on. New Context… refuses an empty name, and a name a context has already, whatever its case.

A field of the project's own takes the context's tags with [ForgeTagContext("Weather")], the context's name as written.
