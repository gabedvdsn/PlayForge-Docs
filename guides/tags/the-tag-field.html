The tag field

Each Tag field, alone or in a list, draws as a tag field, TagDrawer, which shows its tag by its display name and picks another from the tags its contexts offer. The field shows its label, or none in a list, then the tag's parents in secondary text and its leaf in strong text, or its whole path while Show Full Tag Paths is on; None for an empty field, and a dash where the selected assets' tags differ.

The picker

Pressing the field opens its picker: None first, then New Tag… where the field may make tags, then the tags it offers by their paths, in natural order, each under a folder of its root while Group Tags by Root is on and the root has more than one tag. The field's tag is checked. The search finds a tag by any part of its path, and while a tag is highlighted the picker's Context says the contexts it's for, or that it's for none yet.

A field offers the tags of its contexts (Contexts) and the Universal ones, of the active Forge Project: PlayForge's own and those code exposes, those the project's assets use, and those a Tag Library in its folder declares. A tag only another project uses, it leaves out. A pick writes the tag, its name and its display name, into each selected asset, as one Undo step.

Its menu

The field's right-click menu holds None, which empties it; New Tag…; Make It a Child Of…, which picks another tag and puts the field's leaf under it, as Burn under Status.Debuff makes Status.Debuff.Burn; and Copy Path, which copies the tag as the field reads it. New Tag… and Make It a Child Of… show where the field may make tags.

New Tag

New Tag asks for the new tag's Path, which starts as the field's tag's parents, and its Contexts, which start as the field's. Create, or Enter, makes the tag, unique, and gives it to the field; a context chosen beyond the field's declares the tag for it in the Tag Library, so that context's fields offer it too. It refuses an empty path; one of anything but letters, digits, underscores and spaces in names joined by single dots; and one of 64 characters or more, which the padding would cut short, so it could be another tag's. A path that is one of PlayForge's own tags' text, as Died or Health, would make that very tag: New Tag says so, and Use It gives the field PlayForge's own.

Preferences

Preferences → PlayForge holds each user's own, under Tags: Show Full Tag Paths (off), a tag's whole path in strong text in place of its parents before its leaf, in the tag fields and The Forge's Tags view; and Group Tags by Root (on), the picker's tags under their roots. Its Menu Width and Menu Height are the most the picker grows to.
