Tag workers

A tag worker, AbstractTagWorker, acts while its entity's tags meet its requirements, as flames shown while Status.Burning weighs 3 or more.

A worker is one of a Worker Group's Tag workers, its kind picked from the list. Its entity holds it as it holds the group (Workers: Where workers come from).

  • One worker for many entities: One tag worker serves every entity whose group holds it.
