Where its tags come from

An entity's tags come from its sources, which it reads as its tags are compiled:

Source The tags it grants
Its identity Its asset tag, its sides and its Granted Tags (Entities: Tags).
Its attribute set The set's asset tag, and each sub-set's.
Each effect on its shelf The effect's asset tag, its Granted Tags, and those its modifiers add (Effects: Tags). An Instant effect grants its Granted Tags only as it applies.
Each ability it knows The ability's asset tag and its Passive Tags, and its Active Tags while an activation of it runs (Abilities: Tags).
Each item it holds The item's asset tag, and its Granted Tags while it's equipped (Items: Tags).
Its loose tags Each loose tag (Loose tags).

An entity's sides are its Affiliation, or those its registration or SetAffiliation gave it, or a Side Change's while one lasts.

An asset keeps its tags in its Tags section:

  • an ability's is an AbilityTags;
  • an effect's is a GameplayEffectTags;
  • an item's is an ItemTags;
  • an entity's are fields of its EntityIdentity.

Context Tags describe an asset, and are granted to no one.
