Crowd control

Crowd control stops abilities by the tags their caster carries, so no ability has to list Stunned among the tags it must not have. A Crowd Control Rules asset, CrowdControlRules (Create → PlayForge → Crowd Control Rules), holds its Rules, each a CrowdControlRule: the Tag its holder carries, as Stunned, and what it Stops, an ECrowdControlStops: Every Ability, as a stun, or Abilities Tagged, those whose asset tag or a context tag is one of its Ability Tags or under one, as Silenced stops spells.

While the caster carries a rule's tag, or one under it, an ability the rule stops is refused, as Crowd Control, the refusal's Rule naming the tag, and one running is interrupted. An ability's Ignores Crowd Control names the rules it ignores, by their tags, a tag ignoring those under it too. An entity follows its own rules, its Crowd Control, or else its Forge Project's: gas.GetCrowdControl() gives them, and rules.Stopping(ability, tags) the first rule that stops an ability, or null. Abilities: Crowd Control has the rest.
