Templates

Templates let an asset take its fields from another asset of its type, its template.

  • An asset's menu Set Template... picks, from a searchable list of the other assets of its type, the template it becomes a child of.
  • A child takes the template's fields when the template is pushed, with the template's asset menu Push to Its Children, as one Undo step. Saving a template changes no child.
  • A child keeps each field it overrides. An edit to a child's field makes it the child's own, and its inspector marks it as Unity marks a prefab instance's override.

The Editor Tools: Templates covers templates in depth.
