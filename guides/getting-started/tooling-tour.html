Tooling tour

This tour says what each of PlayForge's editor tools is for, and how to open it. The Editor Tools covers each tool in depth.

In this section

  • The Creator Lab: The Creator Lab is a space to try the project's own assets in.
  • The Forge: The Forge is PlayForge's main window: every PlayForge asset of the active Forge Project, in one list beside a detail pane.
  • Forge Projects: A Forge Project holds one game's PlayForge assets, and its settings.
  • The Tags view: The Forge's Tags view lists the project's tags, and what uses each.
  • Spreadsheets: The Spreadsheets commands write the project's numbers by level, or its items' stats, to CSV, and read a sheet back.
  • The Debugger: The Debugger shows the game as it plays, in four views the toolbar switches between.
  • The Problems view: The Problems view lists every problem the validator finds in the project's PlayForge assets, with its severity and its asset.
  • The settings pages: PlayForge's Settings are on two pages: the project's, and your own.
  • Asset fields: Asset fields are Unity's object fields: every reference to a PlayForge asset is one.
  • Import and Clear: Import and Clear sit on each inspector section's header, beside Help.
  • Templates: Templates let an asset take its fields from another asset of its type, its template.
  • Golden tests: The Golden tests are the framework's behavioural contract.
  • The test kit: The test kit (FarEmerald.PlayForge.Testing) plays your own abilities, effects and items in your own tests.
