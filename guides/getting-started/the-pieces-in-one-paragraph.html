Which asset holds which. Every asset derives from BaseForgeAsset, the levelled ones from BaseForgeLevelProvider. An Ability, effect, Item, AttributeSet or entity can LinkToProvider and follow another's level.

An EntityIdentity asset configures an entity:

  • its AttributeSet: attributes with starting Current/Base values, clamping and regulation;
  • its starting Abilities and starting Items;
  • its tags and its level range.

At runtime, a GameplayAbilitySystem (a MonoBehaviour) or a RuntimeGameplayAbilitySystem (a plain class) instantiates that identity.

GameplayEffect assets change attributes. Each effect's impact entry has a delivery, which decides everything about its lifecycle:

Delivery Meaning Reversal
Continuous modifier while the effect is active ("+20% speed") automatic, exact
OnApply one permanent commit ("take 30 damage") never
Periodic one permanent commit per tick ("5 HP/s") never

Abilities run staged task lists (targeting → apply effects → run code-driven sequences). Their cost and cooldown are expressed as effects. Effect & Attribute Pipeline is the full pipeline reference.

To go further:

  • Recipes builds ten common rules from these pieces with no code, as a crit, an execute and a counter, each in the Additional Content sample.
  • Workers maps when each kind of worker runs, and for whom.
  • Cues brings together what effects, abilities, entities and items show and sound: their cues, and the Cue Library that routes cue tags to them.
