Where cues are raised

  • Effects: an effect's Cues component plays its cues as it lands, as an application it takes in lands, as each tick executes, as an impact it deals lands, as it comes off, while it lasts, as an application is refused and as its stacks change (Effects: Cues).
  • Abilities: a Cues component plays an ability's as it activates, as it commits, while it's active, as it ends and as one is refused; a Play Cue task plays them at a stage's exact moment, on the caster, on the target or at the point (Abilities: Cues).
  • Entities: a Cues component plays an entity's as it spawns, dies and comes back, and lasting ones while it holds a tag, as stars over its head while it's Stunned (Entities: Cues).
  • Items: a Cues component plays an item's as it's equipped, while it's equipped and as it's used (Items: Cues).
  • Code: ForgeCues.Burst(sparks, new CueContext(entity, ECueMoment.Raised, point: hit.point)) plays sparks where a ray hit, and ForgeCues.Begin and ForgeCues.End begin and end a lasting one.

A cue's context, CueContext, says what raised it, its Moment, as Applied, Activated or Died; its target and its source; the effect or the ability behind it; where it plays, its Point and Normal, or else its target's place; a magnitude; and, for a lasting cue, the key its beginning and its end share.
