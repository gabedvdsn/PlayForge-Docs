Handlers

Each cue in a list is a handler, its kind picked from the list.

A handler plays at each moment whose list holds it. In a list of lasting cues, as a While Active, it plays from its beginning to its end.

PlayForge ships the handlers below, and you can write your own.

In this section

  • Camera Shake cue: A Camera Shake cue shakes the camera as its moment comes.
  • Sound cue: A Sound cue plays its clip where its Placement says (Placement).
  • Prefab cue: A Prefab cue places its prefab where its Placement says.
  • Floating Text cue: A Floating Text cue floats a text where its Placement says, as a hit's damage, the gold a kill gave or a status's name.
  • Process cue: A Process cue runs a MonoBehaviour process where its Placement says, on a packet loaded for the cue.
  • Sequence cue: A Sequence cue runs a task sequence on a packet loaded for the cue, with its Data list last.
  • Cue Tag cue: A Cue Tag cue plays what its tag routes to (Routes).
  • Writing your own handler: Write a handler of your own by deriving from AbstractCueHandler.
