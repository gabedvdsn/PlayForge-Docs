Handlers

Each cue in a list is a handler, its kind picked from the list. It plays at each moment whose list holds it, and, in a list of lasting cues, as a While Active, from its beginning to its end:

  • Camera Shake shakes the camera through the shaker the game gives ForgeCues.Shaker, an ICameraShaker.
  • Sound plays its clip where its Placement says (Placement).
  • Prefab places its prefab where its Placement says, for its Lifetime, or, as a lasting cue, until its cue ends (Pooling).
  • Floating Text floats a text where its Placement says, as a hit's damage, the gold a kill gave or a status's name (Floating text).
  • Process runs a MonoBehaviour process where its Placement says, on a packet loaded for the cue, with its Data list last (Data packets): a burst's runs its own course, and a lasting one's runs until its cue ends (Pooling).
  • Sequence runs a task sequence on a packet loaded for the cue, with its Data list last: a burst's runs its course, and a lasting one's runs until its cue ends. It goes by the clock its Clock picks, else the sequence's own, else that of the one it plays on.
  • Cue Tag plays what its tag routes to (Routes).

Write a handler of your own by deriving from AbstractCueHandler: override the moments it plays at, or Burst to play every burst one way, reading the moment from cue.Moment, and Begin and End for a lasting one.
