Raises and sinks

Each raise of a list of cues names what the list belongs to, its CueSource:

  • an effect, an ability, an entity or an item, by its asset;
  • a cue tag raised from code, as ForgeCueRoutes.Burst(cue);
  • or a game's own code, where it names nothing.

Code can name one: ForgeCues.Burst(cues, cue, CueSource.Of(effect)).

Before any cue of a raise plays, its world's sink is asked, once: ForgeCueSink.WillPlay(event, raise). The raise holds the cue and its source.

  • The default sink plays everything.
  • A world of its own takes a sink of its own with ForgeCues.UseSink(world, sink). ForgeCues.Sink is the one every other world asks.
  • The sink is asked whatever mutes a cue. So a server that plays none can still send them on, as a network layer's sink does.
  • A cue tag raised from code asks once. Its routes, and a Cue Tag within them, play inside that raise, unasked.

An effect's applier can say where it hit, before applying it: spec.WithHit(point, normal, magnitude). Its cues then play at the point, facing the normal, with the magnitude, as a sword's spark where the blade struck.
