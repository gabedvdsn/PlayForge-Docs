Placement

A cue that places something, a Prefab or a Sound, says where with its Placement:

  • At: on whom.
    • Self: the one the cue is on: an effect's target, an ability's caster, the entity, or an item's holder. Where its raiser gave a point, as a hit's, it's there.
    • Source: who's behind it, as an effect's source or a death's killer.
    • Target: an ability's first target, or the place it targeted. For another's cue, the one it's on.
    • Each Target: one at each of an ability's targets.
    • Point: the cue's point, or the place an ability targeted.
  • Socket: a child of theirs it's placed at, by name, found at any depth, as a hand or a weapon's tip.
  • Offset moves it, and Scatter moves it by a random amount up to that much either way on each axis, both in the space of what it's placed at.
  • Attach: whether it follows them: a lasting cue's only, by default; always; or never. An attached sound plays from a source of its own under them.
  • Facing: as what it's placed at faces, or along the cue's normal at a point; along the cue's normal; away from or toward its source; or the world's way.

Scatter draws from ForgeCues.Random, the cues' own random numbers, so a cue never moves the game's rolls: a server that plays no cues and a client that plays them must draw alike.
