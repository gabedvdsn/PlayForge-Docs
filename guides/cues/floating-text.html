Floating text

A Floating Text cue floats a text where its Placement says, 2 units above the one the cue is on by default: a hit's damage, the gold a kill gave, a status's name as it lands.

  • Text: what it says. {value} is its number, {name} the name of what raised it, as an effect's, and {tag} its cue tag: +{value} gold, or {name} for a status.
  • Value: its number, From the cue's magnitude, as a hit's value from an effect's On Hit, or its Number, read as it plays, as a Tracked Impact's for the gold an instant effect gave. It's written with up to Decimals decimals; without its sign where Absolute says, as damage shown as 35; with a Plus Sign above 0; shortened past a thousand where Abbreviate says, as 1.2k; and not at all for 0 where Hide Zero says.
  • Look: its Color, or its Negative Color below 0 where By Sign says; its Size, Font, Bold and Outline; turned to the camera where Face Camera says, ForgeCues.Camera or else the main camera; and its Sorting Order. A Prefab holding a TextMeshPro text shows it in place of the cue's own, its font, size and outline its own.
  • Motion: its Lifetime; its Velocity, and a Random Velocity either way on each axis; Gravity, for an arc, and Drag; its opacity over its life, Fade, and its scale, Scale, as a pop; on the game's clock where Game Time says, else in real time.
  • Stacking, on one entity: a number Merge Within some seconds of the last adds to it, which shows the sum and floats again, as a burn's ticks; each text floats a Stagger above the one below it, in the lowest place free; and at most Max Shown float at once, the oldest going first.

Its texts come from ForgeCuePool and go back to it. For damage numbers, put one in an effect's On Hit: it plays as each impact the effect deals lands, on the one it lands on, told the value it changed.
