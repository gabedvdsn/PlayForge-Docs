Cues

A cue is what something shows and sounds, apart from what it changes: a spark as a hit lands, a hum while a shield holds, a shake as a giant falls. Effects, abilities, entities and items each list their cues, and a game's own code raises one where it likes. Every cue plays through ForgeCues, after Unreal's gameplay cues: a burst at a moment, as a hit's spark, or a lasting cue's beginning and its end, as a shield's hum, which share a key.

  • Where cues are raised: Effects: an effect's Cues component plays its cues as it lands, as an application it takes in lands, as each tick executes, as an impact it deals lands, as it comes off, while it lasts, as an application is…
  • Handlers: Each cue in a list is a handler, its kind picked from the list.
  • Placement: A cue that places something, a Prefab or a Sound, says where with its Placement
  • Floating text: A Floating Text cue floats a text where its Placement says, 2 units above the one the cue is on by default: a hit's damage, the gold a kill gave, a status's name as it lands.
  • Routes: A Cue Tag cue names a tag, as Cue.Hit.Fire, in place of what it shows, so an effect, an ability and an entity can each raise Cue.Hit.Fire and one place says how it looks.
  • Raised: The Cue Library's inspector lists under Raised each cue tag the project's assets raise through it, by a Cue Tag cue, in tag order.

  • Data packets: A cue that runs a process or a sequence hands it a data packet of its own, loaded for what raised the cue, CuePackets.For(cue, data).
  • Raises and sinks: Each raise of a list of cues names what the list belongs to, its CueSource: an effect, an ability, an entity or an item, by its asset; a cue tag raised from code, as ForgeCueRoutes.Burst(cue); or a game's own code…
  • Silencing and listening: ForgeCues.Enabled = false silences every cue, as on a server or while a simulation runs ahead.
  • Built-in kinds: The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says.
