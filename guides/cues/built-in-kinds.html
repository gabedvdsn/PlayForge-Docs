Built-in kinds

The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says. A project's own kinds join them in the same pickers.

Cue handlers

Kind Menu What it does
CameraShakeCue — Shakes the camera as its moment comes.
FloatingTextCue — Floats a text, as a hit's damage, the gold a kill gave or a status's name, where its placement says.
PrefabCue — Places a prefab where its placement says, for a moment or for as long as the cue lasts.
ProcessCue (Process) — Runs a MonoBehaviour process where its placement says, with a data packet loaded for the cue.
SequenceCue (Sequence) — Runs a task sequence on a data packet loaded for the cue.
SoundCue — Plays a sound where its placement says as its moment comes.
TagCue (Cue Tag) — Plays what the project's cue library has for its tag, or the entity's own Cue Receiver's.
