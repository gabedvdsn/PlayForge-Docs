Routes

A Cue Tag cue names a tag, as Cue.Hit.Fire, in place of what it shows, so an effect, an ability and an entity can each raise Cue.Hit.Fire and one place says how it looks. That place is the project's Cue Library, an asset from Create > PlayForge > Cue Library, which Project Settings > PlayForge names under Forge Project. Each of its routes is a cue tag and the handlers it plays.

  • A tag plays its own route, or else its nearest parent's: with no route for Cue.Hit.Fire, Cue.Hit's plays.
  • A route that doesn't Override plays its parent's after it, so Cue.Hit.Fire's flames add to Cue.Hit's spark.
  • An entity's Cue Receiver component plays a tag its own way first, in place of the library's, or before it with Pass Through. Its Variant plays the library's route under its name, as Cue.Hit.Golem for Cue.Hit, where the library has one, so a golem's hit sounds of stone.
  • Code can give a library of its own, ForgeCueRoutes.Library, in place of the project's.
