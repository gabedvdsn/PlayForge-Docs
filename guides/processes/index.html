Processes

A process is anything that runs over time under ProcessControl: an entity's GAS, a projectile, an aura's pulse, a task sequence (Task Sequences), a regeneration. ProcessControl steps each from Unity's player loop, Update, FixedUpdate or LateUpdate as it asks, moves it between its states, and ends it. A Bootstrapper in the scene brings ProcessControl up.

In this guide

  • Lifecycles: A process's lifecycle says how it starts and what happens when its run is done
  • Kinds: Mono processes (LazyMonoProcess, and AbstractMonoProcess under it) are MonoBehaviours: prefabs or scene objects, with Unity's components.
  • Registering one: The relay is the handle on a registered process: its state, how long it has lived, and what's done to it.
  • Pausing: A pause holds a process in place, at once.
  • Groups: A group is a process that holds other processes, found by a tag.
  • Logging: ProcessControl's own logs are its inspector's toggles.
