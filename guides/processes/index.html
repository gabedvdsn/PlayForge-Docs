Processes

A process is anything that runs over time under ProcessControl. An entity's GAS, a projectile, an aura's pulse, a task sequence (Task Sequences) and a regeneration are all processes.

A scene adapter steps one ProcessWorld from Unity's Update, LateUpdate and FixedUpdate. Register through the builder: ProcessControl.Register(x).HandledBy(h).With(data).In(world).Run(out relay)

ProcessControl does three things with each process:

  • It steps the process from Unity's player loop, in Update, FixedUpdate or LateUpdate, as the process asks.
  • It moves the process between its states.
  • It ends the process.

A Bootstrapper in the scene brings ProcessControl up.

In this guide

  • Lifecycles: A process's lifecycle says how it starts and what happens when its run is done.
  • Kinds: A process is one of two kinds: a mono process or a runtime process.
  • Registering one: You register a process with ProcessControl, which gives you a relay to control it with.
  • Pausing: A pause holds a process in place, at once.
  • Groups: A group is a process that holds other processes, found by a tag.
  • Logging: What processes log is set in two places.
