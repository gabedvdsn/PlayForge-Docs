PlayForge — Recipes

Ten common rules, each built from PlayForge's own assets and fields, with no code. Each is in the Additional Content sample (Package Manager → PlayForge → Samples), under Recipes, a folder each. Each reads the sample's Health attribute, in Recipes/Attributes; point its fields at your own. WORKERS.md maps the workers some of them use.

A curse that bursts

It deals 5 each second for 3 seconds, then 30 as it ends, whether it runs out or comes off.

  • Curse (an effect): Durational, Duration 3, Periodic Ticks on, Ticks 3; its impact -5 Health, which lands each tick. Under its impact's contained effects, Curse Burst, On Remove, landing on its Target.
  • Curse Burst (an effect): Instant, -30 Health.

A counter

Each loss another entity deals its holder sets it off, and it strikes back at that one for 10.

  • Retaliate (an ability): one stage, Apply Effects with Retaliation. Under Activation's Triggers, one trigger: Gameplay Event, the core's Impact Taken, Attribute Health, Sign Loss, Target Instigator.
  • Retaliation (an effect): Instant, -10 Health.

Its own hit is a loss to the attacker, not to its holder, so it doesn't set itself off. For a chance, as Counter Helix's 20%, set the trigger's Chance; to strike everyone near, give the ability Auto Target and leave the trigger's Target at Own.

A crit

A hit doubles on a 25% chance.

  • Critical Strike (a Value Pipeline): one Chance stage, Chance 0.25, Multiplier 2, Note "critical".
  • Crit Blow (an effect): Instant, -20 Health, its impact's Pipeline Critical Strike.

The hit's breakdown notes the crit, as the combat log shows it. The stage's Roll sets pseudo-random and luck.

An execute

A hit that would leave its target under a fifth of its Health takes the rest.

  • Execute (a Value Pipeline): one Execute stage, Share 0.2, Attribute Health.
  • Culling Blow (an effect): Instant, -15 Health, its impact's Pipeline Execute.

Lifesteal

While it lasts, its holder heals half of each loss it deals another.

  • Lifesteal (an attribute), the Share of the Lifesteal worker in your Forge Project's Workers, with Health in its Health role.
  • Vampirism (an effect): Infinite, +0.5 Lifesteal, which the Lifesteal worker heals by, the one your Forge Project's Workers give every entity as a project starts, to its Health role's attribute (WORKERS.md).

An effect has no lifesteal of its own: for a blow that steals more than others, an effect raises its caster's Lifesteal while it should, and an item gives it as one of its Granted Effects (ITEMS.md).

Damage return

While it lasts, half of each loss its holder takes goes back to the one who dealt it.

  • Thorns (an effect): Infinite; its Worker Group, one attribute worker, Return Damage, Target Attribute Health, Share 0.5.

What it returns is typed Returned, so nothing returns it again and no lifesteal heals from it.

Can't die

While it lasts, no blow takes its target below 1, as Shallow Grave.

  • Undying (an effect): Durational, Duration 5; its Worker Group, one attribute worker, Can't Fall Below, Target Attribute Health, Floor 1.

On hit

While it's equipped, each hit its holder deals bites for 5 more.

  • Frost Blade (an item): its On Hit, one entry, Attribute Health, Effects Frost Bite, To Target.
  • Frost Bite (an effect): Instant, -5 Health.

Frost Bite's own hit sets nothing off again.

On kill

While it's equipped, the hit that takes a target's Health to 0 feeds its holder 20.

  • Reaper's Mark (an item): its On Hit, one entry, Attribute Health, Effects Feast, To Holder, Only On Kill on.
  • Feast (an effect): Instant, +20 Health.

A phase

As the boss's Health falls to half, it enrages, once.

  • Boss (an attribute set): Health 1000; in its Worker Group, one analysis worker, Threshold, Target Health, What Share, Threshold 0.5, Crossing Falling, Trigger Once on, Effects Enrage.
  • Enrage (an effect): Infinite, granting Recipe.Enraged.

The phase's own moves start from the tag: an ability with a Tag Added trigger on Recipe.Enraged, or a stage condition that reads it. The worker's Event sends a gameplay event as well, for a trigger of its own.

Formulas

Scaler assets for a genre's formulas, under Formulas, each named for what it computes. A field reads one with its Shared kind (SCALERS.md); each is a Stacking Curve of a count, with an a.

  • 1 ÷ (1 + 0.06 × Armor): Reciprocal, a 0.06, of the hit's target's Armor. In a Value Pipeline's Multiply stage, a hit leaves all of itself at 0 Armor, a half at about 17, never none.
  • 100 ÷ (100 + Armor): Reciprocal, a 0.01: a half at 100 Armor, a third at 200.
  • −Armor: Linear, a −1. In an Add stage, a blow of 100 against 20 Armor lands as 80.
  • 0.15 × Stacks: Linear, a 0.15, of an item's stacks, a bonus that grows by as much with each.
  • 1 − 1 ÷ (1 + 0.15 × Stacks): Hyperbolic, a chance that nears 1 and never reaches it: 13% with one stack, 60% with ten.
  • 0.9 ^ Stacks: Exponential, a share that falls by a tenth with each stack.

The Armor ones read the Armor attribute in Formulas/Attributes, as the hit lands; point them at your own.

A starter set

Plain assets named for what they are, under Starter Set; the recipes above hold the rest of a starter set, a damage over time, an on hit, an on kill and a boss's phase. Each hit reads the recipes' Health.

  • Melee Attack (an ability): Apply Effects, Melee Hit, on what it's cast at; Cooldown Melee Cooldown. Melee Hit: Instant, -10 Health. Melee Cooldown: Durational, 1 s.
  • Bolt (an ability): the same, its Bolt Hit -25 Health, its Bolt Cooldown 3 s.
  • Slow (an effect): Durational, 3 s, Multiply Movement Speed by 0.7, back as it ends.
  • Shield (an effect): Durational, 5 s, +50 to Shield's current and base while it lasts; its Modifiers, one Incoming rule whose Change Impact on Health adds an Absorb stage into Shield, so each hit on its holder's Health comes off its Shield first. Give Shield's element a base of 0, between 0 and its base, so it's 0 without one.

They read the Movement Speed and Shield attributes in Starter Set/Attributes.
