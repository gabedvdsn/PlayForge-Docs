Prefabs

The lab plays the project's own prefabs as well as its assets, so you can try your characters as your game builds them. A prefab plays in the lab where it has a GameplayAbilitySystem whose Entity Data is set.

The library lists them under Prefabs:

  • In the editor, each such prefab in the project's own folders. While a Forge Project is active, it lists those in the project's folders, and those in no project's folder whose entity is the project's or in no project.
  • Those the Creator Lab component's Prefabs list names, as a build has no editor to find them.

You put a prefab down as you put an entity down: drop it on the ground, pick it in the Put Down section, or seat it in a battle. It stands as a unit of the lab's, a LabUnit wrapped around an instance of the prefab:

  • Its body is the prefab's own, with the lab's ring at its feet in its side's color.
  • Its GAS is the prefab's, made from a copy of its entity, at the level picked, on the side picked, in the lab's world.
  • A click picks it by its own colliders, or by a capsule the lab gives it where it has none.
  • Its rigidbodies are moved by the lab, not by physics, and its animators lose their root motion, so the lab's walk moves it.
  • Its own scripts are turned off, so a controller or an AI of its own leaves it to the lab's orders. PlayForge's scripts, and its processes, stay on.

Every lab feature then works on it: selecting and dragging it, its orders and casts, its side, what's dropped on it, its reach ring, the bar over it, and its place in a scenario.

The Creator Lab component's Prefab Scripts says what becomes of a prefab's own scripts:

Choice What it does
Turn Off (default) Turns them off. Each still wakes, as Unity wakes a script that's off.
Keep Leaves them on, as its views that follow its GAS need. A controller of its own may fight the lab's orders.
Note

A NavMeshAgent on a prefab turns off in the lab, as the lab's ground has no NavMesh. Of the processes on a prefab, the lab registers its GAS alone.
