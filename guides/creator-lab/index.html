The Creator Lab

The Creator Lab is a space for trying a project's own assets. You put units down, give them abilities, effects and items, cast, and watch the numbers move.

The Creator Lab: a quick battle with the Demo's heroes, a unit selected.

To open it, choose Window → PlayForge → Creator Lab. It opens in a new scene and plays. The scenes open before are saved first, if you want them saved.

The lab plays every PlayForge asset in the project's own folders.

In this guide

  • The space: The space is the scene the lab plays in: its ground, its camera, its units and the lab's caster.
  • The panels: The lab's panels sit at the edges of the screen.
  • Dropping assets: You give assets to units by dragging them from the library and dropping them on the space.
  • Test dummies: A test dummy is a unit that stands and counts what it takes.
  • Battle: The Battle mode fights teams of bots.
  • Your unit: In either mode you can play a unit of your own, with the Demo's controls.
  • Time: The lab's units live in a world of the lab's own (ProcessWorld), which the lab steps by its own time.
  • Showing its working: The bottom panel's pages show what happens as the lab plays.
  • The lab's rules: The lab's rules tell it a few things about a project: which attribute is a unit's health, which it walks at, which tags stop, hide or shield it, and which ability is its attack.
  • In code: The lab can be made, driven and read from code.
