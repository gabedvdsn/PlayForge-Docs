The Creator Lab

A space for trying a project's own assets: put units down, give them abilities, effects and items, cast, and watch the numbers move. Open it from Window → PlayForge → Creator Lab: it opens in a new scene and plays. The scenes open before are saved first, if you want them saved.

The lab plays every PlayForge asset in the project's own folders.

In this guide

  • The space: The ground is a plane in the middle of the scene, lit from above.
  • The panels: Each panel but Selected collapses to a tab at its edge with its − button.
  • Dropping assets: Drag an asset from the library and drop it on the space
  • Test dummies: The Test dummies section puts down a dummy of any entity or attribute set, on a side: Blue, Red, Green, Gold or Neutral.
  • Battle: The Battle mode fights teams of bots.
  • Your unit: In either mode you can play a unit of your own.
  • Time: The lab's units live in a world of the lab's own (ProcessWorld), which the lab steps by its own time, so its time stands still or runs faster without touching Unity's.
  • Showing its working: The bottom panel's pages show what happens as the lab plays.
  • The lab's rules: The lab needs to know a few things about a project: which attribute is a unit's health, which it walks at, which tags stop, hide or shield it, and which ability is its attack.
  • In code: CreatorLab.Create(library, rules, panels) makes a lab, as the tests do.
