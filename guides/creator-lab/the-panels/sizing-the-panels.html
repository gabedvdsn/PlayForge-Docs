Sizing the panels

You can make the panels larger or smaller, and their words larger, to read them on any screen.

Control What it does
A panel's inner edge Drag it to resize the panel: the library's right edge, the Lab and Selected panels' left edge, and the log's top edge. The log and the status between them move to fit.
UI Size Draws the panels from half to three times as large, their words with them.
Text Size Sets the panels' words from 12 to 24 points, apart from the UI size. A table's rows grow to hold them.
Reset Puts the sizes back as they start.

UI Size, Text Size and Reset are in the Lab panel's Interface section. Type a size, or step it with − and +. Where the screen at the UI size can't hold the library and the panels at the right, both narrow alike.

Each size is also a property of the Creator Lab component:

Property Description
UI Scale How large the panels are drawn, from 0.5 to 3.
Text Size How large their words are, in points, from 12 to 24.
Library Width How wide the library is, before the UI size.
Panel Width How wide the Lab and Selected panels are, before the UI size.
Log Height How tall the log is, as a share of the screen's height.

What you change while the lab plays lasts until it stops, as any change made in Play mode does. To keep your sizes, set them on the Creator Lab object of a scene you save.
