The Lab panel

The Lab panel, on the right under the Selected panel, holds a section for each part of the lab.

Section What it does
Space Picks the mode (Modes). Clear Space takes every unit out.
Put Down Picks the Entity, Side and Level of what you put down. Add Dummy, Add Bot or Put Down Yours puts it down (Test dummies, Battle, Your unit).
Units Lists the units in the space, with their side, level and health. Click one to select it, or double-click it to look at it. Reset Dummies puts each dummy back.
Battle Runs a fight, in the Battle mode (Battle).
Your Unit Says whom you control, and takes control or lets go (Your unit).
Camera Distance, Tilt and Reset View (The camera).
Time Pause, Step and Speed (Time).
Scenarios Saves the lab's setup and loads it again (Scenarios).
