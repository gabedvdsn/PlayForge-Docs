The Lab panel

The Lab panel, on the right under the Selected panel, holds the mode, the test dummies, the camera and the lab's time.

Section What it does
The mode buttons Pick the mode (Modes).
Entity and Side Pick the entity and the side a dummy, a bot or your unit is put down as.
Test dummies Puts down dummies, in the Dummies mode (Test dummies).
Your unit Puts down a unit for you to control, or takes one (Your unit).
Battle Adds bots and runs a fight, in the Battle mode (Battle).
Camera Its Distance and Tilt sliders, and Reset view (The camera).
Time Pause, Step and Speed (Time).
