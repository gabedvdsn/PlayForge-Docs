The Library panel

The Library panel, on the left, lists the active Forge Project's assets by kind, each kind by name.

The kinds are abilities, effects, items, entities, prefabs, attribute sets, attributes and scalers. While the project has no Forge Project, the panel lists every asset of the project. A kind's color shows in its icon alone.

Control What it does
Project Switches the active project, as The Forge does. It also follows The Forge's switch.
The search box Filters the assets.
Refresh Lists the assets again, after you make or delete one.
Click an asset Opens its card under its row. Click it again to close the card.
Drag an asset Carries its card under the pointer, to drop on the space (Dropping assets).

An asset's card says what the asset is and what it does:

Asset Its card
An ability Its level, its targeting, its cost, its cooldown, its components' numbers, and each stage's tasks.
An effect How long it lasts, its chance, the tags it grants, and each impact by amount and attribute, level by level.
An item Its active ability, and the effects it grants.
An entity or an attribute set Its abilities, and its attributes as it starts.
A prefab Its entity, and the entity's abilities and attributes.

A card in the library also has Open in The Forge, in the editor. The asset clicked is also the one a quick battle takes (Battle).
