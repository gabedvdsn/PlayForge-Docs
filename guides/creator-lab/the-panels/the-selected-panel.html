The Selected panel

The Selected panel shows the unit selected, as the Debugger shows an entity.

It sits on the right, over the Lab panel, and shows as a unit is selected. Its Lab tab sets it aside, the selection kept. The Lab panel's Selected tab brings it back, as each selection does.

Its header names the unit and says what it is, as "Entity · Brute · Red · Level 3 · Dummy".

Button What it does
Take Control Makes the unit yours (Your unit).
Release Lets your unit go.
Follow Has the camera follow the unit.
Remove Takes the unit out.
Deselect Selects nothing.

Under the header, a section for each part of the unit. Each is a table: click a row to select it, and the section's foot row acts on it.

Section Its table Its foot row
Attributes Each attribute's Current and Base values, and the effects that change it. Sets the selected attribute's current and base values.
Effects Each effect on the unit, its time left, its stacks and where it came from. Remove takes the selected one off. Apply lands the one picked, from the unit itself.
Abilities Each ability the unit knows, its level, what it's doing, and its cooldown's time left. Cast casts the selected one, as the unit would. Interrupt stops it. − and + change its level, and Forget takes it. Teach gives the unit the one picked.
Tags Each tag the unit has, its count, and whether it's loose. Remove takes back a loose tag. Add Tag gives the unit the tag typed, or picked from the project's.
Items Each item the unit holds, and its level. Take takes the selected one. Give gives the unit the one picked, equipped.
What It Took A test dummy's count of what it took (Test dummies).

An ability cast from the panel that needs a target waits for your click, as your unit's do (Controls). A tag you add is loose: it stays until you remove it.
