The lab's rules

The lab needs to know a few things about a project: which attribute is a unit's health, which it walks at, which tags stop, hide or shield it, and which ability is its attack. With no rules given, it takes the attributes playing the project's Health and Movement Speed roles (Attributes: Roles), or else finds the attributes named Health and Movement Speed (or Move Speed), and walks a unit without a speed at 4 a second. To say more, make a Lab Rules asset (Create → PlayForge → Lab Rules) and give it to the lab's CreatorLab component:

  • Health and Movement Speed, or Walk Speed where a unit has none. Left empty, each is the project's.
  • Stops: tags that stop a unit moving, as Stunned. Hides: tags under which the other side can't see or target it, as Invisible. Shields: tags under which no one can target it.
  • Attack Range Key: the Local Data key a unit's attack range is under, or Attack Range for every unit; a unit whose attack has a reach of its own, as a Cast Range, attacks from as far as it reaches.
  • Attack Slot: which of an entity's starting abilities is its attack, from 0, or −1 for none.
  • Driver: the project's Lab Driver, which the unit you control is ordered about through (Your unit).

The Demo's roster is the Demo's rules: its units, camera and dummy are the lab's, so the two can't drift.
