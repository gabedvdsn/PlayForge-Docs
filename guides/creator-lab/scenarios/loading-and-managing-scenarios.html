Loading and managing scenarios

The section's table lists the library's scenarios, with their units and when each was saved. Click one to select it.

Control What it does
Load Sets the lab up as the scenario saved it, in place of what's in the space. A double-click on a row does the same.
Save Over Saves the lab as it is now over the scenario selected. It asks first.
Delete Moves the scenario selected to the trash. It asks first.

A unit whose entity is gone is left out as a scenario loads. In a build, the lab loads the scenarios its project's catalog names, but saves none.
