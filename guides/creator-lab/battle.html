Battle

The Battle mode fights teams of bots. Pick an entity and a side above, a level in Level, and Add bot; each side stands in a line of its own, Blue to the south, Red to the north, Green to the west and Gold to the east. Quick battle makes Blue against Red, A side of each, of the entity selected in the library, or picked at random from the project's. Play starts the fight and the lab's time; the line under the buttons says who stands, and who won, or that no one stands. Reset makes every bot anew where it started, at full, with the items and abilities you gave it since and their levels.

A bot takes the nearest foe it can target, casts what's ready, and otherwise attacks with its attack. Its order and its moments are its rules':

  • Cast Order Key: the Local Data key an entity's order is under, as "W Q E", the letters its abilities other than its attack, in its entity's order. With none, each in turn.
  • Cast When Key: the key for when it casts an ability: InRange (the default), Hurt (below 60% health), Low (below 35%, away from its foe, as an escape), Stunned (while something stops it) or FoeLow (its foe below half).
  • Reach Key: the key for an ability's reach, 0 for anywhere, where it has no reach of its own, as a Cast Range; one that has reaches as far as that does.

Low on health, a bot backs off a moment once in a while. The Demo's AI hero is a bot, its roster giving the keys.
