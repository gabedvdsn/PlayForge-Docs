The panels in code

The lab's panels are its Panels, a LabPanels, built of the lab's own controls, which draw PlayForge's editor style in a game as in the editor:

Control What it is
LabSection A section under a foldout header, with its count.
LabTables A table's head, rows, cells and foot row, and its selected row.
LabDropdown A field that picks one of its choices from a LabMenu, as wide as its longest choice.
LabGlyph A glyph of PlayForge's, a LabGlyphShape, as an asset type's icon.
LabCard An asset's card, of its LabCardData.
