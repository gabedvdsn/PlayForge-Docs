The panels

The lab's panels sit at the edges of the screen. The middle of the screen is the space's.

The panels look as PlayForge's editor windows do: each section under a header you can fold, tables of values with a row you select, and a foot row of actions on the row selected.

Each dropdown opens a menu with a search, ready to type in: your words narrow its choices, the arrows move through them, and Enter picks the one lit.

Panel Where What it holds
Library Left The active Forge Project's assets, by kind.
Selected Right, over the Lab panel The unit selected. It's shown as a unit is selected.
Lab Right, under Selected The space, what's put down, the units, the battle, your unit, the camera, the lab's time, its interface and its scenarios.
Log Bottom A page for each way the lab shows its working. It's collapsed until you open it.
The status Top, between the panels What a cast waits for, what's selected, what just happened, and the keys for what you can do now.

Each panel but Selected collapses to a tab at its edge with its − button.

In this section

  • Sizing the panels: You can make the panels larger or smaller, and their words larger, to read them on any screen.
  • The Library panel: The Library panel, on the left, lists the active Forge Project's assets by kind, each kind by name.
  • The Selected panel: The Selected panel shows the unit selected, as the Debugger shows an entity.
  • The Lab panel: The Lab panel, on the right under the Selected panel, holds a section for each part of the lab.
  • The Log panel: The Log panel, at the bottom, shows the lab's working as it plays.
  • The status: The status runs along the top of the screen, between the panels, and says what's going on.
