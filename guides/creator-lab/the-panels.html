The panels

Each panel but Selected collapses to a tab at its edge with its − button. The middle of the screen is the space's.

  • Library (left): the active Forge Project's assets by kind, abilities, effects, items, entities, attribute sets, attributes and scalers, each kind by name, or every asset of the project while it has no Forge Project. Project switches the active project, as The Forge does, and follows The Forge's switch. The search box filters them; Refresh lists them again after you make or delete one. A kind's color shows in its icon alone.
  • Selected (right, over the Lab panel): shown as something is selected. Its Lab tab sets it aside, the selection kept, and the Lab panel's Selected tab brings it back, as each selection does. Click an asset in the library to see its numbers level by level, as "−80/−120/−160 Health"; Open in The Forge opens it there. Click a unit to see its attributes, tags, effects with their stacks and time left, abilities with their levels and cooldowns, and items. There, − and + change an ability's level, × takes an ability or an item, Remove takes the unit out, and Take control makes it yours, or Release lets it go.
  • Lab (right, under Selected): the mode, the test dummies, the camera and the lab's time.
  • Log (bottom): collapsed until you open it, a page for each way the lab shows its working; see below.
  • The status (top, between the panels): what a cast waits for, in its targeting's words, as "Bolt: Click a target · Esc or a right-click cancels"; what's selected, whom you control, and whether the lab is paused; and the keys for what you can do now. Clicks go through it to the space.
