Test dummies

A test dummy is a unit that stands and counts what it takes.

The Lab panel's Put Down section puts down a dummy of any entity, attribute set or prefab, on a side: Blue, Red, Green, Gold or Neutral.

Button Where What it does
Add Dummy Put Down Puts down a dummy of the entity picked, on the side picked, at the level picked.
Reset Dummies Units Puts each dummy back where it was put, at full.
Clear Space Space Takes every unit out.

A dummy:

  • counts what it takes: its damage a second, the last blow by its impact types and the effect it came from, and a short history, which the Selected panel shows;
  • doesn't die unless asked: falling, it's revived where it stands, at full;
  • mends to full when it's left alone for three seconds.

Changing assets as the lab plays

An asset you change while the lab plays takes effect the next time it's read.

  • An effect or an entity changed in the inspector or The Forge as the lab plays is read on its next application, as every asset is.
  • What a unit was made from, its attribute set and its starting abilities, shows on a new unit.
