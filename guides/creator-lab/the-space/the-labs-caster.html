The lab's caster

The lab's caster is the source of what you drop while no unit is selected. It has no body, so nothing picks or sees it.

What it casts and lands passes every check but a death:

  • it pays no cost and takes no cooldown;
  • its abilities pass their Cast Range, their tag requirements, the crowd control, and their Caster and Target Checks;
  • its effects land past their affiliation and their conditions.

To select it, click the anvil in the Units section's header. The Selected panel shows it as a unit, named The Lab.

It holds no attributes of the project's until you give it an attribute set:

  1. Select the lab's caster.
  2. In the Selected panel's header, pick a set in Set.
  3. Select Use.

The caster is made anew with the set's attributes, and stays selected. Clear takes the set away. A caster that falls, as its set's Health may let it, rises again at once.
