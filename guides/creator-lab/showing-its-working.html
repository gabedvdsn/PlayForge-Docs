Showing its working

The bottom panel's pages show what happens as the lab plays. Each records only while the lab runs.

  • Journal: the lab's effect journal over every unit: each effect landing, with its number traced, refused and why, ticking, hitting, coming off, and each worker that ran, by its hook and its side, with the actions it queued and whether they ran; newest first, on the selected unit or, with Selected unit off, on any. Click an entry to see its parts: a number's, a hit's breakdown, or a worker's actions.
  • Combat: the selected unit's combat log, newest first, each hit with its steps under it, "-30 as made → Math -30 → -15 (inline) → landed -15". The project's combat log writes it (Project Settings → PlayForge → Combat Log).
  • Stops: a list of what pauses the lab's world: an effect lands, is refused or has a worker run, an attribute crosses a value, a tag comes or goes, an ability is refused, each on the selected unit or any, and naming an effect, an ability, a tag or an attribute where it asks for one (Any for an effect or an ability takes any). As one fires, the world pauses and the page says which and what fired it. Go plays on, Step Frame goes one frame, and Step Tick goes on until an effect ticks on the selected unit, or on any, up to ten seconds, or a stop fires.
  • Watches: pin a number on the selected unit, an attribute's current or base value, a tag's count, an effect's stacks or time left, or a Scaler asset read on it, and see each as a line over the last minute of the lab's time, in its own color and scaled to its own range, with its value now.
  • Log: PlayForge's messages as ForgeLog lets them through, by system, newest first, those about the selected unit where Selected unit is on. System and Level give a system a level of its own, which the Console keeps too; As all gives it back to the Logging preference's.
