Showing its working

The bottom panel's pages show what happens as the lab plays. Each records only while the lab runs.

In this section

  • Journal: The Journal page is the lab's effect journal over every unit, newest first.
  • Combat: The Combat page is the selected unit's combat log, newest first.
  • Stops: The Stops page holds a list of what pauses the lab's world.
  • Watches: The Watches page pins numbers on the selected unit, and draws each over time.
  • Log: The Log page shows PlayForge's messages as ForgeLog lets them through, by system, newest first.
