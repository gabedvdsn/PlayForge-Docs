Reading a sheet back

A row names its asset both ways: by its asset tag, which stays as files move and are renamed, and by its name, which reads well in a spreadsheet. A row with a tag finds the asset with that tag; a row with none finds the one asset of that name. These are reported, and their rows left:

  • a tag no asset has, or a name no asset has, or one that two assets share (give that row its tag);
  • a field the asset hasn't got, or a kind a sheet can't set;
  • an item with no effect adding to a column's attribute;
  • a cell that isn't a number;
  • two rows setting one shared effect's number to different values.

The import window lists each change: the asset, the field, what it reads now and what it will. Each is ticked; untick one to leave it. The rows that can't be read sit under a fold. Import makes the ticked changes as one Undo step. A sheet read back just as it was written changes nothing.
