Spreadsheets

Games balance in spreadsheets: a column of damage by level, a row of stats for each item. PlayForge writes its numbers by level and its items' stats to CSV, which any spreadsheet opens, and reads a sheet back, listing each change before it makes it. The Forge's window menu (its tab's ⋮) holds them under Spreadsheets:

  • Export Numbers… writes a sheet of every number by level in the active Forge Project; Export Selected Numbers… the selected assets' alone.
  • Export Item Stats… writes a sheet of every item's stats; Export Selected Item Stats… the selected items'.
  • Import… reads a sheet back, of either kind, and opens the import window.

A sheet is UTF-8, marked so, as Excel and Google Sheets open it. In code: ForgeSpreadsheets.NumbersOf(assets) and StatsOf(items) give a sheet's text, and ForgeSpreadsheets.Read(csv) what it would change.

In this guide

  • Numbers by level: A row for each number whose kind is a Table or By Level (Scalers), of every asset, and of each Scaler asset, whose table its fields share
  • Item stats: A row for each item, and a column for each attribute its granted effects add to
  • Reading a sheet back: A row names its asset both ways: by its asset tag, which stays as files move and are renamed, and by its name, which reads well in a spreadsheet.

In the API

The types of this guide's area: ForgeSpreadsheets, ForgeSpreadsheetWindow, SpreadsheetChange, SpreadsheetImport.
