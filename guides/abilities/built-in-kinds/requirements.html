Requirements

Kind Menu What it does
AbilityLevelValidation (Ability Level) — Activates only where the ability's level compares with a number, as a form it unlocks at a rank.
AffiliationValidation (Affiliation) — Activates only on a target that stands to its caster as it says: an enemy, an ally, itself, or anyone else.
AttributeValidation — Activates only where an attribute of the checked entity, or its share of its base, compares with a value.
CheckRangeValidation (Check Range) — The Cast Range component's check: its target within the component's range of its caster. It comes and goes with the component.
ConditionValidation — Activates only while its condition holds: a tag, an attribute, a level, or any other condition.
CooldownValidation — Activates only where the ability isn't cooling down, or with its charges counted, has one in hand.
CostValidation — Activates only where the caster can pay the cost.
FacingValidation (Facing) — Activates only where its target lies within an angle in front of its caster, or its caster within an angle behind its target.
HasEffectValidation (Has Effect) — Activates only where the checked entity has an effect on, by asset or by tag; or, with Without, where it hasn't.
HoldsItemValidation (Holds Item) — Activates only where the checked entity holds an item; or, with Equipped, wears it.
IsAliveValidation — Activates only while the checked entity lives: the caster among the Caster Checks, the target among the Target Checks.
LineOfSightValidation (Line of Sight) — Activates only where nothing on the layers given blocks a line from the caster to its target; 3D physics colliders block it.
TagWeightValidation (Tag Weight) — Activates only where the checked entity holds a tag at a weight that compares with a number, as combo points or stacks.
