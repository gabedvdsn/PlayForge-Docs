Workers

Workers are reusable behaviours, picked from a list, that react to what happens to an entity. This group is subscribed to the entity when it learns the ability, and removed when the ability is removed. Name labels the group. It holds four lists:

  • Attribute workers run before or after an attribute changes, for example to reduce incoming damage.
  • Impact workers inspect the changes this entity deals.
  • Tag workers react when the entity's tags change.
  • Analysis workers examine the frame at its end, for example to detect death.

Each list's + adds an empty worker to pick a kind for. The section's import button copies a worker group from another asset.

On Hit

On Hit, above the workers, holds the effects the entity's hits apply while it knows the ability, as a passive's. Its entries are an item's: an attribute, the effects, to the one hit or the holder, only on a kill, impact types and a condition (Items: On Hit).

Modifiers

Modifiers, under On Hit, change other effects as they're made, while the entity knows the ability: its holder's own, as a boon that adds a burst to its holder's every Chill, or those landing on it, as a cloak that halves the stuns its wearer takes. Each entry is a rule of its own or names a shared Effect Modifier asset (Effects: Modifiers).

Lifesteal

An ability gives its holder lifesteal by an effect that raises the holder's Lifesteal, the attribute the project's Lifesteal worker names as its Share, as a passive applies it to its caster, which the Lifesteal worker heals by (Workers: Lifesteal): a vampiric mask's Infinite effect of +0.15 Lifesteal heals its wearer 15% of each blow it deals. Its Lifesteal list is gone.
