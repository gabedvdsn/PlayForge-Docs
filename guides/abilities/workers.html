Workers

Workers are reusable behaviours, picked from a list, that react to what happens to an entity. This group is subscribed to the entity when it learns the ability, and removed when the ability is removed.

Name labels the group. It holds four lists:

List What its workers do
Attribute Run before or after an attribute changes, for example to reduce incoming damage.
Impact Inspect the changes this entity deals.
Tag React when the entity's tags change.
Analysis Examine the frame at its end, for example to detect death.
  • Each list's + adds an empty worker to pick a kind for.
  • The section's import button copies a worker group from another asset.

In this section

  • On Hit: On Hit, above the workers, holds the effects the entity's hits apply while it knows the ability, as a passive's.
  • Modifiers: Modifiers, under On Hit, change other effects as they're made, while the entity knows the ability.
  • Lifesteal: An ability gives its holder lifesteal by an effect that raises the holder's Lifesteal, as a passive applies it to its caster.
