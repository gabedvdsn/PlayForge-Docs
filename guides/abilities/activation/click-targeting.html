Click targeting

Click targeting waits for a press and aims along the game's input, ForgeInput.Current. Its tasks are the Select tasks: Position, Direction, GAS Target, GAS Or Position and GAS Target In Range.

Input and cameras

By default, the game's input is the Input System's pointer, a mouse, a pen or a touch, where its package is in and on. Otherwise it's the Input Manager's mouse. Each is aimed through Camera.main, and with no camera, a press aims nowhere.

To aim another way:

  • For a camera of your own, give the default one: new LegacyPointerInput { Camera = camera }, or InputSystemPointerInput.
  • For a gamepad's reticle or a controller's ray, write an IForgeInput.
  • Put either in with ForgeInput.Use(input).

Targets handed in the data

An activation that already holds its target, under TargetReal, or its point, under Position, takes it with no click, as an AI's does. This holds unless the task's Take Target From Data is off.

A handed target the task can't take, out of range or failing its requirements, fails the targeting at once.

Prompts

While a click targeting task waits, its slot's IsTargeting is true. The task's Prompt gives the words to ask with, as "Click a target", for a game's own screen. The Creator Lab and the Demo show them this way (The Creator Lab).
