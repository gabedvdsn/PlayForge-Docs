Waived checks

An activation can waive some of its checks: its cost, its cooldown and its requirements. Each is a flag of EUsageCheck: Cost, Cooldown and Requirements.

Waived by How
A component Its Waives, for this activation, as a Toggle that's on waives its cost and its cooldown.
The activation's data Tags.WAIVES, which holds the checks this activation waives.
The caster Its GAS's WaivedChecks, the checks every use of its abilities waives. The Creator Lab's own caster waives all three (The Creator Lab: The space: The lab's caster).
  • A waived Cost or Cooldown passes its check, and the activation doesn't pay it.
  • Waived Requirements pass the tag requirements, the crowd control, the Caster and Target Checks, and what the ability's components refuse of the caster, the target and the point picked, as a Cast Range does. A check or a component that refuses as a cost, a cooldown or a death still asks.
  • A caster whose WaivedChecks holds Requirements lands its effects past their affiliation and their conditions too (Effects: Impact: Affiliation).
