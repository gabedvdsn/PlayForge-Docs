Activation

The Activation section sets how the ability starts: when it may start, what sets it off, what it costs and how it picks its target.

Property Description
When Busy What happens when the ability is asked to activate while another ability is busy. See When Busy.
While Targeting Whether the ability gives way, while its targeting waits for the player, as another of the entity's abilities activates. See While Targeting.
Buffer Window and Buffer Priority The input buffer's window and priority, under Queue. See The input buffer.
Activate At Start When the ability is one of the entity's Starting Abilities, it tries to activate once, as the entity's GAS starts. An ability that an item or code grants later doesn't try it.
Triggers What sets the ability off on its own. See Triggers.
Chance to Activate How likely an activation is to start, from 0 to 1, rolled once for each, a player's or a trigger's, after its other checks. A miss is refused with Chance.
Activation Roll Makes Chance to Activate pseudo-random or lucky. It shows for a chance that can miss.
Proc Coefficient What its effects' hits carry for on-hit effects, where an effect sets none of its own.
Ignores Crowd Control The crowd control rules the ability ignores, by their tags, as Black King Bar's Avatar ignores Silenced; a tag ignores the rules under it too. The other rules still stop it, and still interrupt it as it runs (see Crowd Control).
Cost and Cooldown Gameplay effects applied to the caster as the ability starts, or by the stage that takes them. See Cost and Cooldown.
Attribute Costs What the ability spends of attributes as it's used, beside its Cost effect. See Attribute Costs.
Targeting The task that picks what the ability acts on. See Targeting.
Use Implicit Targeting Records the caster as the activation's target before targeting. See Implicit targeting.

In this section

  • How an ability starts: One activation of an ability goes through these steps
  • Requesting an activation: Code asks for an ability in one of three ways, and each returns an ActivationResult.
  • Refusals: Each form of request can say why it was refused, through its result's Refusal or an out ActivationRefusal.
  • When Busy: When Busy sets what happens when the ability is asked to activate while another ability is busy.
  • While Targeting: While Targeting sets whether the ability, while its targeting waits for the player, gives way as another of the entity's abilities activates.
  • Triggers: Triggers are what set the ability off on its own.
  • Cost and Cooldown: Cost and Cooldown are gameplay effects (Effects) that the ability applies to its caster.
  • Targeting: Targeting is the task that picks what the ability acts on, such as an entity the player clicks, or the caster itself.
  • Click targeting: Click targeting waits for a press and aims along the game's input, ForgeInput.Current.
  • Auto targeting: Auto targeting takes the entities within a range.
