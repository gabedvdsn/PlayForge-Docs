Requirements

Requirements are what an activation must pass. If one fails, the ability doesn't run.

  • The tag requirements table holds tag checks, a row for each side. Caster checks the caster, with the Caster Checks. While active checks the caster for as long as the ability runs: when it fails, the ability is interrupted, so a channel that must not have State.Stunned ends as a stun lands, and while it fails, the ability can't activate. Target checks the target the same way, after targeting. Each row's Requires cell lists the tags the entity must have, and its Must not have cell the tags it must not have, such as State.Stunned, or says None. A row passes when the entity has every tag it requires and none it must not have, so an empty row always passes. Click a cell to edit its list under the table, with the row's Template and Sync With Template (see Effects). Each entry is a tag query: a Tag, a Match mode such as Include Children, and a comparison with a number. Greater Than 0 means "the tag is present".
  • Caster Checks check the caster. They run when the ability is asked to activate, before targeting, and again after targeting. The defaults are Cooldown, Cost and Is Alive. You pick each check's type from a list, which offers the caster's types and those that check either side.
  • Target Checks check the target, after targeting. The default is Is Alive. If targeting found no target, they are skipped. Their list offers the target's types and those that check either side.

A check a component stands behind shows under its list, locked, as Check Range does under the Target Checks while the ability holds a Cast Range. No list holds it: it comes and goes with its component, which enforces it, and the section's line counts it among the checks.

The check types, by the list that offers them:

  • Caster Checks:
    • Cooldown: passes when the ability isn't cooling down, or, with its charges counted (see Charges), has one in hand.
    • Cost: passes when the ability has no cost, or the cost would leave each attribute it spends at 0 or more, and its Attribute Costs can be paid. The caster must have each attribute.
    • Ability Level: compares the ability's level with Level, using Comparison, as a form it unlocks at a rank. It starts at 2 or more.
  • Either list, checking the caster among the Caster Checks and the target among the Target Checks:
    • Is Alive: passes when the checked entity isn't dead.
    • Attribute: compares the checked entity's Current value of Attribute with Value, using Comparison; with Share Of Base, its share of its base, from 0 to 1, as "below half its health".
    • Tag Weight: compares the checked entity's weight of Tag with Weight, using Comparison, as combo points or stacks. It starts at 1 or more; an entity without the tag holds it at 0.
    • Has Effect: passes when the checked entity has Effect on, or any effect tagged Tagged or under it, by its asset tag, a context tag or a tag it grants; with Without, when it has none of it. Naming neither, it passes.
    • Holds Item: passes when the checked entity holds Item, or, with Equipped, wears it. Naming none, it passes.
    • Condition: passes while its condition, When, holds, as an effect's requirements and an On Hit read theirs: a tag, an attribute, a level, a comparison of numbers, or a condition of your own (see Scalers). It's read against the activation, with the checked entity as its target, so among the Caster Checks its target is the caster. With none, it passes.
  • Target Checks:
    • Affiliation: passes when the target stands to its caster as Target says, as a search's Takes does. Its Enemies share none of the caster's sides and its Allies one, the caster itself among them; Other Allies leaves the caster out, Self is the caster alone, All But Self anyone else, and Everyone anyone. A caster with no sides takes anyone as either.
    • Line of Sight: passes when nothing on the Blocking layers blocks a line from the caster to its target, run Height above each, so the ground under them doesn't. 3D physics colliders block it; triggers and the two ends' own colliders don't.
    • Facing: passes when the target lies within Angle in front of its caster, or, with Behind Target, the caster stands within the angle behind its target, as a backstab wants. Each faces as its targeting packet does, along the ground: 90 takes 45° to either side, and 360 anywhere.
    • Check Range: the Cast Range component's, which no list offers. It shows while the ability holds a Cast Range, saying its range, and passes, as the component enforces the range (see Cast Range).

A failed Caster Check refuses the activation as the kind its RefusedAs says: Cooldown, Cost or Dead for those three, which name none, and Rule, naming the check, for any other; a failed Target Check ends it after targeting, told as Target, naming the check (see Activation). Each says why in Validate's error, which the refusal carries as its Detail, as "Its caster's Mana, 20, can't pay 30".

A check of your own implements IAbilityValidationRule: Validate says whether the activation passes, and why not, and GetName names it; RefusedAs, Rule unless it says otherwise, is the kind its refusal of the caster is told as, so an ammunition check can say Cooldown as the built-in does. [ForgeMenu] names and describes it in the list, and [ValidationSide] says which list offers it, Caster or Target; without it, both do. A component stands behind a check by overriding its TargetCheck or CasterCheck with an AbstractComponentValidationRule<T> of its own kind: the check shows under the list while the ability holds the component, and the component enforces it, in CanStart.

The section's Clear button empties the tag requirements and leaves the checks, since an ability without them would skip its cost and cooldown.

Crowd Control

Crowd control holds for every ability at once, so no ability has to list Stunned among the tags it must not have. A Crowd Control Rules asset (Create → PlayForge → Crowd Control Rules) holds its rules, each a Tag the caster carries, as Stunned, and what it Stops: Every Ability, as a stun, or Abilities Tagged, those whose asset tag or a context tag is one of its Ability Tags or under it, as Silenced stops spells and Disarmed attacks. An entity's abilities follow its own Crowd Control (Entities: Abilities), or, where it names none, its Forge Project's (Attributes: Roles).

While the caster carries a rule's tag, or one under it, an ability the rule stops is refused with Crowd Control, the refusal's Rule naming the tag, and one that runs is interrupted as the tag lands, as a stun breaks a channel. An ability's Ignores Crowd Control (see Activation) opts it out of the rules it names. Code asks with rules.Stopping(ability, tags), the first rule that stops the ability, or null.
