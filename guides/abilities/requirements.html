Requirements

Requirements are what an activation must pass. If one fails, the ability doesn't run.

Requirement What it checks
Tag requirements The tags of the caster, of the caster while the ability runs, and of the target. See Tag requirements.
Caster Checks The caster, before targeting and again after it. See Caster Checks and Target Checks.
Target Checks The target, after targeting. See Caster Checks and Target Checks.
Crowd control Rules that hold for every ability at once. See Crowd Control.

In this section

  • Tag requirements: The tag requirements table holds tag checks, a row for each side.
  • Caster Checks and Target Checks: An ability's checks are rules it runs on its caster and on its target.
  • Check types: PlayForge's check types are grouped below by the list that offers them.
  • Failed checks: A failed check refuses or ends the activation, and says why.
  • Writing your own check: A check of your own implements IAbilityValidationRule.
  • Crowd Control: Crowd control holds for every ability at once, so no ability has to list Stunned among the tags it must not have.
