Components

An ability's components are features it carries, each a class with hooks into its life, added with the list's +. A kind that makes no sense twice is offered only while the ability holds none of it.

Component What it does
Cast Range The one range the ability has.
Charges How many uses the ability holds.
Toggle An effect the ability turns on and off on its caster.
Turn To Face How the ability turns its caster to face its target or its point before its first stage.
Auras An effect on those near its holder, while the entity knows the ability.
Cues What the ability shows and sounds at its moments.

To write a component of your own, see Component hooks, Writing a component and Components in code.

In this section

  • Cast Range: A Cast Range component is the one range the ability has: how far from its caster its target or its point may be.
  • Charges: A Charges component says how many uses the ability holds, each use spending a charge.
  • Toggle: A Toggle component holds an Effect the ability turns on and off on its caster, as a mana shield.
  • Turn To Face: A Turn To Face component says how this ability turns its caster to face its target or its point before its first stage.
  • Auras: An Aura component holds an effect on those near its holder, while the entity knows the ability, as a banner that inspires the allies round its bearer.
  • Cues: A Cues component plays what the ability shows and sounds at its moments, on its caster.
  • Component hooks: A component hears its ability's life through hooks, each a no-op unless the component overrides it.
  • Writing a component: A component of your own derives from AbstractAbilityComponent and overrides the hooks it needs, each a no-op otherwise.
  • Components in code: Code finds a component by what it does, not by its class.
