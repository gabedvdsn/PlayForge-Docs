Built-in kinds

The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says. A project's own kinds join them in the same pickers.

Components

Kind Menu What it does
AuraAbilityComponent — Gives those near the holder an aura's effects while it knows the ability.
CastRangeAbilityComponent — How far from the caster the ability's target may be.
ChargesAbilityComponent — How many uses the ability holds, each coming back as it recharges.
CuesAbilityComponent — Shows and sounds the ability's moments: as it activates, commits, ends or is refused, and while it's active.
ToggleAbilityComponent — Turns an effect on with one use and off with the next.
TurnToFaceAbilityComponent — Turns its caster to face its target or its point before its first stage, its own way, or not at all where its entity's Turns To Face would.

Tasks

Kind Menu What it does
ApplyEffectsAbilityTask Effects Applies its effects to the ability's targets.
ApplyRemoveEffectsAbilityTask Effects Applies its effects as its stage starts and takes them off as the stage ends.
ApplyToCasterTask Effects Applies its effects to the caster, whatever the ability targets.
AreaEffectTask Effects Applies its effects to each entity of the sides it takes within a radius of the caster, the target or a point.
BlinkTask Movement Puts the caster at the target at once: at the point within its reach, or behind the entity.
CancelAbilitiesTask Events and Tags Cancels the caster's running abilities tagged with one of its tags.
ChannelAbilityTask Waits Holds its stage for a duration nothing interrupts, as a cast bar fills.
ConsumeItemTask Items Uses up items the caster holds, as a potion is drunk.
CreateMonoProcessAbilityTask Process Spawns a process, as a projectile, whose effects are the caster's at the ability's level.
DashTask Movement Moves the caster toward the target, or away from it, over a moment.
DelayTask Waits Waits a while before its stage goes on.
DisjointAbilityTask Process Makes every disjointable shot homing on the caster lose it, as a dodge.
GrantTagsTask Events and Tags Grants tags to the caster or the target while its stage runs.
InjectAbilityTask Sequence Sends a command into the ability's own run, as an interrupt.
PlayCueTask (Play Cue) Cues Plays its cues at this moment of the stage: on the caster, the target or the point.
RunProcessTask Process Runs processes for the caster and waits for them, up to a time.
SendEventTask Events and Tags Sends a gameplay event to the caster or to each target.
SetAbilitiesTask Asset Loaders Puts abilities in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetAssetsTask Asset Loaders Puts assets in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetAttributeSetsTask Asset Loaders Puts attribute sets in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetAttributesTask Asset Loaders Puts attributes in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetEffectsTask Asset Loaders Puts effects in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetEntitiesTask Asset Loaders Puts entities in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
SetTagsTask Asset Loaders Puts tags in the ability's data under a key, for its later tasks.
UnityEventAbilityTask Utility Invokes UnityEvents, for objects in the scene to answer.
WaitForAttributeTask Waits Waits for an attribute of the caster or the target to cross a value.
WaitForEventTask Waits Waits for a gameplay event sent to the caster or the target.
WaitForTagTask Waits Waits for the caster or the target to gain a tag, or to lose it.

Targeting tasks

Kind Menu What it does
AutoTargetByAffinityTask Automatic Targets the entities in range of an affiliation, as the nearest ally to heal or every enemy in reach.
AutoTargetFromDataTask Automatic Targets the entity the ability's data holds under a key, as a trigger's subject.
AutoTargetSelfTask Automatic Targets the caster itself, checked against the task's tag requirements.
AutoTargetTask Automatic Targets the entities in range that pass its tag requirements and conditions.
CompositeTargetingTask — Runs several targeting tasks in turn, each adding its targets, as an entity and then a point.
SelectDirectionTargetTask Picked The player clicks a direction from the caster.
SelectGasOrPositionTargetTask Picked The player clicks an entity, or else a point on the ground.
SelectGasTargetInRangeTask Picked The player clicks an entity within range of the caster.
SelectGasTargetTask Picked The player clicks an entity.
SelectPositionTargetTask Picked The player clicks a point on the ground.

Requirements

Kind Menu What it does
AbilityLevelValidation (Ability Level) — Activates only where the ability's level compares with a number, as a form it unlocks at a rank.
AffiliationValidation (Affiliation) — Activates only on a target that stands to its caster as it says: an enemy, an ally, itself, or anyone else.
AttributeValidation — Activates only where an attribute of the checked entity, or its share of its base, compares with a value.
CheckRangeValidation (Check Range) — The Cast Range component's check: its target within the component's range of its caster. It comes and goes with the component.
ConditionValidation — Activates only while its condition holds: a tag, an attribute, a level, or any other condition.
CooldownValidation — Activates only where the ability isn't cooling down, or with its charges counted, has one in hand.
CostValidation — Activates only where the caster can pay the cost.
FacingValidation (Facing) — Activates only where its target lies within an angle in front of its caster, or its caster within an angle behind its target.
HasEffectValidation (Has Effect) — Activates only where the checked entity has an effect on, by asset or by tag; or, with Without, where it hasn't.
HoldsItemValidation (Holds Item) — Activates only where the checked entity holds an item; or, with Equipped, wears it.
IsAliveValidation — Activates only while the checked entity lives: the caster among the Caster Checks, the target among the Target Checks.
LineOfSightValidation (Line of Sight) — Activates only where nothing on the layers given blocks a line from the caster to its target; 3D physics colliders block it.
TagWeightValidation (Tag Weight) — Activates only where the checked entity holds a tag at a weight that compares with a number, as combo points or stacks.

Charge recharges

Kind Menu What it does
AllAtOnceRecharge — Every charge comes back at once, after the last one is spent.
EachChargeRecharge — Each spent charge comes back on a cooldown of its own.

Stage policies

Kind Menu What it does
AllProxyStagePolicy — The stage ends as its last task does.
AnyProxyStagePolicy — The stage ends as its first task does.
