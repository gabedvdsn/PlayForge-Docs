Cast Range

A Cast Range component is the one range the ability has: how far from its caster its target or its point may be.

Property Description
Range How far from its caster the target or point may be, a number that can scale by level. 0 reaches anywhere.
Flat The range is measured along the ground unless Flat is off.

How the range is enforced:

  • An activation handed a target or a point beyond it, under TargetReal or Position, as an AI's is, is refused as Range. So nothing of it is paid, and an AI can walk closer and ask again.
  • Targeting that picks a target beyond it, whichever task picked it, ends the activation before its stages, told as Range too.

Where the range is used:

  • The auto-targeting tasks measured from the caster, and Select GAS Target In Range, search within it where their Use Cast Range is on, as it is by default. Their own range is set aside.
  • The Creator Lab's units attack from their attack's Cast Range. Its bots cast an ability once their foe is within its Cast Range, where it has one (The Creator Lab).

It's the ability's reach, an IAbilityReach. Code reads it through ForgeReach, as ForgeReach.TryRead(caster, ability, out range), and a reach of your own takes its place in each of these.

While the ability holds it, its Target Checks show its Check Range, locked, saying the range (see Requirements). An ability holds one at most.
