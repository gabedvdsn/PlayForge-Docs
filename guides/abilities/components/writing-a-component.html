Writing a component

A component of your own derives from AbstractAbilityComponent and overrides the hooks it needs, each a no-op otherwise.

  • It's the ability's, shared by everything that holds it. So what it keeps for one entity's ability it keeps through its context: ctx.GetState<T>(this).
  • Describe says what it does in one line with its numbers, as a Cast Range's "Range 600". The Components section's line, The Forge's summary and the ability's breakdown say it beside its name.
  • DescribeUsage says what it adds to the ability's use, as Charges' "3 charges". The Activation section's line says it after its cost and cooldown.

Editor rules

A component's rules in the editor derive from ForgeComponentRule<T>, in the editor's assembly, with a public constructor that takes nothing.

  • The Problems view runs each on every asset that holds a component of its kind.
  • The ability's inspector says what it finds under its Components list, as it says the Charges'.
