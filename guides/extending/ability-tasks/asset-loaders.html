Asset loaders

An asset loader puts assets in the activation's data for its tasks to read.

Derive from AbstractSetAssetsTask, whose Activate does nothing. A loader overrides IsCriticalSection, and does its work in Prepare(data), with data.AddPayload(tag, asset).

Hooks

Hook When PlayForge calls it
Prepare(data) Once an activation, after its targeting and before its first stage. The loader's work goes here.
IsCriticalSection Required, as for every ability task.

An ability's loaders run once an activation, after its targeting and before its first stage. Each is prepared, activated and cleaned at once, so a loader's Activate isn't awaited.

In the picker

An ability's Asset Loaders offer every class derived from AbstractSetAssetsTask.
