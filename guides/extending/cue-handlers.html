Cue handlers

A cue handler plays feedback, a shake, a sound, an animation, at an effect's, an ability's, an entity's or an item's moments, or where a cue tag routes.

This section covers writing one; Cues: Handlers covers where cues are raised and PlayForge's handlers. Traps, last in this section, lists the traps of handlers and sinks.

In this section

  • Writing a cue handler: A cue handler of a game's own plays feedback its own way, as setting a trigger on the Animator of the one it plays on.
  • Sinks: A sink decides whether a raise plays at all, as a server's that plays none, or a client's that plays only its own player's.
  • Traps: These traps apply to every cue handler and sink.
