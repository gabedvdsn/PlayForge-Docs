Scalers, number kinds and conditions

A number field's Kind says how its number is worked out, and a Conditional's or a rule's condition says whether it holds.

This section adds to Scalers: Writing your own scaler, which covers the kinds' names, their inspectors and a number field in a class of a game's own. Here are their members, the variances and behaviours, and an example of each. Traps, last in this section, lists the traps they share.

In this section

  • Number kinds: A number kind is the simplest way to a number of a game's own: it reads its site through a NumberContext.
  • Conditions: A condition says whether something holds, as a Conditional's or a rule's does.
  • Scalers: A scaler of the full kind holds values for each level and behaviours, for a number that reads levels in a way of its own.
  • Cached scalers: A cached scaler works out an attribute set element's Scaling from the entity's cached attribute values.
  • Variances: A variance varies a Randomized scaler's value, handed a roll from 0 to 1.
  • Scaler behaviours: A scaler behaviour adjusts the value a scaler works out, as one of the scaler's Behaviours, in order.
  • Traps: These traps apply to every kind in this section.
