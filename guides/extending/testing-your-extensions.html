Testing your extensions

PlayForge's test kit runs a game's own kinds in a world of entities stepped frame by frame, in Unity's Test Runner. This section covers what it offers a kind of a game's own; Testing Your Content covers setting it up and writing tests.

  • A component, a worker or a scaler goes into an asset the kit makes: Make.Effect(name, new EffectConfig { ... }) takes Workers, effect workers of the game's own, and a MagnitudeScaler, a scaler of its own.
  • An ability task goes into Make.Stage(new PulseTask { ... }), and the stage into Make.Ability(name, stage); World.Cast(caster, ability, target) casts it.
  • A hit is World.Hit(source, target, effect), and its rolls World.Rolls(...), a scripted source put in for the test; its time World.Clock(step).
  • A number kind or a condition needs no world: number.Evaluate(NumberContext.For(caster, target, level)) and condition.Holds(context).

The kit's assembly, FarEmerald.PlayForge.Testing, is referenced by the game's test assembly; it's compiled only where Unity's Test Framework package is in the project.
