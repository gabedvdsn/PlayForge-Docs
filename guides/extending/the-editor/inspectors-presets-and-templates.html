Inspectors, presets and templates

PlayForge's inspectors share a base, and a game's own effect fields can start from the project's templates.

BasePlayForgeEditor is the base of PlayForge's asset inspectors. The Forge's presets and a project's templates are where new assets start (The Editor Tools).

A GameplayEffect field of a game's own marked [ForgeEffectTemplate("Cost")] or [ForgeEffectTemplate("Cooldown")] offers, in its right-click menu, to copy or use the project's Cost or Cooldown template. Those are the two keys it knows.
