Sinks

A sink decides whether a raise plays at all, as a server's that plays none, or a client's that plays only its own player's.

Derive from ForgeCueSink. raise.Cue is the cue, and raise.Source what raised it.

Hooks

Hook When PlayForge calls it
WillPlay(ECueEvent cueEvent, in CueRaise raise) Once for each raise of a list of cues, before any of them plays: whether it plays.

In the picker

No picker offers a sink. It's given every world as ForgeCues.Sink, or one world with ForgeCues.UseSink(world, sink). ForgeCues.Enabled = false plays none.
