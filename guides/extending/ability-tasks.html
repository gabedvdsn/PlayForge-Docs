Ability tasks

An ability task is a step of an ability's stage: it's picked in a stage's Tasks, runs with the stage, and is handed the activation's data.

What a game derives from: ability tasks, targeting tasks, ability components, validation rules and recharges. Task instances belong to the asset and are shared.

This section covers writing one; Abilities: Stages covers stages.

In this section

  • Stage tasks: A stage task is one step of an ability's stage, as applying an effect to the target several times.
  • Asset loaders: An asset loader puts assets in the activation's data for its tasks to read.
