Leveling

An entity's level is kept under its asset tag. A level provider is an asset whose level range another asset follows.

  • Link Mode: Standalone (default) keeps the entity's own range. Linked To Provider shows a provider's range instead.
  • Starting Level (default 1): the entity's level when its GAS initializes, and its lowest level.
  • Max Level (default 99): its highest level. The level always stays within this range.
  • Level Provider, while linked: the asset whose range the section shows, an Entity, Ability, Effect, Item or Attribute Set. Right-click the field to pick from one type. A link that would make a loop is refused, with an error in the Console. While no provider is set, a warning says the entity uses its own range.
  • Link Items & Abilities links this entity's Starting Items and Starting Abilities to it, so their per-level values follow its range. Unlink Items & Abilities removes the links that point at this entity. Each asks first, and one Undo takes it back.

The entity's level feeds per-level values: the effects it applies as an origin, its Max Abilities and item limits, and the cached scalers in its attribute set. Code changes the level through the GAS, with SetLevel or ModifyLevel and the entity's asset tag. At runtime, the entity's level range comes from its own Starting Level and Max Level, even while it is linked.
