Workers

Workers are reusable behaviours, picked from a list, that react to what happens to an entity. This group is subscribed when the entity's GAS initializes, and stays for the entity's lifetime. The attribute set's workers are subscribed at the same time. Name labels the group. It holds four lists:

  • Attribute workers run before or after an attribute changes, for example to reduce incoming damage.
  • Impact workers inspect the changes the entity deals.
  • Tag workers react when the entity's tags change.
  • Analysis workers examine the frame at its end, for example to detect death.

Each list's + adds an empty worker to pick a kind for. The section's import button copies a worker group from another asset.

A Threshold, an analysis worker, acts as an attribute crosses a value, without code: Megaera rages as her Health falls past half, and Ice Blast shatters below 13%. It reads its Target attribute's What: its current, its base, what's missing of it, or its share of its base, so a Threshold of 0.5 on its share is half. Crossing says when it fires: while its Comparison holds, as it always did; or as what it reads falls to the threshold or below, rises above it, or either. Trigger Once fires at its first crossing alone; off, each time. As it fires it applies its Effects to the entity, from the entity itself, or, with Applied By set to Crosser, from the one whose change crossed it this frame, so a shatter is its caster's, kill and all; and it sends its Event, a gameplay event its abilities' triggers hear, its source the one who crossed it and its subject the entity. It checks at the frame's end, as the death watcher, a Threshold that kills, does, so the frame's hits and heals net out. An entity that revives re-arms it, and its first check after notes its side again.

Two attribute workers keep the common rules. Can't Fall Below holds its attribute at its Floor: a loss that would take it lower takes it to the floor, as Shallow Grave keeps its target at 1 Health, in a buff's worker group while it lasts. Return Damage sends its Share of each workable loss its attribute takes from another back to the one who dealt it, typed Returned, so a return on the other side doesn't send it back again and no lifesteal heals from it, as Blade Mail.

Modifiers, above the workers, change other effects as they're made for as long as the entity lives: its own, as a Frost Mage's every Chill granting Frozen, or those landing on it. Each entry is a rule of its own or names a shared Effect Modifier asset (Effects: Modifiers).

An entity's own lifesteal is its Lifesteal, the attribute its project's Lifesteal worker names as its Share, its set's value for it, which the Lifesteal worker heals by (Workers: Lifesteal); its Lifesteal list is gone.
