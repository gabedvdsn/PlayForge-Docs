How a change landed

A combat log entry also tells how its change came to land.

  • Made is its change as made, before any step.
  • ChangeSteps lists each step it took on the way:
    • each attribute worker that heard it, with its Timing (before the commit or after), its Execution (inline, or the actions it Queued), and the change Before and After it;
    • a barrier's soak;
    • a gain's scaling, such as Healing Received's.
  • StepsText reads them as a line: "-30 as made → Math -30 → -15 (inline) → Answering (queued 1, after) → landed -15".

The steps are recorded only where the log keeps entries, or where something hears the entity's OnAttributeChangeStep.
