Built-in kinds

The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says. A project's own kinds join them in the same pickers.

Components

Kind Menu What it does
AuraEntityComponent — Gives those near the entity an aura's effects while it lives.
BountyEntityComponent — An effect the entity's killer takes as it dies, as gold or experience.
CueReceiverEntityComponent — Plays cue tags its own way for this entity, before the project's cue library.
CuesEntityComponent — Shows and sounds the entity's moments: as it spawns, dies and revives, and while it holds a tag.
RespawnEntityComponent — Brings the entity back a while after it dies.
TurnsToFaceEntityComponent — Its abilities turn it to face their target or point before their first stage.

Respawn places

Kind Menu What it does
HomeRespawnPlace (Home) — Where the entity stood as it registered: its spawn.
PointRespawnPlace (Point) — A point in the world.
WhereItDiedRespawnPlace (Where It Died) — Where the entity died.

Local Data kinds

Kind Menu What it does
LocalAbility (Ability) — An ability.
LocalAttribute (Attribute) — An attribute.
LocalBool (Bool) — A bool.
LocalEffect (Effect) — An effect.
LocalEntity (Entity) — An entity's identity.
LocalFloat (Float) — A number that can scale, read where it's used.
LocalInt (Int) — A whole number that can scale, read where it's used.
LocalMonoBehaviour (MonoBehaviour) — A component on a prefab, as a process to spawn.
LocalObject (Object) — Any asset, as a prefab or a material.
LocalScriptableObject (ScriptableObject) — A ScriptableObject asset.
LocalString (String) — A string.
LocalTag (Tag) — A tag.
