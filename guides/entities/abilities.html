Abilities

  • When Busy (default Queue): what the entity's abilities set to Entity's Policy do when one is asked to activate while another is busy. Activate Anyway starts it at once. Refuse starts it only when no ability under that choice is busy, and refuses it otherwise. Queue makes it wait in a queue while one is busy. Abilities explains each.
  • Targeting Yields (default on): an ability whose While Targeting is the entity's rule gives way, while it waits for its target, as another of the entity's abilities activates. Abilities explains it.
  • Starting Abilities: abilities the entity learns when its GAS initializes, each at its own Starting Level. Those set to Activate At Start then try to activate.
  • Max Abilities (default 99): the most abilities the entity can hold. Its kind can make it a scaler, which changes the limit with the entity's level (Scalers).
  • Allow Duplicates (default off): let the entity hold the same ability more than once.
  • Crowd Control: the crowd control rules its abilities follow, as a stun stops every ability and Silenced spells, or, where it names none, the project's (Abilities: Crowd Control). A runtime entity's identity names them too.
