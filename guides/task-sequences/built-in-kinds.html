Built-in kinds

The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says. A project's own kinds join them in the same pickers.

Injections

Kind Menu What it does
BreakStageRepeatInjection — Breaks out of a stage's repeat loop (for WithRepeat or RepeatUntilSkippedPolicy).
ChainInterruptInjection — Marker injection for chain-level interrupts.
DelegateSequenceInjection — An injection that does what a function of your own says: applyFunc is handed the run's runtime and returns whether it applied, as one that adds to a count on a key press or jumps to a stage by name.
InterruptSequenceInjection — Interrupts the entire sequence.
JumpToStageInjection — Jumps to a specific stage by index.
SkipAndMaintainInjection — Skips the current stage but maintains it running in background.
SkipStageInjection — Skips the current stage and moves to the next.
StopMaintainedAllInjection — Stops all maintained stages.
StopMaintainedAtIndexInjection — Stops the maintained stage at a stage index.
StopMaintainedLastInjection — Stops the oldest maintained stage.

Stage policies

Kind Menu What it does
RepeatUntilSkippedPolicy — Stage repeats infinitely until explicitly skipped, interrupted, or broken.
WhenAllStagePolicy — Stage completes when ALL tasks complete.
WhenAnyStagePolicy — Stage completes when ANY task completes.
WhenNStagePolicy — Stage completes when N tasks complete.

Clock choices

Kind Menu What it does
CasterClockChoice (Caster's) — An ability's caster's own time, its time scale times the game's; the game's for anything else.
GameClockChoice (Game's) — The game's time, whatever an ability's caster's is.
ScaledClockChoice (Scaled) — A pace of its own over another clock, as half speed or double; over the caster's unless it picks another.
UnscaledClockChoice (Unscaled) — The game's time without its time scale: it runs while the game is paused or slowed.
