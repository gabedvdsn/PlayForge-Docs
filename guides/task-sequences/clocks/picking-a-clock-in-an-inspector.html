Picking a clock in an inspector

An inspector picks a clock with an AbstractClockChoice field, marked [SerializeReference].

Choice The clock a run goes by
None The clock it would have had.
Caster's The caster's own.
Game's The game's.
Unscaled The game's time without its time scale.
Scaled A pace of its own over the clock it picks: the caster's, unless it picks another.

A [Serializable] class of your own made from it is offered beside them. Its ClockFor(data) gives the clock for a run on a packet.

These have a clock choice:

  • In the full package, an ability's Run Sequence task. It gives the sequence it runs a clock of its own, and leaves the ability's other tasks the caster's.
  • The Task Sequence Examples' components.
  • In the full package, a Sequence cue. Its run goes by its pick, else the sequence's own clock, else that of the one the cue plays on.
