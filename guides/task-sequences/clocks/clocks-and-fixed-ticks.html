Clocks and fixed ticks

Under ProcessControl's fixed tick, a sequence waits for the next tick. The ticks run on the game's time, so they stand with it.

  • A clock that runs while the game's time stands says so: its FollowsTicks is false, as the unscaled clock's is. Its sequences wait for Unity's frames instead.
  • Code outside a sequence awaits ForgeTick.Next(clock) for the next step of a clock's time.
  • A synced sequence run as a process steps with ProcessControl, by its ticks, whatever its clock.
