Components

An item's components are features it carries, each a class with hooks into the item's life. Add one with the list's +. A kind that makes no sense twice is offered only while the item holds none of it.

A component hears these moments:

Hook When it runs
Given As the item is given to an entity.
Equipped As the item is equipped.
Unequipped As the item is unequipped.
Removed As the item is removed.
Used As its holder's use of the item's Active Ability ends, having paid what it costs.

Its context's Restoring says a moment comes from a load. A load puts back what the component's earlier moments did, such as the effects it applied.

In this section

  • Auras: An Aura component holds an effect on those near its holder while the item is equipped, such as a banner that inspires the allies round its bearer.
  • Uses: A Uses component counts the uses of the item's Active Ability, such as a potion's sips or a wand's charges.
  • Set Bonus: A Set Bonus component gives its holder a bonus effect while it has enough of a set's pieces equipped.
  • Cues: A Cues component plays what the item shows and sounds on its holder.
  • Writing your own component: A component of your own derives from AbstractItemComponent and overrides the hooks it needs.
