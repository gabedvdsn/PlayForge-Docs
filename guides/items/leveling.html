Leveling

The Leveling section sets the item's level range. An item has its own level on each entity that holds it, kept under the item's asset tag. Its Granted Effects take this level, and its Active Ability is granted at it.

A level provider is an asset whose level range another asset follows.

Property Description
Link Mode Standalone (default): keeps the item's own range.
Linked To Provider: takes the range from a level provider instead.
Starting Level The start of the item's own range, 1 by default. It's kept within the range.
Max Level The end of the item's own range, 4 by default.
Level Provider Shown while linked: the asset the range comes from, an Entity, Ability, Effect, Item or Attribute Set.

Both levels are at least 1. While linked, Starting Level and Max Level show the range in use, read-only.

  • Right-click the Level Provider field to pick from one type.
  • A link that would make a loop is refused, with an error in the Console.
  • While no provider is set, a warning says the item uses its own range.

Two buttons link the item's Granted Effects and Active Ability to it, or unlink them. Each asks first, and one Undo takes it back.

  • Link Ability & Effects links the Granted Effects and the Active Ability to this item, so their per-level values follow its range.
  • Unlink Ability & Effects removes the links that point at this item.

A given item starts at its Starting Level, unless code gives it at another level. Its level then stays between that level and the Max Level. Code changes it through the entity's GAS, with SetLevel or ModifyLevel and the item's asset tag.

The link serves the inspector: the Leveling section, and the scalers of effects linked to this item, read the provider's range.

Note

At runtime, the item's level comes from its own Starting Level and Max Level, even while it is linked.
