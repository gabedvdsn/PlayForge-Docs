Leveling

An item has its own level on each entity that holds it, kept under the item's asset tag. Its Granted Effects take this level, and its Active Ability is granted at it. A level provider is an asset whose level range another asset follows.

  • Link Mode: Standalone (default) keeps the item's own range. Linked To Provider takes the range from a level provider instead.
  • Starting Level and Max Level: the item's own range, by default 1 and 4. Both are at least 1, and the Starting Level is kept within the range.
  • Level Provider, while linked: the asset the range comes from, an Entity, Ability, Effect, Item or Attribute Set. Right-click the field to pick from one type. A link that would make a loop is refused, with an error in the Console. While no provider is set, a warning says the item uses its own range. While linked, Starting Level and Max Level show the range in use, read-only.
  • Link Ability & Effects links the Granted Effects and the Active Ability to this item, so their per-level values follow its range. Unlink Ability & Effects removes the links that point at this item. Each asks first, and one Undo takes it back.

A given item starts at its Starting Level, unless code gives it at another level. Its level then stays between that level and the Max Level. Code changes it through the entity's GAS, with SetLevel or ModifyLevel and the item's asset tag.

The link serves the inspector: the Leveling section, and the scalers of effects linked to this item, read the provider's range. At runtime, the item's level comes from its own Starting Level and Max Level, even while it is linked.
