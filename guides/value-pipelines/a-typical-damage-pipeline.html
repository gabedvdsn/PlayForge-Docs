A typical damage pipeline

  1. Amplification: Multiply by the caster's Damage Amplification (a Bonus Share, read as 1 + x).
  2. Critical: Chance, the caster's Crit Chance, × 2.
  3. Armor: Multiply by a curve of the target's Armor.
  4. Resistance: Multiply by the target's Damage Resistance (a Resistance, read as 1 − x).
  5. Barrier: Absorb into the target's Barrier.

Every damage effect names it, so a blow of 100 from a caster with 50% amplification, on a target with 20% resistance, lands as 120.

The Demo's blows go through two such pipelines, Physical Damage and Magic Damage in its Effects/Pipelines: each multiplies a blow by its dealer's Attack or Magic Amplification, a Stacking Curve of it read as 1 + it, then by what its target's Armor leaves, 100 ÷ (100 + Armor), or its Magic Resistance, 1 − it, kept between 0 and 1. Its Physical and Magic Strike templates name them, and so does each blow that takes one, so a new attack takes its kind's from its template.
