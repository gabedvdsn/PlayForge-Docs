Tags

  • Granted Tags are applied to the target while the effect is active. An Instant effect grants them only for the moment it applies.
  • Granted Abilities are given to the target as the effect lands, each at the level of what applied it, and taken back as it comes off. One that's running then is interrupted. An ability the target can't take, such as one it knows already when duplicates aren't allowed, isn't lent, so the target keeps its own as the effect comes off. An Instant effect lends none, as it's never on the target.
  • A change of the target's sides while the effect lasts is a Side Change component (Side Change).
  • Context Tags describe the effect, such as Fire. Each change the effect makes carries these and its origin's context tags, and workers can filter by them. They are not applied to the entity.
  • Removes Effects Tagged makes the effect a cleanse. As it lands, every other effect on the target tagged with one of these, or a child of one, by its asset tag, a context tag or a tag it grants, comes off, at the next tick, as a removed effect does. Status.Debuff takes off Status.Debuff.Poison and Status.Debuff.Burn, and State.Burning takes off whatever grants it.
  • Purges makes the effect a purge, by the project's purge levels (Purging): as it lands, the effects on the target its strength reaches, of the kinds it names and applied by whom it says, come off.
  • Blocks Effects Tagged makes the effect an immunity. While it's on the target and its ongoing requirements hold, an effect tagged with one of these, or a child of one, the same way, can't land, and applying it returns false. It leaves the effects already on the target; add the same tags to Removes Effects Tagged to clear them as it lands.
