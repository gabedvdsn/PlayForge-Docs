Purging

A project's Purge Levels, under Forge Project in Project Settings → PlayForge, rank its purges. Each Tier is a tag, weakest first, as Purge.Basic, Purge.Strong; an effect is at the first tier whose tag it carries, as its asset tag or a context tag, or a child of one. A purge of a tier takes the effects at that tier or under it, as a Strong dispel takes the Basic too, and an effect at no tier is never purged, but by death.

A purge, an effect's Purges or ForgePurge.Purge(entity, purge) in code, has a Strength, a tier's tag; its Kinds, tags an effect must carry one of to be taken, as Status.Debuff, or any where it names none; Applied By, whom it takes the effects of, by who applied each against the one purged: itself, its allies (its buffs) or its enemies (its debuffs), an applier sharing none of its sides an enemy; and Filters of a project's own, each an AbstractPurgeFilter whose Takes says yes or no, as Except Tagged spares the effects carrying the tags it names. An effect a purge takes comes off at the next tick, as a removed effect does, and a purge never takes the effect doing it. ForgePurge.Purge(entity, strength) purges by a strength alone, of every kind.

A staying entity's death purges its effects too, all but those it keeps: each tier's Kept On Death keeps the effects at it, and the table's Kept On Death categories keep theirs whatever their tiers, an ability's cooldowns, lent effects and toggles as a project starts (Entities: Death).
