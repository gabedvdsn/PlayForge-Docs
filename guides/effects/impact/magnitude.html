Magnitude

The Magnitude is the amount the impact applies.

It is a Constant number, or, by its kind, a scaler's value at the origin's level, such as a By Level table of values per level. Combine joins the scaler and the number (Scalers).

  • A Continuous modifier works its magnitude out again whenever the attribute is recalculated.
  • A commit works it out once.

Set By Caller

A Set By Caller scaler reads a magnitude that your code sets, under a tag, as it applies the effect. You set it in one of two places:

  • on the spec, with SetByCaller(tag, magnitude);
  • on an ability activation's data, which each effect the ability applies takes as it's made, its cost, cooldown and contained effects among them.

Only the tag itself matches. Where none is set, the scaler reads its Default.

Other kinds

A magnitude can also read what the effect has dealt, a part of another effect, ability or item on either side, or what a worker answers. SCALERS.md lists each kind.
