Levels

What the effect does, level by level. Its line, which the effect's header and The Forge's Summary column show, is its first impact by level, an add signed, as "-10/-20/-30 Health" or "+20 Armor", at its provider's levels where it's linked to one and at levels 1 to 5 where it isn't. ForgeAssetLines.Of(asset) gives any asset's line in the editor.

The whole reading is code's, for a tooltip or a game's own screen: AbilityInspector.BuildEffectSeries(effect, 1, 5, caster, target) reads the effect at levels 1 to 5 against a caster and a target, either of which may be null. Each impact gives its Magnitude and Total at each level, its Line, as its field's summary reads it, its Parts at each level, and what it Needs; the effect gives its Duration, Ticks, Interval, Chance and Contained effects, and Describe(level) says what it does at a level, an impact to a line: "-15 Health each tick, 12 ticks over 12 s".

NumberTrace.Of(number, context) explains any one number the same way, each part with what it read and what it gave:

Health as it lands, level 2:
-1 × (Caster's Attack Damage + 80 if Caster's Combo Charges > 0, else 0) = -100
  Group = 100
    Add: Caster's Attack Damage = 100
    Add: Conditional = 0
      When: Caster's Combo Charges > 0: doesn't hold
      False: 0 = 0

A read given no caster or no target gives, for a part that reads one, what its field falls back to, and says what it needs, as "the target". A kind or a condition of your own adds its parts by overriding Explain: trace.Add(Min, context, this, "Min").
