Requirements

Chance to Apply comes first: how likely the effect is to land, from 0 to 1, which can scale by level. It gates the whole application, as the Applying rows do. Each application draws once from PlayForge's random source (ForgeRandom), and a miss is refused, so applying returns false, as an immunity's refusal does, and the target's callbacks hear it, OnEffectRefused, with why (Entities: Effect Journal). 1, the default, always lands, and 0 never does; neither draws, so an effect without a chance leaves the numbers others draw as they were. A contained effect draws its own: a strike with a bleed at 0.3 lands every time, and its bleed about one time in three. The section's summary shows a chance short of 1.

Chance Roll, under it, says how the chance rolls, as every chance PlayForge rolls can, and shows for a chance that can miss:

  • Pseudo Random: each miss raises the next roll's odds by a constant and a hit starts them again, so streaks even out and the chance over many rolls stays the same, as Dota's pseudo-random distribution: a 25% chance's first roll has about 8.5%, its next about 17%, and so on. Each caster keeps its own streak for each chance.
  • Luck: the attribute playing the project's Luck role (Attributes: Roles), read on the caster, or with Own Luck a number of the chance's own, its Luck, shown under Own Luck alone: above 0 it rolls that many more times and keeps the best, below 0 the worst.

A chance of 1 or more always comes up and one of 0 or less never does, and neither draws. The same roll is on a trigger's Chance, the Chance stage of a Value Pipeline, an ability's Chance to Activate, and the Chance condition, which holds on a roll of its number, as a critical strike chance read from an attribute: in a hit it rolls once and keeps the roll, so a hit read twice, its preview and its commit, reads the same.

Under it are tag checks against the effect's target, and against its source, the entity behind its origin. The switch shows one side's table at a time: On the target first, then On the source. Each table has a row for each group:

  • Applying: must pass for the effect to be applied. The effect is also refused if its Removed By row is already met.
  • While on: while it fails, the effect stays, but its ticks don't execute. Its Continuous modifiers stay in place.
  • Removed by: when it's met, the effect is removed.

Each row has two cells. Requires lists the tags the entity must have, and Must not have the tags it must not have, or says None. A row passes when the entity has every tag it requires and none it must not have. Click a cell to edit its list under the table, with the row's Template, a shared Requirement Template, and Sync With Template, which keeps the row's tags the same as its template's. A template's Category is a tag of its own context, Requirement Category, which groups templates. Each entry is a tag query: a Tag, a Match mode and a comparison with a number, where Greater Than 0 means "the tag is present".

Empty Applying and While On rows always pass. An empty Removed By row never removes anything. While On and Removed By are checked whenever an effect is applied to or removed from the target. While On is also checked before each tick.

Under the tables are its Conditions for each phase, each a condition read in the effect's context, its caster's and its target's numbers in reach, as "the target below 30% Health". Apply Conditions must each hold for it to land; Ongoing Conditions pause it while one fails, as While On does; and Removal Conditions take it off once each holds, with its Removed By rows where it names any, as Attribute Shift stops at its floor. A phase passes only when its tags pass and its conditions all hold. The Ongoing and Removal conditions are checked at the entity's end of frame while the effect lasts, as no tag needs to change for an attribute they read to, and before each tick as well.
