Resolve Order

The project's Resolve Order, under Forge Project in Project Settings → PlayForge, decides the order an area's effects land on those it takes. With it, specific behaviour can be coded, as a totem whose aura keeps its allies alive resolving after them.

Property Description
Tag One carrying the tag, or one under it, resolves at the entry's Order.
Order Lower resolves first. One carrying none of the entries' tags is at 0.
  • One carrying several tags takes the first entry it matches, so the list's order is its precedence.
  • An entity carries a tag on its identity, as a totem always does, or while something grants it, as an aura can grant its holder one while it holds.
  • The area's own order breaks ties.

These land in the resolve order:

  • Apply Effects and Apply/Remove Effects land on an ability's targets in it, though the targeting keeps its own order and its primary target.
  • An effect's Area component lands on its target and those it takes in it, its target among them.
  • An aura puts its effect on those that came near in it.

The tasks' and the Area's Use Resolve Order, on by default, turns it off for that one. Code reads it through ForgeResolveOrder: OrderOf(target), Sort(targets), and TargetsOf(data) for an activation's targets in order.

A death waits for its entity's end of frame, and an aura comes off a dead holder at its next check or as the holder ends. So one area's hits resolve together: a blow that kills the totem doesn't take its aura off the hero the same explosion strikes next.

The order matters for what changes as each lands, as an effect that lands on the caster with each hit, or a transfer.
