Cue kinds

A cue's kind decides what it plays. Each list holds its cues as the Workers list holds workers, each cue's kind picked from the list.

Kind What it plays
Camera Shake Shakes the camera through the shaker the game gives ForgeCues.Shaker, an ICameraShaker. With none given, it shakes nothing.
Sound Plays its clip where its Placement says, at the target by default (Cues: Placement).
Prefab Places its prefab where its Placement says, at the target by default, for Lifetime seconds.
Floating Text Floats a text where its Placement says, as a hit's damage or a status's name (Cues: Floating text).
Process Runs a MonoBehaviour process where its Placement says, on a data packet loaded for the cue, as a shield's while it lasts (Cues: Data packets).
Sequence Runs a task sequence on a data packet loaded for the cue, as a flicker while the effect lasts (Cues: Handlers).
Cue Tag Plays what its tag routes to (Cues: Routes).

A Camera Shake's Strength and Duration are number fields read for its effect as it plays, as are Prefab's Lifetime and Sound's Volume.

A Prefab cue has more to it:

  • In While Active, it stays for as long as the effect lasts, following the target.
  • A cue raised at a point, as a Play Cue task's, places it there.
  • With Pooled, it takes its objects from a pool, ForgeCuePool, and gives them back.
  • The pool makes Preallocate of them ahead as it first plays.
  • An IPooledCue on the prefab resets it as it's taken and given back.

Write a cue of your own by deriving from AbstractCueHandler and overriding the moments it plays at. Or override Burst to play every burst one way, reading the moment that raised it from cue.Moment.
