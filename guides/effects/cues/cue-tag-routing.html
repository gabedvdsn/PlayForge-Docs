Cue Tag routing

A Cue Tag cue plays what its tag routes to, in place of naming what it shows. So an effect, an ability and an entity can each raise Cue.Hit.Fire, and one place says how it looks.

  • The project's Cue Library, an asset its settings name, plays the route for the tag, or else for its nearest parent.
  • A route that doesn't Override plays its parent's after it.
  • An entity's Cue Receiver component plays a tag its own way first, in place of the library's, or before it with Pass Through.
  • The receiver's Variant plays the library's route under its name, as Cue.Hit.Golem for Cue.Hit, where the library has one.
