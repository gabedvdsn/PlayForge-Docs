Ticks

A tick makes a lasting effect's change again, as a new commit.

  • Ticks (Durational) spaces its ticks evenly over the duration. The last falls as the duration ends, so 5 ticks over 10 seconds tick every 2 seconds.
  • Tick Every (Infinite) sets the seconds between ticks.
  • Tick On Application runs one tick as soon as the effect is applied, before the first periodic tick. It runs again when re-application refreshes or extends the effect.
  • Use Tick On Application with Periodic Ticks. On its own, it is a shape that the Problems view flags.

Additional Executions applies while the effect ticks. Each tick executes the change a number of times, N plus this, combined with its scaler.

N comes from the effect's container and is normally 1. A stacking container that shares one duration, or takes it from one stack, uses its stack count.

A tick only executes while the While On requirements hold (see Requirements).
