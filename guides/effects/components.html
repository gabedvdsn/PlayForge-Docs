Components

An effect's components are features it carries, each a class with hooks into its life, added with the list's +. A component hears the effect Land on the entity it's on, Tick each period, Hit as an impact it deals lands, and End as it comes off: an instant effect at once, a lasting one as it's removed, at the moments its workers do; and Death as the one it's on dies while it's on them, whether the entity stays or ends, before what the death ends comes off. A lasting effect's components hear it Lend what it gives while it lasts, as it starts to last: as it lands, before its Land, as it takes another's place under its tag, and as a load or a move puts it back, where it doesn't land again; and TakeBack, as it stops: as it comes off, before its End, as another takes its place, and as a load clears it or a move takes it away. An Aura lends its aura so. A kind that makes no sense twice is offered only while the effect holds none of it.

It hears Reapply as an application it takes in lands, as a stack or a refresh; Refused as an application of it is refused, with why, on the one it was aimed at; and StacksChanged as its stacks change while it lasts, with the counts before and after. It hears a tick only as one executes: one its ongoing condition stops isn't heard.

A component of your own derives from AbstractEffectComponent and overrides the hooks it needs, each a no-op otherwise. It's the effect's, shared by everything that holds it, so what it keeps for one application of it it keeps through its context: ctx.GetState<T>(this). Describe says what it does in one line with its numbers, as an Area's "Lands on enemies within 300", which the Components section's line and The Forge's summary say beside its name.

Auras

An Aura component holds an effect on those near the one it's on, while it lasts. Items, abilities and entities have one too, each its own kind. Each has the Effect it holds, a Radius from its holder, read at each check, Flat, whom it Takes by how each stands to its holder, its Allies, itself among them, to start, and an Interval, 0.25 s by default, at which it checks who's near, by its holder's own time. It puts its effect on each that comes near, from an origin of its own, and takes it off as they leave, die or end, as its holder dies or ends, or as the aura goes; an effect of its that ends on its own, a durational one, goes on again at its next check. An Infinite effect holds as long as they're near. Two of an item hold two, as their effect's stacking says. A purge counts an aura's effect Lent. Each Aura component gives its aura in its own hooks: an effect's as it lends, an ability's as it's granted, an item's as it's equipped, an entity's as it registers. An entity's auras are its AuraSet, gas.GetService<AuraSet>(), a service it steps (Entities: Services), and ForgeAuras.Provide(entry, holder) and Remove give one of your own.

Area

An Area component lands the effect on those near its target as it lands, as Storm Hammer stuns everyone near the one it hits: each within its Radius of the target, read as it lands, along the ground unless Flat is off; of those it Takes by how each stands to its caster: its Enemies, its Allies, itself among them, its Other Allies, Self, All But Self or Everyone (a caster with no sides takes everyone as its allies and enemies); and at most Max Targets besides the target, the nearest first, or every one at 0. Each is found by its position, with no collider. Each takes the effect from the same origin, so its modifiers and its chance to apply are its own, and an effect that lands by an area spreads no further. A Radius of 0 is no area. It lands on its target and those it takes in the project's resolve order, its target among them, unless its Use Resolve Order is off (see Resolve Order). An effect holds one at most; without one, it lands on its target alone.

Transfers

A Transfer component moves an amount from one attribute to another, as Essence Shift steals Agility or Attribute Shift moves Agility into Strength. From, the effect's caster or its target, gives its From Attribute, and To gets its To Attribute, each side its own, so an entity can give to itself. Slot says which values move: Current, as a pool's Mana; Base; or Current And Base, as a stat's Strength. Amount can scale by level. Only what the giver has moves, so one with 3 of a steal of 5 gives 3, and nothing is made. Delivery says when: On Apply moves once as the effect lands and Periodic each tick, both for good; Continuous moves as the effect lands and holds while it lasts, a modifier on each side, read again only where a stack or a level changes its amount, and gives back as the effect ends, as a load or a move takes it, or as the entity it's on goes. Each side's change is exact: no worker takes it for a hit, no barrier soaks it and nothing scales it, so the getter gets what the giver gave, as far as its own bounds let it; what its maximum can't hold is lost, as an overheal is. The Problems view warns of a transfer whose Delivery the effect's duration can't make, as it does an additional impact's, and of one that names no attribute to give or get, each numbered among the effect's transfers. An effect holds as many as it moves, each its own.

Side Change

A Side Change component changes the target's sides while the effect lasts: to its caster's, as they are as it lands (Source Caster, a mind control), or to the Sides it names (Named, a pacify's Neutral), which show under Named alone. Of several on one entity, the highest Priority holds, read as it lands, a tie going to the latest, and as the last ends its own sides come back. Its tags follow, so whom effects and areas take changes with it. Interrupts breaks what the target runs as the change lands or ends, where it changes the sides, not where a change of higher priority keeps them. An Instant effect doesn't last, so it changes nothing. An effect holds one at most; it was a field of the effect's own, Side Change in its Tags section, with a switch.

Spread

A Spread component spreads its effect, or the Effect it names, from the one it's on to those near them, at the moments its When names, any of them: as the effect Lands, each Tick, as it comes off (End), however it ends, or as the one it's on dies (Death), as a plague jumps from its victim to the enemies near it. It takes each within its Radius of the one it's on, read at the effect's level, along the ground unless Flat is off; of those it Takes by how each stands to its caster, its Enemies to start, as an Area's; at most Max Targets each time, the nearest first, or every one at 0; and passes over those who have the effect it spreads already, unless Skip Holders is off. Each takes the effect from the same origin, its caster's, so its modifiers and its chance to land are its own, in the project's resolve order unless Use Resolve Order is off. A landing it made spreads on only so far as its Hops allow: at 0, it spreads only from where the effect landed of its own; at 1, once more from each it spread to; and on. An effect holds as many as it has moments to spread at, each its own.
