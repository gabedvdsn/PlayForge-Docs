What an effect carries besides its impacts: components with lifecycle hooks, cues for presentation, modifiers that change other effects, and purges.

Add a component with the Components list's +. A kind that makes no sense twice is offered only while the effect holds none of it.

Component What it does
Aura Holds an effect on those near the one it's on, while it lasts.
Area Lands the effect on those near its target as it lands.
Transfer Moves an amount from one attribute to another.
Side Change Changes the target's sides while the effect lasts.
Spread Spreads the effect from the one it's on to those near them.
Cues Shows and sounds the effect's moments.

In this section

  • Auras: An Aura component holds an effect on those near the one it's on, while it lasts.
  • Area: An Area component lands the effect on those near its target as it lands, as Storm Hammer stuns everyone near the one it hits.
  • Transfers: A Transfer component moves an amount from one attribute to another, as Essence Shift steals Agility or Attribute Shift moves Agility into Strength.
  • Side Change: A Side Change component changes the target's sides while the effect lasts.
  • Spread: A Spread component spreads its effect, or the Effect it names, from the one it's on to those near them, as a plague jumps from its victim to the enemies near it.
  • Component hooks: A component hears its effect's moments through hooks, at the moments its workers do.
  • Custom components: A component of your own derives from AbstractEffectComponent and overrides the hooks it needs.
