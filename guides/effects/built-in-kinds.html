Built-in kinds

The kinds PlayForge ships for this guide's pickers, each with its menu and what it does, as its picker says. A project's own kinds join them in the same pickers.

Components

Kind Menu What it does
AreaEffectComponent — Lands the effect on others near its target too.
AuraEffectComponent — Gives those near the target an aura's effects while the effect lasts.
CuesEffectComponent — Shows and sounds the effect's moments: as it lands, ticks, stacks or comes off, while it lasts, and as it's refused.
SideChangeEffectComponent — Changes the target's sides while the effect lasts, as a mind control.
SpreadEffectComponent — Spreads the effect, or another, from its target to those near it.
TransferEffectComponent — Moves an amount of an attribute from the caster or the target to either.

Changes

Kind Menu What it does
AddContainedEffectChange — Adds a contained effect, landing as it lands, each tick, or as it ends.
AddOverflowChange — Adds an overflow effect, landing as an application would pass its most stacks.
ChanceChange — Changes its chance to apply.
ChangeImpactChange — Changes the numbers of its impacts by a Value Pipeline stage.
DurationChange — Changes how long it lasts.
GrantTagsChange — Adds tags it grants while it's on.
StacksChange — Changes its most stacks, and how many a landing gives.

Stacking

Kind Menu What it does
InitializationOperation — The stacks a new effect starts with.
StackAmountOperation — How many stacks an application adds.
StackBoundsConfig — The fewest and the most stacks the effect holds, and what happens at the most.

Purge filters

Kind Menu What it does
ExceptTaggedPurgeFilter — Spares the effects carrying one of its tags.
