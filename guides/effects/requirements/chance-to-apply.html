Chance to Apply

Chance to Apply comes first: how likely the effect is to land, from 0 to 1, which can scale by level. It gates the whole application, as the Applying rows do.

  • Each application draws once from PlayForge's random source (ForgeRandom).
  • A miss is refused, so applying returns false, as an immunity's refusal does.
  • The target's callbacks hear a miss, OnEffectRefused, with why (Entities: Effect Journal).
  • 1, the default, always lands, and 0 never does. Neither draws, so an effect without a chance leaves the numbers others draw as they were.
  • A contained effect draws its own: a strike with a bleed at 0.3 lands every time, and its bleed about one time in three.
  • The section's summary shows a chance short of 1.

Chance Roll

Chance Roll, under it, says how the chance rolls, as every chance PlayForge rolls can. It shows for a chance that can miss.

Property Description
Pseudo Random Each miss raises the next roll's odds by a constant, and a hit starts them again. So streaks even out, and the chance over many rolls stays the same.
Own Luck Uses a number of the chance's own, its Luck, in place of the luck attribute.
Luck Shown under Own Luck alone. The chance's own luck.
  • Pseudo Random works as Dota's pseudo-random distribution: a 25% chance's first roll has about 8.5%, its next about 17%, and so on.
  • With Pseudo Random, each caster keeps its own streak for each chance.
  • Without Own Luck, the luck is the attribute playing the project's Luck role (Attributes: Roles), read on the caster.
  • Above 0, luck rolls that many more times and keeps the best. Below 0, it keeps the worst.

A chance of 1 or more always comes up, and one of 0 or less never does. Neither draws.

The same roll is on a trigger's Chance, the Chance stage of a Value Pipeline, an ability's Chance to Activate, and the Chance condition.

The Chance condition holds on a roll of its number, as a critical strike chance read from an attribute. In a hit it rolls once and keeps the roll, so a hit read twice, its preview and its commit, reads the same.
