Identity

  • Name: the attribute's display name. Give each attribute its own Name: some lookups compare attributes by Name, such as an attribute worker matching the attribute it watches.
  • Abbreviation: a short form, such as HP. The collapsed section shows it.
  • Description: free text for your UI.
  • Textures: icons, each paired with a tag. The texture tagged Primary is the attribute's icon. Without one, the first texture is used.
  • Role: what the attribute is, which says how bonuses to it add up and how a number reads it:
    • Value, every attribute's unless it says: bonuses add and multiply as each effect says, and a number reads it as it is.
    • Bonus Share, as Damage Amplification at 0.2 for 20%: bonuses add, +20% and +30% making 0.5, and × Attribute reads it as a multiplier, 1 + x, so an attack of 100 that reads it deals 150.
    • Multiplier, as Damage Dealt at 1: as a Value, read as it is.
    • Resistance, a share taken off or a chance, as Damage Resistance: bonuses stack with diminishing returns, each on what's left, so two of 30% make 51% and none takes it past 1; × Attribute reads what's left, 1 − x. The lasting bonuses diminish; a permanent change, which an instant or periodic impact commits, adds as it is.
    • Barrier, as a shield before Health: it absorbs for the attribute its Absorbs For names, each workable loss to that one coming off it first, as much as it holds, and the rest going on; two hits in a frame soak no more than it holds, and what it soaks comes off it at the frame's end. Decay Per Second, a number field read at its holder's level, takes it down by its holder's own time, a number a second or, with Decay As Share Of Base, that share of its base, as Topaz Brooch's barrier fades; Fades With Effect fades what a lasting effect gives it to 0 by the time that effect ends. A hit the barrier soaks whole is no loss to the other, so an On Hit counting it doesn't go off.

Gains Scaled By names an attribute that multiplies each workable gain to this one's current, on the same entity: Health's by Healing Received, so a curse at 0.5 halves heals and Ice Blast's 0 blocks them. A loss passes it by, and a gain to the base, as more maximum, does too.

An effect's impact can add its own way (Add Behaviour: as the attribute's role says, a plain Sum, or Diminishing), and a number can read its own way (× Attribute's Read As).

An attribute that plays one of its project's roles says so, as "Plays Health in Arena".
