Templates

An asset can take another asset of its type as its template, as a prefab instance takes its prefab. The asset is the template's child: it takes the template's fields when the template is pushed, and keeps the fields it overrides.

This section covers setting a template, pushing it, overrides and what a push leaves. It's apart from the project's templates that Create copies (New assets and presets).

In this section

  • Setting a template: You set an asset's template from the asset menu in its inspector's header.
  • Pushing: A push gives a template's children its fields.
  • Overrides: An override is a field a child holds as its own, which a push leaves as it is.
  • In code: Three editor types handle templates in code, and BaseForgeAsset holds an asset's side.
