The Debugger

The Debugger (RuntimeDebugger, Window → PlayForge → Debugger) shows the game as it plays, and changes its entities as it shows them. This section covers its four views, Entities, Processes, Tags and Timeline, and the Profiler's PlayForge modules.

The Debugger's Entities view in play mode, an entity selected.

Outside Play mode, the Debugger says so, with Enter Play Mode.

In Play mode, it follows ProcessControl: its lists change as processes register and leave. Their values change in place, as often as Samples a Second, in its window menu, says: 2, 4, 10 or 30 times a second, 4 to start.

While the Debugger reads them, ProcessControl times each process's steps.

In this section

  • The toolbar and status bar: The Debugger's toolbar and status bar frame each of its views.
  • Entities: Entities lists the hubs ProcessControl runs, GameplayAbilitySystem and RuntimeGameplayAbilitySystem alike, and those of each world code makes and steps itself (ProcessWorld.Owned), as the Creator Lab's units.
  • Processes: Processes lists every process ProcessControl holds.
  • Tags: Tags shows every tag the listed entities hold, as a tree of their paths.
  • Timeline: Timeline shows what the entities' abilities and effects did over the last seconds.
  • Profiler modules: Two modules chart PlayForge's counters in Unity's Profiler.
