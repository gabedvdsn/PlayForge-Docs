The Debugger

The Debugger (RuntimeDebugger, Window → PlayForge → Debugger) shows the game as it plays, and changes its entities as it shows them. This section covers its four views, Entities, Processes, Tags and Timeline, and the Profiler's PlayForge modules.

Outside Play mode it says so, with Enter Play Mode. In Play mode it follows ProcessControl: its lists change as processes register and leave, and their values change in place, as often as Samples a Second, in its window menu, says: 2, 4, 10 or 30 times a second, 4 to start. While it reads them, ProcessControl times each process's steps.

The toolbar switches between the views and holds the search and each view's own tools. The status bar holds ProcessControl's state, the processes and how many run, wait or are being created, the entities by hub, and on its right what the window's last action did, as "Applied Burning" or "Burning was refused". In a narrow window, as a docked column, each view's list sits above its detail and the frame count moves to the status bar. Each detail's sections keep their fold for the session.

Entities

Entities lists the hubs ProcessControl runs, GameplayAbilitySystem and RuntimeGameplayAbilitySystem alike, by Name, Hub, Mono or Runtime, level (Lvl) and Effects, the number on it. A MonoBehaviour hub goes by its GameObject's name, which tells two of one asset apart. The toolbar's hub filter shows Both hubs, Mono hubs or Runtime hubs. With Follow Selection on, selecting an entity's GameObject, or a child of it, in the scene shows that entity. A double click selects a MonoBehaviour hub's GameObject.

The detail's header names the entity, as "Entity · Goblin Raider · Mono hub · Level 3", with Select in Scene and Open Asset, which opens its asset in a window of its own. Its sections each have a table, a row a click selects, and a foot row that acts on the row selected and adds to the table:

  • Attributes: each attribute's Base and Current, the current's tooltip breaking it down by retention group, and what Changed by it: each source of what it took in the last second, as "Burning -8/s", then each effect that holds a modifier on it. The foot row's Base and Current set the selected attribute's values on Enter, as a change the entity makes on its own account, an unworkable one (Workers: Workable and unworkable); a double click on a value goes to its field.
  • Effects: each effect on the entity, the time it has Left, its Stacks, and where it's From, as "Player · Fireball". Remove takes the selected one off, where its source applied it; an effect picked in the foot row's field, with Apply, lands from the entity itself.
  • Abilities: each ability's level (Lvl), its State, as Stage 2/3, Active, Cooldown or Ready, and the Cooldown it has left. Activate sets the selected one off as the entity would, and Interrupt stops its run; an ability picked in the foot row's field, with Level and Grant, is granted at that level.
  • Tags: each tag the entity holds, its Count, by weight, and what it's From. Remove takes back the selected tag where it's a loose one; a tag typed in the foot row's field, or picked from the project's with its button, is granted as a loose tag with Add Tag, or Enter.
  • Combat Log: the entity's combat log, newest first, as its project writes it. A click on an entry shows its hit's run through its pipeline and its steps (Entities: Combat Log).

Processes

Processes lists every process ProcessControl holds, each column sorting: its ID, its name (Process), its Handler, its Lifecycle, the update loops it steps in (Timing), its State, with the state it moves to next where something has asked, as "Running → Terminated", its Lifetime, and its average time a Step over the last second. The toolbar's filter shows All states, Running or Waiting, and its ProcessControl menu sets ProcessControl's own state, from Ready to Terminated Immediately.

The detail names the process, its ID and whether it's a Mono or a Runtime process, with Select in Scene for a Mono one. Pause, Resume, Terminate, as its lifecycle allows, and Terminate Now act on it and the processes it handles. Its sections say how it runs: Process, its lifecycle, step timing, step priority and handler, with Show, which selects the handler's process in the list; State, its state, the state it's queued to move to, and its Run: going, held by a pause, not started, cancelled, so Resume starts a new one, or ended; and Time, its lifetime, its time running, its step time and its peak step (Processes).

Tags

Tags shows every tag the listed entities hold, as a tree of their paths, each with how many Entities hold it and their Weight, a parent counting its children's. The hub filter applies. A tag's detail lists who holds it under Held By: each Entity, its Weight, what grants it, Granted by, and that one's Source: its attribute set, an effect and who applied it, an ability's asset tag or its passive or active tags, an item, the entity's own asset tag, affiliation or granted tags, or a loose tag, granted directly.

Timeline

Timeline shows what the entities' abilities and effects did over the last seconds. While the Debugger is open in Play mode, it records each ability's runs, stage by stage, each refused activation, each effect's time on the shelf, each refused effect, and an effect journal of the entities it lists, whatever view shows; it starts afresh each time Play mode does. The toolbar's Record stops and starts it, Clear forgets what it recorded, and Last 10 s picks how far back the strips reach: 5, 10, 30 or 60 seconds.

Its list has a track for each entity's ability or effect, the newest first, its number of Runs, and a strip of its spans: a bar for a run or an effect's time on the shelf, and a tick for an instant effect or a refusal. The search finds a track by its entity or its asset.

  • An ability's track shows its Stages, each with its policy and its tasks, and when the selected run reached it and how long it stayed, or that it didn't; then its Runs, the latest first, each with its Start, its Length, the Stages it reached, and its Outcome: Running, Ended, Stopped, or the refusal and why. A click on a run shows its stages.
  • An effect's track shows its Applications, each with its Start, End and Length, its refusals among them, then its Journal: each landing, tick, hit and removal of the effect on the entity, and each worker that ran, newest first. A click on an entry shows its parts: a number's trace, a hit's breakdown, or what a worker queued and what came of it (Entities: Effect Journal).

A track keeps its last 100 spans, and the journal its last 1,000 entries.

Profiler modules

Two modules chart PlayForge's counters in Unity's Profiler; turn them on in its list of modules.

  • PlayForge Processes (PlayForgeProcessesProfilerModule) charts Processes Running, Processes Waiting, Processes Created, Registrations, Terminations, Steps, State Changes, Step Time, Sequences Running and Stages Started. Its details list the selected frame's costliest processes, each by ID, type and step time, which PlayForge writes into the capture as the Profiler records, so a saved capture shows them too.
  • PlayForge GAS (PlayForgeGASProfilerModule) charts Mono Entities, Runtime Entities, Effects Active, Effects Applied, Effects Removed, Impacts, Activations, Refused Activations, Tag Compiles and Recomputes.
