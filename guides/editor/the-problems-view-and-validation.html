The Problems view and validation

PlayForge's validator checks every PlayForge asset in the Unity project as it changes, and keeps what it finds. The Problems view lists it.

This section covers the view, fixing problems, the severities, PlayForge's rules, the validator, and adding a rule of your own.

In this section

  • The Problems view: The Problems view (ForgeProblemsWindow) lists what the validator finds.
  • Fixing problems: A problem that one step fixes has a quick fix in the Problems view, which you apply with one click.
  • Severities: A problem's severity is a ForgeProblemSeverity, which says how much the problem matters.
  • PlayForge's rules: Each of PlayForge's rules finds what an inspector warns about in place, in the same words.
  • The validator: ForgeValidator runs every ForgeValidationRule on each asset it checks.
  • A rule of your own: A rule of your own adds a check to the validator.
