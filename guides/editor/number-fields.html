Number fields

Every number a PlayForge asset authors is a number field, a ScalableFloat or a ScalableInt, which ScalableFloatDrawer and ScalableIntDrawer draw. This section covers the field in the editor, its menu and the windows it opens, and the Try sections; Scalers: A magnitude field covers the kinds and what each reads.

The field

A number field shows its name, its kind, Constant or another, its number or the range it gives, as "6–8 s", and its field menu (⋮).

  • The kind menu files the kinds by category, and offers only those the field's context can work out, as its code declares with [NumberContext]. Fields of several selected assets whose kinds differ show the kind as mixed, and choosing one gives it to each.
  • A kind other than Constant shows a line of what the field reads, as "0.3 × Target's Health (base)", then Combine, how the field joins its number with the kind, a whole-number field's Rounding, and the kind's own settings, on a shaded block under the field.
  • A field that reads its scaler and has none says it reads 0, with Make It Constant. A kind the field's context can't work out says so, and the field reads its number.
  • A field whose unit is "%" shows and takes its number as a percentage. One with limits keeps its constant within them, on a slider where they're a range.

The field menu holds Save as Scaler Asset…, Scaler Assets, a submenu of the active project's Scaler assets to share, the one shared checked, Import Values…, Quick Fill… and Make Constant. Each change is one Undo step, and reaches each selected asset's own field. A scaler field of its own, as a Group member's scaler, has the same menu.

Its windows

  • Save as Scaler Asset (SaveScalerAssetWindow): a Name, starting as the asset's and the field's, as "Burning Duration", and a Folder, the Scaler type's in the active project, which Choose… changes. Save and Use saves the field's scaler as a Scaler asset and makes the field share it. It waits for one selected asset.
  • Import Values (ScalerValuesWindow): the active project's Scaler assets, then the fields of its other assets with values per level. Pick one to compare its values with the field's, level by level, with both curves, then Copy Values, as one Undo step.
  • Quick Fill (QuickFillWizard): fills the field's values, or the points of one spread over levels, from a pattern, a QuickFillWizard.EFillPattern: Same Value, Straight Line, Slow Then Fast, Fast Then Slow, Steps, Add Each Level, Multiply Each Level, Ease In and Out or Custom Curve. Its rounding, a QuickFillWizard.ERounding, is None, Whole Numbers or Nearest Step. A preview shows the values as the table will, with their curve, and Apply writes them as one Undo step.

ScalerActions does in code what the kind menu and the field menu do, each one Undo step that reaches every selected asset: SetKind, MakeConstant, CanMakeConstant, UseScalerAsset, SaveAsScalerAsset and CopyValues.

Try

A Scaler asset's inspector and a Value Pipeline's have a Try section, which reads the asset at each level of a range, 1 to 5 to start, against a stand-in caster and target and the values a caller sets, each typed or filled from an Entity. What's typed is kept for the session, never in the asset (Scalers: Scaler assets, Value Pipelines: Trying a pipeline).

ScalerTry does the same in code. Its From and To are the levels; its Caster and Target are each a ScalerTry.Side, holding a ScalerTry.AttributeRow for each attribute a read asks of it, given with Set, taken with Lack, or filled from an entity with FillFrom; and its Caller holds a ScalerTry.CallerRow for each key, given with SetCaller and taken with UnsetCaller. Read(scaler) gives a ScalerTry.Reading: the value at each level, and the parts behind each. ValuePipelineTry puts a Value through a pipeline, its chances coming up or missing as ChancesComeUp says, against its Numbers, a ScalerTry; Read(pipeline) gives a ValuePipelineTry.Reading, a ValuePipelineTry.Run for each level, with each stage's step, what came out, and the stop that stopped the hit, where one did.

The drawers

Drawer Draws
ScalableFloatDrawer, ScalableIntDrawer Every ScalableFloat and ScalableInt: the number field
ScalerDrawer A scaler field on its own, as an effect's Delta Time Scaler or a Group member's scaler: its kind, the range it gives and its settings, with a number field's menu
ScalerBehaviourDrawer A scaler's behaviour (AbstractScalerBehaviour); the list's − removes one
VarianceDrawer A Randomized scaler's variance: Percentage, Flat, Multiplier, or one of your own
ConditionDrawer A condition (GameplayCondition), as a Conditional's When: its kind, a project's own among them, its line, then its fields
LevelThresholdDrawer A Threshold scaler's threshold: its level and its tier's name on one row, then what the scaler reads from there
MagnitudeModifierGroupMemberDrawer, CachedMagnitudeModifierGroupMemberDrawer A Group's member: how its result joins the members' before it, then its scaler
NumberSiblingFieldDrawer The field a Relative to a Field number reads, picked from the other number fields of its object; a field that reads it back stays out, so a loop can't be picked
