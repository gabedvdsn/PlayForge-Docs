Number fields

Every number a PlayForge asset authors is a number field, a ScalableFloat or a ScalableInt, which ScalableFloatDrawer and ScalableIntDrawer draw.

This section covers the field in the editor, its menu and the windows it opens, and the Try sections. Scalers: A magnitude field covers the kinds and what each reads.

In this section

  • The field: A number field shows its name, its kind (Constant or another), its number or the range it gives, such as "6–8 s", and its field menu (⋮).
  • Its windows: The field menu opens three windows: Save as Scaler Asset, Import Values and Quick Fill.
  • Try: A Scaler asset's inspector and a Value Pipeline's have a Try section, which reads the asset at each level of a range.
  • The drawers: These drawers draw number fields and the scalers in them.
