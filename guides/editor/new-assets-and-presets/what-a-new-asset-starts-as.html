What a new asset starts as

A new asset is made in four steps:

  1. It starts as a copy of its type's New Asset default, where Project Settings → PlayForge names one.
  2. Its preset fills it, or it becomes a copy of its template.
  3. It's named, with the asset tag its name gives. The tag is the type, then the name's words capitalized and joined, punctuation left out, such as Item.IronSword for Iron Sword.
    • An attribute takes no asset tag.
    • A Scaler asset's name is its scaler's, and a Requirement Template's is its requirements'.
  4. It's saved in the folder, its file named for it. Its spaces, and any character a file can't have on any platform, are left out. A number is added where the folder has the name already.

The first asset made while the Unity project has no Forge Project makes one, named for the game, in a folder of its own under the PlayForge Folder.
