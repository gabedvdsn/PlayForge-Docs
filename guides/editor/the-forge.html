The Forge

The Forge (TheForge) is PlayForge's main window. It lists the active Forge Project's assets beside a detail pane that shows the one selected.

Open it from Window → PlayForge → The Forge, or press Alt+Shift+F to open it with its search focused. The Forge follows the project as assets are made, edited, moved and deleted, so it has no Refresh.

In code, TheForge.ShowWindow() opens The Forge as the menu does. TheForge.OpenToAsset(asset) opens it on an asset, with its type's filter on and the asset selected, as an inspector's Open in The Forge does.

This section covers The Forge's toolbar, its list, its detail pane, the asset menu, the window menu and its keys.

In this section

  • Your first Forge Project: While the Unity project has no Forge Project, The Forge asks you to make the first one, in place of its list (Forge Projects).
  • The toolbar: The toolbar runs along the top of The Forge.
  • The list: The list shows the active project's assets that the toolbar's filters and search let through, one row each.
  • The detail pane: The detail pane shows the selected asset's Summary above its inspector.
  • The asset menu: The asset menu acts on the selected assets.
  • The window menu: The window menu is the ⋮ on The Forge's tab.
  • Keys: These keys work in The Forge and its dialogs.
