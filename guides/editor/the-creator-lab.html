The Creator Lab

The Creator Lab is a scene for trying a project's own assets: units, test dummies, and its time in your hands. This section names its editor parts, in FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.Editor. The Creator Lab covers the lab.

The Creator Lab: a quick battle, a unit selected.

The lab's assemblies are left out where PLAYFORGE_NO_LAB is defined.

In this section

  • Opening the lab: CreatorLabMenu opens the lab from Window → PlayForge → Creator Lab.
  • The lab's library: EditorLabLibrary is the lab's library in the editor.
  • The lab's inspectors: LabRulesEditor, LabDriverEditor and LabScenarioEditor are the inspectors of the Lab Rules, Lab Driver and Lab Scenario assets.
