Fields and drawers

PlayForge's property drawers draw its fields wherever they're shown: in its own inspectors, in the Inspector of a component, and in an inspector of your own built with UI Toolkit. Most draw in UI Toolkit alone. This section covers the kinds of field they make, then lists the drawers.

Asset fields

A field that holds a PlayForge asset, a Value Pipeline, Crowd Control Rules or an Effect Modifier is Unity's object field, with a button beside it:

  • Open, while it names an asset, shows the asset in a window of its own, so the asset that holds the field stays in the Inspector.
  • New, while it's empty, opens the New Asset window, its name starting as the holder's and the field's, as "Goblin Attribute Set", then makes the asset where The Forge makes its type and puts it in the field.

While the Unity project has a Forge Project, the field's picker offers the active project's assets alone, in PlayForge's dropdown.

An effect field has a line under it that says how the effect takes its levels: Linked to the asset that holds the field, with its levels; linked to another asset, with Link Here; or Not linked, with Link. Link and Link Here make the effect take the holder's levels, as one Undo step, and an effect New makes takes them too. Its right-click menu holds:

  • on an ability's Cost and Cooldown, Copy the Cost Effect Template, which makes a copy of the project's Cost Effect named for the ability, as "Fireball Cost Effect", taking its levels, and Use the Cost Effect Template, which puts the project's Cost Effect itself in the field; the Cooldown's alike;
  • Pick an Unlinked Effect..., a pick of the project's effects that take no asset's levels;
  • New Linked Effect..., which names a new effect in the New Asset window and makes it take the holder's levels.

Kind fields

A field that holds an object of one of several classes, as an ability task, a worker, a component, a cue handler or a purge's filter, shows the kind it holds in its box: the name its [ForgeMenu] gives, or its class's, spaced out, without the ending its kind shares, as Apply Effects for ApplyEffectsAbilityTask. Pressing the box opens PlayForge's dropdown of the kinds it can hold, under the submenus their [ForgeMenu] paths name, as Effects for Apply Effects, with None first where the field may be empty and the kind it holds checked. Its search finds a kind in any submenu, and its Context says the kind's description.

  • Picking a kind makes a new object of it for each selected asset, as one Undo step, and the kind's own fields show under it, a field marked [ShownWhen] only while the field it names says so.
  • A class Unity can't find, as after it's renamed, shows as missing, and keeps its data.
  • Workers, components, cue handlers and the other kinds that fold close to one line of their values, and keep their fold for the session as the object moves in its list.
  • A component kind an asset can hold once isn't offered while its list holds one.

Tag fields

A tag field shows its tag by its display name, its parents in secondary text and its leaf in strong text, or its whole path while Show Full Tag Paths is on. Pressing it opens the tag picker: the tags its context offers in the active project, with None, and New Tag… where the field may make one. Its right-click menu holds None, New Tag…, Make It a Child Of… and Copy Path. Tags covers tag fields in full.

Lists

PlayForge's lists are Unity's reorderable lists. A list whose field holds its objects by reference, as a stage's tasks or a worker group's workers, adds an empty element, for its kind to be picked. A list whose elements hold such objects, as a Group's members, adds a copy of the last element, with copies of its objects of its own. As an element is removed or dragged, each keeps its fold.

PlayForge's dropdown

Every picker, a kind field's, the tag picker, an asset field's, Set Template..., a sequence reference's, and a number field's kind menu and field menu, opens PlayForge's dropdown: a title, a search field, the choices in their submenus, and at its foot a Context that says more of the choice the pointer or the keys are on. It grows no wider than Menu Width nor taller than Menu Height (Preferences → PlayForge), and a longer list scrolls. Up and Down move, Enter picks, Right opens a submenu and Left goes back out of one, and Escape closes it.

The drawers

The base classes:

  • AbstractForgeObjectDrawer<T>: an asset field, with Open and New. A drawer made from it adds its own items to the field's right-click menu.
  • AbstractTypeRefDrawer<T>: a kind field. A drawer made from it chooses which kinds the picker offers, how they're named, and whether the field may be empty.
  • AbstractGenericDrawer<T>: a kind field that folds to a line of its values.
  • AbstractWorkerDrawer<T>: a folding kind field that's always of some kind, as a worker.
  • AbstractComponentDrawer<T>: a component, which offers a kind an asset can hold once only while its list holds none.
  • InlineFieldsDrawer: a field's own fields, drawn where it's held, with no foldout of their own.
