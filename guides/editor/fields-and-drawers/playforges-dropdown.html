PlayForge's dropdown

PlayForge's dropdown is the menu every PlayForge picker opens: a kind field's, the tag picker, an asset field's, Set Template..., a sequence reference's, and a number field's kind menu and field menu.

It holds a title, a search field, and the choices in their submenus. At its foot, a Context says more of the choice the pointer or the keys are on.

It grows no wider than Menu Width nor taller than Menu Height (Preferences → PlayForge). A longer list scrolls.

Key What it does
Up, Down Move between the choices.
Enter Picks the choice.
Right Opens a submenu.
Left Goes back out of a submenu.
Escape Closes the dropdown.
