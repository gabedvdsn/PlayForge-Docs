Kind fields

A kind field holds an object of one of several classes, such as an ability task, a worker, a component, a cue handler or a purge's filter.

Its box shows the kind it holds: the name its [ForgeMenu] gives, or its class's name spaced out, without the ending its kind shares. For example, ApplyEffectsAbilityTask shows as Apply Effects.

Picking a kind

Pressing the box opens PlayForge's dropdown of the kinds the field can hold. They sit under the submenus their [ForgeMenu] paths name, such as Effects for Apply Effects.

  • None comes first, where the field may be empty.
  • The kind the field holds is checked.
  • The search finds a kind in any submenu.
  • The Context says the kind's description.

Picking a kind makes a new object of it for each selected asset, as one Undo step.

What the field shows

  • The kind's own fields show under it. A field marked [ShownWhen] shows only while the field it names says so.
  • A class Unity can't find, such as after it's renamed, shows as missing, and keeps its data.
  • Workers, components, cue handlers and the other kinds that fold show one line of their values when closed. They keep their fold for the session as the object moves in its list.
  • A component kind an asset can hold once isn't offered while its list holds one.
