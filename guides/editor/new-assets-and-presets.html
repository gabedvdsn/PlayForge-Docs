New assets and presets

The Forge's Create makes an asset of any type it lists, blank or from a preset, ready to fill in. This section covers the New Asset window, what a new asset starts as, PlayForge's presets and a project's own templates.

In this section

  • Create: Create, on The Forge's toolbar, lists each type The Forge lists.
  • The New Asset window: The New Asset window (ForgeNewAssetWindow) names a new asset and says where it goes.
  • What a new asset starts as: A new asset is made in four steps
  • PlayForge's presets: PlayForge's presets fill a new effect, item, ability or entity with a common setup.
  • The project's templates: A project can keep assets of its own that new assets start as copies of.
