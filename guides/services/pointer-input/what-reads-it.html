What reads it

Three parts of PlayForge read ForgeInput:

  • Click targeting, the Select tasks (Position, Direction, GAS Target, GAS Or Position and GAS Target In Range), waits for PointerPressed of its Mouse Button. Then it aims along TryGetPointerRay (Abilities: Activation).
  • The task libraries' waits: WaitForKey and WaitForAnyKey read KeyPressed, and WaitForPointer reads PointerPressed, in SequenceTaskLibrary and SyncSequenceTaskLibrary alike (Task Sequences: Waiting).
  • The Creator Lab: its presses and keys, the pointer's place, the middle button held to pan, and the wheel to zoom (The Creator Lab).
