Sign and math policies

Three enums say how PlayForge reads the sign of a change and turns a number into a factor: ESignPolicy, ESignPolicyExtended and EMathApplicationPolicy.

  • ESignPolicy is a change's sign: Positive; Negative; ZeroBiased, where its parts sum to 0 but aren't all 0, as +5 to an attribute's current value and −5 to its base; and ZeroNeutral, where every part is 0. ForgeHelper.SignPolicy reads it from one number, which is never ZeroBiased, or from a change's current and base parts, and a change's own SignPolicy, as a SourcedModifiedAttributeValue's, is its parts read so. An impact worker made from AbstractContextImpactWorker takes the hits of one sign, its Impact Sign.
  • ESignPolicyExtended adds Any, which takes every sign, as a focused attribute worker's Sign Policy does by default (Workers: The map).
  • EMathApplicationPolicy turns a number into a factor: AsIs; OnePlus, one plus it, as a bonus of 0.2 multiplies by 1.2; or OneMinus, one minus it, as a resistance of 0.2 leaves 0.8. AttenuateScalerBehaviour's Policy and MathAttributeWorker's Operation Policy read it.
