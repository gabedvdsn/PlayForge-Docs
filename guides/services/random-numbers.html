Random numbers

ForgeRandom is where PlayForge draws its random numbers. The game's rolls and random picks all draw from its source, an IForgeRandom.

Member What it draws
ForgeRandom.Value A number from 0 to 1, as Random.value.
InsideUnitCircle, InsideUnitSphere A point within a radius of 1.
Range(min, max) A float from the first to the second.
Range(min, maxExclusive) A whole number, up to but not including the second. Where the range is empty, the first itself.

In this section

  • The source: ForgeRandom draws from Unity's source, UnityRandom, through UnityRandom.Instance, until code puts another in.
  • What draws from it: These draw from ForgeRandom
