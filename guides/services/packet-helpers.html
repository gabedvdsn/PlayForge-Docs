Packet helpers

ProcessTaskUtil holds extension methods for a data packet's targets and the effects it carries, and three loops for async code that wait through ForgeTick.

  • data.GetTargetingPacket(tag, out packet) gives the primary AbstractTargetingPacket under a tag, or false where there's none, and then interrupts the packet's sequence, where it's a SequenceDataPacket, unless it's called with autoInterrupt: false.
  • data.SetTargetingPacket(tag, packet) files one as the primary, and data.SetTargetingPackets(tag, targets) files the targeting packet of each target, in their order and in place of what was there, so the first is the primary.
  • data.TryApplyEffects(target, tag) applies each effect loaded under the tag to a target, an ITarget or a GameObject that has one, each made from the ability's origin with the magnitudes its caller set. It's false where the packet isn't an ability's, an AbilityDataPacket, where it holds no effects under the tag, or where there's no target.
  • data.TryGetLoadedAssets<T>(tag, out assets) and data.GetLoadedAssets<T>(tag) give every value of a kind under a tag, as a DataValue<T>, empty where there's none.
  • ProcessTaskUtil.WhileAsync(condition, token) waits while a condition holds, a tick at a time; ProcessTaskUtil.DoWhileAsync(body, condition, token) awaits the body, then a tick, while the condition holds, the body at least once; and ProcessTaskUtil.DoWhile(body, duration, token) does so until the duration has passed, by ForgeTime.Time, or by its unscaled time where its useUnscaledTime says. Each takes a PlayerLoopTiming, Update by default, and waits as ForgeTick.Next does (Fixed ticks).
