Log levels

A message is let through where its level is at least its system's level. That's the system's own, where it has one, or else ForgeLog.MinimumLevel, Info by default.

Method What it does
ForgeLog.SetLevel(system, level) Gives a system a level of its own.
ForgeLog.SetLevel(system, null) Gives the system back to ForgeLog.MinimumLevel.
ForgeLog.LevelOf(system) Gives the system's level now.
ForgeLog.HasOwnLevel(system) Says whether the system has a level of its own.
ForgeLog.ClearLevels() Gives every system back.
Note

What's said only when asked is information, of level Info: its switch makes it, and the level shows it (Process and sequence logs). A level above Info hides it, however it's switched on.

In this section

  • In the editor: In the editor, you set the level in PlayForge's preferences.
  • In a build: A build reads no preference.
