Entity clocks

An entity's clock, gas.GetClock(), runs at the entity's TimeScale: OwnScale times AttributeScale.

Property Description
OwnScale The scale code sets, as a hit-stop's 0.
AttributeScale The scale the entity's Time Scale attribute gives, read as each of the entity's steps begins. It's 1 where its identity names none (Entities: Attributes).

Setting TimeScale sets OwnScale. So a hit-stop holds under a slow, and the slow is still there after.

Its DeltaTime and FixedDeltaTime are the game's times its scale. Its Time is its steps added up since it started. Its unscaled times and its frame are the game's.

A scale of 0 holds the entity's time still: its effects, its cooldowns among them, and its abilities' waits and sequences stand. It isn't a pause, which ProcessControl's is (Processes: Pausing).

// A hit-stop on the entity struck: its own time held, its slows and hastes kept for after
var clock = gas.GetClock();
clock.OwnScale = 0f;
// ... a moment later
clock.OwnScale = 1f;
