Chance

ForgeChance rolls every chance PlayForge has. Each rolls by its ChanceRoll, which says whether it's pseudo-random and whose luck it reads.

PlayForge's chances are:

  • an effect's Chance to Apply;
  • an ability's Chance to Activate;
  • a trigger's Chance;
  • a Value Pipeline's Chance, Block and Evade stages;
  • the Chance condition.

Each has a ChanceRoll beside it, its Chance Roll, Activation Roll or Roll, which says how it rolls (Effects: Requirements):

Property Description
Pseudo Random Each miss raises the next roll's odds, and a hit starts them again (Pseudo-random rolls).
Own Luck The roll reads the chance's own Luck, in place of the caster's attribute in the project's Luck role (Luck).
Luck The chance's own luck, a number read for the roll.

In this section

  • Rolling a chance: ForgeChance.Roll(chance, in context, roll, key) says whether a chance, from 0 to 1, comes up for the caster its NumberContext gives.
  • Pseudo-random rolls: A pseudo-random roll evens out streaks: each miss raises the next roll's odds, and a hit starts them again.
  • Luck: A roll's luck, ForgeChance.LuckOf(in context, roll), is the first of these that applies
  • The Chance condition: The Chance condition, ChanceCondition, holds on a roll of its Chance, rolled by its Roll.
