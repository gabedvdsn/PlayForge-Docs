Logging

ForgeLog is where everything PlayForge says goes. Each message has a level and a system, and a gate on the two decides what reaches Unity's Console.

  • Levels, ForgeLog.ELevel, from the most said to the least: Verbose, Info, Warning, Error, and None. None lets nothing through, errors neither (Log levels).
  • Systems, ForgeLog.ESystem: General, Effects, Abilities, Attributes, Workers, Numbers, Processes, Tags, Items, Saves, Input, Lab and Sequences. Each can take a level of its own.

Its messages reach the Console with [PlayForge] before them: a warning as a warning, and an error as an error.

Its methods are marked [HideInCallstack]. So the Console's Strip Logging Callstack leaves them out of the stack, and a double-click on a message opens the code that logged it.

In this section

  • Logging from code: Your code logs through ForgeLog as PlayForge does.
  • Hearing the log: ForgeLog.Logged hears each message let through, as a ForgeLogEntry
