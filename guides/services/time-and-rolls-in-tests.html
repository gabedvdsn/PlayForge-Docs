Time and rolls in tests

The test kit makes time and chance a test's own, through the same two services (Testing Your Content: The world).

Method What it does
World.Clock(step) Puts in a SteppingClock, and returns it.
World.Rolls(0.1f, 0.9f) Puts in a ScriptedRandom.
ForgeChance.ForgetStreaks(caster) Starts a caster's pseudo-random streaks clean.

A SteppingClock gives step seconds of PlayForge's time each frame: its DeltaTime, UnscaledDeltaTime and FixedDeltaTime. Its Time, UnscaledTime and FrameCount stay Unity's. Its Step can change.

A ScriptedRandom gives each draw the next of its rolls, in turn and round again, and its Drawn counts them. Its points in a circle and in a sphere are zero.

A chance comes up where it's above the roll. So a roll of 0 always lands, and one of 0.99 almost never does. A roll with luck draws more than one.

The world gives back the clock and the source it replaced as it ends. Outside it, ForgeTime.Use(new SteppingClock(step)) and ForgeRandom.Use(new ScriptedRandom(0f)) do the same, put back by hand.
