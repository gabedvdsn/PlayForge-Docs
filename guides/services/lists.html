Lists

ReadOnlyListView<T> and ForgeUtils are two small list helpers: a view of a list that foreach walks without allocating, and the index of a list's least or greatest item.

  • ReadOnlyListView<T> is a read-only struct over a List<T>: its Count, an indexer, and a GetEnumerator that hands foreach the list's own struct enumerator, so walking it allocates nothing. Walked as an IEnumerable<T>, as LINQ walks it, its enumerator is boxed, as any list's is. A view of no list reads as empty. An effect spec's GetImpactedAttributes(delivery) returns one.
  • ForgeUtils.IndexMin(list, selector) and ForgeUtils.IndexMax(list, selector) give the index of the item whose selected value is least or greatest, the first of equals, and 0 for an empty list.
