The base of an ability's targeting task, the one task that picks what an activation acts on before its stages run, as a click picks an entity or a search takes the enemies in range. Its Activate files the targeting packet of what it picks under the activation's Target key, whose entity becomes the activation's target, or calls WhenTargetingInvalid, which ends the activation where BreakRuntimeOnInvalid is on. Targeting holds the activation's claim while it runs, and its slot's IsTargeting says while it chooses.

A task of your own overrides Activate; Prompt, the words a game shows while it waits for the player; TargetIsValid, which targets it takes; and ConnectInputHandler and DisconnectInputHandler, to hook an input handler of its own as it's prepared and unhook it as it's cleaned, an override of Prepare calling the base's last. TryGetDataTarget and TryGetDataPoint take a target or a point the activation's data already holds, as an AI's does, and WaitForPointerPress waits for a press of the game's input.

IsCriticalSection

Determines whether another ability proxy can be active at the same time as the proxy containing this task.

For example, a proxy with some animation events has a critical section, and another proxy with a critical section must not interrupt the conclusion of the animation (and the injections relevant to the animation).

If a proxy with a critical section is active, no other proxy with a critical section can be active, regardless of ability activation policy. Under Activate Anyway or Refuse, the other is refused as Critical; under Queue, it waits in the input buffer.

WaitForPointerPress(int, CancellationToken)

Waits for the targeting input to press the button, and gives the ray it aims along as it does: by default, from the camera through the pointer. Null is a press with nothing to aim through, as with no camera. The click-targeting tasks read the Input Manager's mouse and Camera.main themselves, and threw with no camera.
