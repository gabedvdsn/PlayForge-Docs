Class PrefabCue

Namespace

FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

Places a prefab where its placement says: a burst as its moment comes, gone after its Lifetime, or, in a Cues component's list of lasting cues, as an effect's While Active, for as long as its cue lasts. Its placement puts it on the one the cue is on, its source, an ability's target or each of its targets, or at the cue's point, as a hit's: at a socket, moved and scattered, following them while it lasts or as it says. Pooled, it takes its objects from ForgeCuePool and gives them back, as Unreal recycles its cue actors.
