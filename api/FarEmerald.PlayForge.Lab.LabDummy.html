A test dummy, and the Demo's training dummy: a unit that stands and counts what it takes. It shows its damage a second over a window, the last blow with its types, and a short history of blows. It doesn't die unless asked: falling, it's revived where it stands at once. Left alone a few seconds, it mends to full, by the mend effect given, or else one of the lab's own.

Guide: The Creator Lab
