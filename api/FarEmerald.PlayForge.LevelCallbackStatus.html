What a change to one of an entity's levels did, as its level system's callbacks and a hub's SetLevel and ModifyLevel report it: the level under its key, the level and max level before, and whether it took. IsLevelUp, IsLevelDown and the deltas compare before with after; Success is false where no level is under the key or nothing changed.
