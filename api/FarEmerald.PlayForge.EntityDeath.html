What an entity does as it dies and as it revives: a MOBA hero, or anyone who dies and comes back, keeps its entity while its body goes. Where it stays: the effects its death ends, by tag, as buffs; the effects it takes as it dies, as a respawn timer; the effects it takes as it revives, as its health back; and its body, a MonoBehaviour process it spawns where it is, as it starts and as it revives, and ends as it dies. A runtime entity takes its position from its body.
