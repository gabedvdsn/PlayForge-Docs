What an effect has dealt: its last impact, its total, or how many impacts, as each effect spec tracks them once they land, after workers, so a blow reads as it landed, after the target's armor. Whose: this effect, the one whose number it is, as a burn that grows each tick by its own count; an effect on the caster or the target, by its asset, its containers summed, as a combustion consuming a burn's total; or every effect there tagged with a tag, summed. An impact is signed, so damage is negative; BySize reads it as a size, as a heal of half a blow wants. Where nothing is tracked, as an effect that isn't on the target, it reads 0.

At its first application an effect has dealt nothing: its last impact and total are 0, and so is its count. An effect's magnitude is worked out as it's applied and again at each tick, so a number on its own count reads the ticks so far. A save restores an effect's time and stacks but not what it has dealt, so after a load its tracked impact starts again at 0.
