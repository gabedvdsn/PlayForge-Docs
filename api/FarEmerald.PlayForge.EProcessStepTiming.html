Enum EProcessStepTiming

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

Which of Unity's loops a process is stepped in while it runs, each step calling its WhenUpdate, WhenLateUpdate or WhenFixedUpdate: one of Update, LateUpdate and FixedUpdate, two of them, all three, or None, never stepped. A GAS hub steps in Update and LateUpdate: Update ticks its effects, and LateUpdate runs its end of frame. Under ProcessControl's Fixed Tick Rate, each tick steps the Update and LateUpdate processes.
