A gameplay cue: what something shows and sounds, apart from what it does. An effect's, an ability's, an entity's and an item's Cues components list their cues, each a list for each of its moments, and ForgeCues plays them all. A burst plays as a moment comes; a lasting cue begins as its moment comes and ends as it's over, as an effect that lands and later comes off, and a restore ends it and begins it again, playing no burst. Each hook is a no-op until a handler overrides it. An effect changed attributes and tags and nothing else, so what it showed was each game's own code.
