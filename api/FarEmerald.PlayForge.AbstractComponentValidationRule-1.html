A check an ability's component stands behind, as Check Range is the Cast Range component's: shown among the ability's checks while the ability holds the component, and tied to it. The component enforces what it says, so the rule passes; no list holds it and the picker doesn't offer it, as it comes with its component and goes with it. A component of your own brings one with its TargetCheck or CasterCheck.
