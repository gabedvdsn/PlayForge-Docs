Namespace FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab

Classes

CreatorLab

The Creator Lab: a space where an author puts units down, gives them their project's abilities, effects and items, casts, and watches the numbers move. It builds what it needs as it wakes: PlayForge, where the scene has none; the ground, a light and its camera; a world of its own, which it steps by its own time; its own caster, a neutral source for what the author drops; and its panels.

Window → PlayForge → Creator Lab opens it in a new scene. A test makes one with Create(ILabLibrary, LabRules, bool) and drives it through the same calls the panels make.

LabAssets

What the lab knows of an asset of each kind: its kind, its name, its color.

LabBodyDriver

The lab's own driver: its units walk in straight lines, set on a target, and cast as the lab's do.

LabBot

A bot's mind, the Demo's AI and the Battle sample's fighters made general: it takes the nearest foe it can target, casts what's ready in its order when its moment fits, handing its target in the activation's data, which click targeting takes with no click, and otherwise attacks. Low, it backs off a moment once in a while.

Its order is its entity's Local Data under the rules' cast order key, as "W Q E", the letters its casts in its entity's order; with none, each in turn. An ability's moment is its Local Data under the rules' cast-when key: InRange, the default, within its Cast Range, or else its Local Data reach, 0 for anywhere; Hurt, below 60% health; Low, below 35%, cast away from its foe, as an escape; Stunned, while something stops it; FoeLow, with its foe below half.

LabCamera

The lab's camera: it looks down at an angle at a point on the ground, from the distance and tilt it's set to. Scroll pulls it back, a middle drag pans it, and ResetView() puts it back as it started. It follows a unit when given one, as the Demo's follows its hero, and shakes as a cue asks, as the game's camera shaker PlayForge's cues reach through ForgeCues.

LabControls

A player's controls for one unit, as a MOBA's: right-click the ground to move, right-click a foe it can target to attack it, Q, W, E and R for its abilities past its attack, 1 to 6 for its items' abilities, S to stop, Escape to cancel a cast. An ability that needs a target waits for a left-click, as its own click targeting reads it; meanwhile a ring on the ground shows the ability's reach, another ability's key casts that one instead, and a right-click order lets go of it. It reads PlayForge's input, ForgeInput, so a test or a game's own input stands in, and gives its orders through a driver: its own, its unit's rules', or the lab's. The Creator Lab's take its controlled unit; the Demo's player is these, its hero's.

LabDriver

A project's driver as an asset: each order a virtual that does as the lab's does, so a project overrides those its game gives its own way, and names the asset on its Lab Rules. Create → PlayForge → Lab Driver.

LabDummy

A test dummy, and the Demo's training dummy: a unit that stands and counts what it takes. It shows its damage a second over a window, the last blow with its types, and a short history of blows. It doesn't die unless asked: falling, it's revived where it stands at once. Left alone a few seconds, it mends to full, by the mend effect given, or else one of the lab's own.

LabEffects

The effects the lab makes for itself, as an asset's inspector would fill one in: a dummy's mend, where the project gives none. Each lives in memory alone and goes with what made it.

LabPanels

The lab's panels, UI Toolkit over the space: the library on the left, the mode on the right with the selected over it, shown as something is selected and set aside by its tab, and the log at the bottom; each other panel collapses to a tab at its edge. The middle stays the space's. Over the space, each unit's bar, and a status at the top that clicks go through: what a cast waits for, what's selected, whom the author controls, and the keys for what can be done now. An asset dragged from the library and dropped on the space is the lab's to apply, cast or give. Neutral panels, Unity's own controls, and a type's color in its icon alone.

LabRules

A project's rules for the Creator Lab, as an asset. An attribute left empty is the one playing its role in the project, Health or Movement Speed, or else found by its name among the project's: Health, and Movement Speed or Move Speed. With no asset, the lab makes one and finds both.

LabRulesExtensions

The checks a unit's rules answer, in one place for the lab, its bots and the Demo.

LabSeat

A bot's place in a battle: what it's made of, its side, its level and where it starts, and what the author gave it since, which a reset gives again as it makes the bot anew.

LabShowing

What the lab watches its units for, and the journals it keeps while it does: its effect journal over every unit, its stops, which pause its world as one fires and say which, and its watches, sampled over the last minute of its time. The lab steps it after each step of its world.

LabSides

The lab's sides: Blue and Red for a fight, Green and Gold for more teams, and Neutral, the side of no one, which the lab's own caster stands on. Each is an affiliation tag of the lab's own, made as it's first asked for.

LabStop

A stop: what it watches for, on the selected unit or any, and what it names, an effect, an ability, a tag, or an attribute and a value. The lab's world pauses as one fires, and says which.

LabSummary

The selected panel's lines: an asset's numbers level by level, as "−80/−120/−160 Health", and a unit's attributes and effects as they are now.

LabUnit

A unit in the lab, and the Demo's hero: a GameplayAbilitySystem on a body of its own, which walks where it's sent, attacks what it's set on, casts its abilities and its items', and holds its items. Its attributes, tags, levels and Local Data are the GAS's; this is the space's side of it. Movement is the lab's, a straight line on the ground at its speed, as a project's own body would be for a game.

LabUnitSpec

What a unit is made from, and where it stands.

LabWatch

A watch: a unit's attribute, a tag's count, an effect's stacks or time, or a number read on it, pinned, and its values over the last minute of the lab's time, one each tenth of a second.

ListLibrary

A library of the assets given, as a test or a build gives them.

Structs

LabBlow

A blow a dummy took: when, how much, of what types, and from which effect.

LabSide

A side a unit stands on in the lab: an affiliation tag, as its registration's data gives it, a name and the color of its ring.

Interfaces

ILabLibrary

The assets the lab plays: in the editor every PlayForge asset of the project or of its active Forge Project, in a build those a project names. The library panel lists them by kind.

ILabRules

What the lab needs to know of a project to play its units: the attribute that is a unit's health, the one it walks at, the tags that stop, hide or shield it, where its attack range is kept, and which of its abilities is its attack. PlayForge has no movement or health of its own, so a project says which of its attributes are these. The Demo's roster says the same of the Demo.

ILabUnitDriver

How a unit the author controls is ordered about: moved to a point, set on a unit to attack, made to cast, stopped. PlayForge has no movement of its own, so a project whose units move and cast their own way gives its own driver, on its Lab Rules, and the lab's controls and the Demo's give their orders through it. Without one, the lab's: a unit walks in a straight line and casts as the lab's units do.
