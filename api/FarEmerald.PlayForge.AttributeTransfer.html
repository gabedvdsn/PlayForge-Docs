Class AttributeTransfer

FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

One impact that moves an amount from one attribute to another, each side its own: the target's Agility to the caster's, as Essence Shift steals it, or one entity's Agility to its Strength, as Attribute Shift moves it. Only what the giver has moves, so nothing is made from nothing. As its Delivery says, it moves once as the effect lands, each tick, or for as long as the effect lasts, a modifier on both that gives back as it ends.
