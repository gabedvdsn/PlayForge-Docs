Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge

A text a Floating Text cue floats: it moves, fades and scales over its life by its cue's motion, on the game's clock or in real time, faces the camera where asked, and goes back to its pool as its life ends. A number merged into it starts its life again.

Guide: Cues
