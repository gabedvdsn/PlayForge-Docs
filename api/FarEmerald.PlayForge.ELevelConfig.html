The level range a scaler's values spread over, where they spread: the level provider's (LockToLevelProvider, Provider's Levels), the scaler's own Max Level (Unlocked, Own Max Level), or the provider's up to that Max Level (Clamped, Clamped to Provider), so the values reach their last at the lower of the two.
