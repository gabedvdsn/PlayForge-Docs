The fixed-rate tick. With ProcessControl's tick rate set, the game steps in ticks, each a fixed step of its time, a number of them a frame: every process steps once a tick, its end of frame with it, and a sequence waits for the next tick where it waited for the next frame. So a run comes out the same at any frame rate. Off, as it is by default, a wait is Unity's next frame, as it was. A world of its own has a tick of its own, in force while it steps or moves a process, so its sequences wait on its steps. The ticks run on the game's time and stand with it, so a sequence on a clock that runs while it stands, as UnscaledClock does, waits for Unity's frames.
