Where something is, for targeting: its position, rotation and scale, the entity it is (target) and its transform where it has them, and the direction it was aimed along, as Select Direction sets. Each entity gives one (ITarget.GetTargetingPacket), and a targeting task files the packet of what it picks under the activation's Target key, each packet's entity becoming one of the activation's targets. TargetingPacket follows a transform, and StaticTargetingPacket holds its own values.
