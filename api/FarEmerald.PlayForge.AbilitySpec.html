Class AbilitySpec

Namespace

An ability as one entity knows it, made as the entity learns it: the Ability (Base) and its caster (Source), with its level kept on the caster under the ability's asset tag (GetLevel). It's the origin of the effects the ability applies, so they take its level and context tags, and an activation's data holds it as its EffectOrigin. It applies the ability's cost and cooldown (ApplyUsageEffects, PayCosts, SpendCooldown), reads the ability's numbers at its level (Read), finds its components (Find<T>), and keeps what they hold for this entity (ComponentStates) and its cooldown model (CooldownModel), which a HUD reads.
