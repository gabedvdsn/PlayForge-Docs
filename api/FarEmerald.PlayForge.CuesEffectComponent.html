Cues: what an effect shows and sounds at its moments, on the one it's on: as it lands, as an application it takes in lands, as each of its ticks executes, as an impact it deals lands, as it comes off, while it lasts, as an application of it is refused, and as its stacks change. An effect holds one at most.
