Templates as prefabs work. A template's changes reach its children when it's pushed to them, as an explicit step: each child, and each of theirs, takes the template's fields but those it overrides, and one Undo takes the push back. An edit to a child's field makes the field the child's own, an override, which its inspector marks, and Revert to Template takes back. Saving a template opened a dialog in the middle of the save, loaded every asset of its type, and changed the children with no Undo.
