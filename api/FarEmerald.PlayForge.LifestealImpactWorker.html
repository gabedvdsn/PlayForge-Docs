The Lifesteal worker: it heals its holder a share of each workable loss it deals another, the value of its holder's Share, the attribute it names, which an effect on the holder raises while it should. The losses that count are to its Receives attribute, and it heals its Heals attribute, each the Health role's where it names none. An effect has no lifesteal of its own. Only hits of the impact types it names count, as attacks, so a second worker on another attribute makes a spell vamp. A project gives it to every entity through its Forge Project's Workers, and an entity can hold one of its own; one that holds two heals a hit once. It allocates nothing, healing or not.
