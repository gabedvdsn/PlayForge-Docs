Cue Receiver: plays cue tags its own way for its entity, as a golem's hits, before the project's cue library does, as Unreal's actors handle cues through their GameplayCue interface. Its own routes play first; where it plays nothing for a tag, or passes it through, the library's route plays, its Variant's where the library has one, as Cue.Hit.Golem for Cue.Hit. An entity holds one at most.
