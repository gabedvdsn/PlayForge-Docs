Toggle: an effect an ability turns on its caster with one use, paying its cost, and off with the next, starting its cooldown. It applies its ability's usage in the ability's place, waives the cost and the cooldown while its effect is on, so it can always turn off, and claims its effect as a Toggle's for a purge. As its holder forgets the ability, the effect comes off, where it stayed on. An ability holds one at most.
