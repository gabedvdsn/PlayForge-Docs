Class IndependentDurationsContainer

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge

FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The container of Stacks Have Independent Durations: each stack keeps its own timer and ticks and comes off as its time runs out, so the effect lasts as long as its longest stack. Refresh restarts its oldest stack's time, and Extend adds a stack.
