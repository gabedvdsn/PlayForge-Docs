WithClock(IForgeClock)

Sets the clock the sequence runs by: its waits, delays, timeouts and elapsed time, its tasks' tweens and its synced tasks. UnscaledClock runs a menu's sequence while the game is paused, and a ScaledClock plays it slowed or sped up. A packet that names a clock of its own runs by that one instead; with neither, a sequence runs by its packet's owner's, an ability's caster's, or the game's.
