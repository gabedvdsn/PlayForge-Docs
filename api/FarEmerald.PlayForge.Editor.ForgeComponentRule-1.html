A check of one kind of component, wherever an asset holds it: an ability's, an effect's, an item's or an entity's. The validator finds it as it finds every rule, and it hears each component of its kind the asset holds, with the component's place in its list, which a problem names as its property path; an ability's inspector says its problems under the Components list. Derive from it, with a public constructor that takes nothing, for a component of your own, as PlayForge's components' rules do.
