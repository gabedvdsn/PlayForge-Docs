A number field: its name, then its kind, Constant or another, and either its number or its range, as in "6–8 s", and its field menu. Another kind adds a one-line summary of what it reads, how the field combines its number with the kind, then the kind's own content: its values per level and their curve, its Scaler asset, or its fields. The kind menu files kinds by category and offers only those the field's context resolves (NumberContextAttribute), and a field whose unit is "%" shows and takes its number as a percentage. Its constant keeps the field's limits, on a slider where it has a range, and its label shows the field's tooltip.
