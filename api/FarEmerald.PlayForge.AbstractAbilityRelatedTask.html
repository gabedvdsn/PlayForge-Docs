The base of every task an ability runs: its targeting task, an AbstractTargetingAbilityTask, and its stages' tasks and asset loaders, each an AbstractAbilityTask. Each is handed the activation's data: Prepare as its stage starts, Activate, the work its stage waits on, and Clean as the stage ends; IsCriticalSection says whether it's critical work, which commits its ability to its stage and keeps out another ability's critical work while it runs.

One task object serves every activation of its ability, so what it keeps for one belongs in the activation's data, not in its fields. Read gives one of its numbers at its ability's level, EntityOf its caster or its first target, and ApplyTo applies an effect from the ability with the activation's magnitudes.

IsCriticalSection

Determines whether another ability proxy can be active at the same time as the proxy containing this task.

For example, a proxy with some animation events has a critical section, and another proxy with a critical section must not interrupt the conclusion of the animation (and the injections relevant to the animation).

If a proxy with a critical section is active, no other proxy with a critical section can be active, regardless of ability activation policy. Under Activate Anyway or Refuse, the other is refused as Critical; under Queue, it waits in the input buffer.
