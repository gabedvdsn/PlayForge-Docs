Class LabUnit

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.dll

A unit in the lab, and the Demo's hero: a GameplayAbilitySystem on a body of its own, which walks where it's sent, attacks what it's set on, casts its abilities and its items', and holds its items. Its attributes, tags, levels and Local Data are the GAS's; this is the space's side of it. Movement is the lab's, a straight line on the ground at its speed, as a project's own body would be for a game. Its body is a capsule the lab makes, or an instance of a prefab of the project's, which it wraps as it does its own.
