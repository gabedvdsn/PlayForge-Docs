Class EffectJournal

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

An effect journal: each application, refusal (by requirement, immunity or chance), tick, impact and removal of an effect on the entities it listens to, with its number's trace as it lands, and each worker that ran for it, by its hook and its side, with the actions it queued and whether they ran. It keeps its last Keep entries, oldest first. It records only while it listens, and an entity no journal listens to pays nothing: the Creator Lab's bottom panel and the Debugger's Timeline read one.
