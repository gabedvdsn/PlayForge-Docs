Class AbilityReader

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

A reader of an ability's cooldown: Assign(Tag) names the tag, and each Update step it finds the GAS's longest effect duration under it. It shows nothing of what it finds.
