Saves an entity at rest and loads it back. Nothing captured an entity's state, so a game couldn't keep a battle between sessions.

Capture(IGameplayAbilitySystem, ForgeAssetCatalog, IForgeEntityIds) takes what lasts on an entity into a ForgeSnapshot: its attributes' permanent commits, its levels, the abilities it knows, its items with their stacks, worn or not, its effects with their time left, its loose tags and its time scale. Modifiers aren't saved: they come back with the effects that carry them. Restore(IGameplayAbilitySystem, ForgeSnapshot, ForgeAssetCatalog, IForgeEntityIds) ends what's running, lets go of what the entity has, and puts back what the save had, on the entity it was taken from or a fresh one of its kind.

A restore tells nothing as it goes. No effect is told as applied or removed, no attribute as changed, and nothing of an effect's application runs again: its workers, its commits on application, its contained effects. It tells OnRestored once, as it ends, for a listener to read the entity afresh.

Copy(IGameplayAbilitySystem, IGameplayAbilitySystem)

Copies what lasts on one entity onto another, as a mirror clone takes its original's state: what Capture(IGameplayAbilitySystem, ForgeAssetCatalog, IForgeEntityIds) takes, put back as Restore(IGameplayAbilitySystem, ForgeSnapshot, ForgeAssetCatalog, IForgeEntityIds) puts it, with no catalog and no IDs, as both entities are in this session. An effect another entity applied stays that entity's, and the entity's own level goes onto the other's own. A copy took a catalog naming every asset, which an asset made as the game runs never has.

Restore(IGameplayAbilitySystem, ForgeSnapshot, ForgeAssetCatalog, IForgeEntityIds)

Loads a save onto the entity. What's running ends and the entity lets go of what it has, then what the save had comes back: its abilities, items and effects, its levels and commits, its loose tags and time scale. One compile of the tags tells what the load changed, and OnRestored fires once. False where the save can't load, as onto a hub not yet built from an entity.
