A stage that can stop a hit: it rolls its chance once as the effect lands, before anything of it does, and on a roll that comes up nothing of the effect lands, its damage, tags, contained effects and on-hits, and each side hears it. A lasting effect's ticks aren't rolled again. As the hit commits it passes the value on.
