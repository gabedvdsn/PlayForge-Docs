Class DeathAction

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The deferred action that kills its entity as its turn comes, at Critical priority: the entity is marked dead, which its callbacks, its components, its effects and its killer hear, and it ends, or stays dead, at its frame's end, as its Death says. It's dropped where the entity is dead already; a death watcher and the Health role's watch queue it.
