An Attribute Set asset: the attributes an entity starts with, and how each starts, its Current and Base, its bounds and its rounding, with those of its sub-sets. An entity names one, and as its GAS initializes each element becomes its attribute's root value, the value before any effect, which a cached scaler can tie to the entity's level or to another attribute.

Where an attribute appears in more than one element, the elements' Collision and the collision settings of the set the entity names, Within This Set and With Sub-Sets, decide its root. The entity carries the set's tag and its sub-sets', and holds their worker groups' workers as it lives. The set's level range is its own, or a level provider's while it's linked, and its cached scalers can size their values per level by it.

How the set settles an attribute it gives twice itself, in its own elements or in a sub-set's: the highest element, the lowest, their average, the first, the last, or their sum (Combine).
