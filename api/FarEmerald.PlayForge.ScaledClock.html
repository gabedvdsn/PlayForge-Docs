Class ScaledClock

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

A time scale of its own over another clock, its source: a sequence on it runs at its scale, as a finisher's slow motion or a flourish at double speed, and the sequences that share one slow, speed up or stop together as it's set. Its source is the clock given, or, with none, the clock in force: the game's, or a world's or a tick's while one steps.

Named in: Task Sequences: Clocks · Getting Started: Quickstart: a damage ability in ~10 minutes
