A rule that holds while its condition does, as an effect's requirements and an On Hit read theirs: a tag, an attribute, a level, a comparison of numbers, or any condition of your own. It's read against the activation, its caster and its ability's level and Local Data, with the entity its list hands it as the target: the target among the Target Checks, and the caster itself among the Caster Checks. With no condition, it holds.
