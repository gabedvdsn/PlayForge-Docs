The Forge, PlayForge's main window: a list of the project's PlayForge assets beside a detail pane that holds the selected asset's inspector, under a summary of what it does and what uses it. The toolbar holds Create, the type filters, Tags, the search and the problem count; the status bar holds the counts. It follows the project, with no Refresh and no timer, and the validator's problems with it. One click selects a row and shows its asset. It had Create, View, Analysis and Settings tabs: the Analysis tab's power scores stay hidden until Items and Entities score too, and the Settings tab's settings are on the two settings pages.
