The lab's dropdown menu, under the field that opened it: a search over its choices, which has the keyboard as it opens, so typing narrows them; its choices, as wide as the longest one, the one chosen checked and the one lit in the selection's blue; the arrows to move, Enter to pick the one lit, Esc to close. One is open at a time, and a press outside it closes it.
