An entity's levels, each under a tag: its own, under its identity's asset tag, and each of its abilities' and items', under theirs, registered as they're given. TrySetLevel and TryModifyLevel set or change one, as a hub's SetLevel and ModifyLevel do, and its Callbacks hear each change; an effect that reads a level reads it again as it changes. A hub's LevelSystem gives it.
