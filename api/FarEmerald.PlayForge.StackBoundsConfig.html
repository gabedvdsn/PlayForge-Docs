The stack behaviour Stack Bounds: a Min and a Max of stacks, each read at the effect's level. A new container is given the Min as it's made, and no application takes the effect past the Max, more where a modifier raises it: one that would overflows, its Overflow Effects landing from its origin on the target or the caster, as Overflow Lands On says, with Deny Refresh On Overflow and Clear Stacks On Overflow saying what becomes of the effect's time and stacks. It applies after the other behaviours, so its bounds have the last word.
