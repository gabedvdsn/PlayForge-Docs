Namespace

A rule an ability's activation keeps, for its source or its target. It took the generic IValidationRule, which nothing else took; it declares its own Validate.

A rule is a question, asked as often as the activation needs it answered: as it's requested, each frame a buffered request waits, and again once targeting has found its target, as the caster may have changed meanwhile. So a rule changes nothing and draws nothing: one that rolled a chance rolled it each time it was asked.

Validate checks the entity getSource hands it: the caster among an ability's Caster Checks, the target among its Target Checks. Refused, it says why in error, as "Hale's Health, 80, isn't < 50". A kind says which of the two it belongs among with ValidationSideAttribute.
