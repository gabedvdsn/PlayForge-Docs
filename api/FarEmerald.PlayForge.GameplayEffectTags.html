An effect's tags: its Asset Tag, by which the effect system finds it as it's applied again or removed, and which its target carries while it lasts; its Context Tags, which describe it and go with each change it makes, never applied to the entity; its Granted Tags, applied to its target while it's on; and the tags it cleanses (Removes Effects Tagged) and makes its target immune to (Blocks Effects Tagged). Names says whether an effect is tagged with a tag, or a child of one, by any of the three.
