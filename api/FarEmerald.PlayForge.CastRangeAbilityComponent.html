Cast Range: how far from its caster an ability's target may be, the one range it has, read at its level: the ability's reach, an IAbilityReach. An activation handed a target or a point beyond it is refused as Range, and targeting that picks one beyond it ends the activation there, told as Range too; a targeting task with a range of its own searches within it, from the caster; and the lab's bots cast it only once their foe is within it, each asking the ability's reach through ForgeReach. 0 reaches anywhere. An ability holds one at most. Its ability's Target Checks show its Check Range while it holds the component, which this enforces.
