An ability: one thing an entity can do, as a fireball, a dash or a heal. An entity learns it from its Entity asset's Starting Abilities, from an equipped item's Active Ability or from code (AbilitySystemComponent.GiveAbility), and keeps it in a slot with a level of its own, between its StartingLevel and MaxLevel, kept under its asset tag.

An activation passes its caster's tag requirements and its Caster Checks (SourceActivationRules), lets its Behaviour's targeting task pick its target, which its Target Checks (TargetActivationRules) then check, applies its Cost and Cooldown effects to its caster and spends its AttributeCosts, as it starts or in the stage that takes them, and runs its stages of tasks. Its Definition says how it activates, its Tags what it is, grants and requires, and its Components add features with hooks into its life; its WorkerGroup, OnHit and Modifiers hold on its entity while the entity knows it.
