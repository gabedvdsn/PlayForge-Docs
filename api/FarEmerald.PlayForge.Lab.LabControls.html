Class LabControls

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab

FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.dll

A player's controls for one unit, as a MOBA's: right-click the ground to move, right-click a foe it can target to attack it, Q, W, E and R for its abilities past its attack, 1 to 6 for its items' abilities, S to stop, Escape to cancel a cast. An ability that needs a target waits for a left-click, as its own click targeting reads it; meanwhile a ring on the ground shows the ability's reach, another ability's key casts that one instead, and a right-click order lets go of it. It reads PlayForge's input, ForgeInput, so a test or a game's own input stands in, and gives its orders through a driver: its own, its unit's rules', or the lab's. The Creator Lab's take its controlled unit; the Demo's player is these, its hero's.

Guide: The Creator Lab

Named in: The Creator Lab: In code: Your unit in code
