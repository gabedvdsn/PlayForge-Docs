An entity's wait to revive, as its Respawn starts it: the time left, by the entity's own clock, and where it revives, at the end of the frame its time runs out. A revive before its time, as a buyback, ends the wait. A service of the entity, so a HUD reads it as gas.FindService<RespawnTimer>().
