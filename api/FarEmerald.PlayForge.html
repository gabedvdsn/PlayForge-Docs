Namespace FarEmerald.PlayForge

Classes

Ability

An ability: one thing an entity can do, as a fireball, a dash or a heal. An entity learns it from its Entity asset's Starting Abilities, from an equipped item's Active Ability or from code (AbilitySystemComponent.GiveAbility), and keeps it in a slot with a level of its own, between its StartingLevel and MaxLevel, kept under its asset tag.

An activation passes its caster's tag requirements and its Caster Checks (SourceActivationRules), lets its Behaviour's targeting task pick its target, which its Target Checks (TargetActivationRules) then check, applies its Cost and Cooldown effects to its caster and spends its AttributeCosts, as it starts or in the stage that takes them, and runs its stages of tasks. Its Definition says how it activates, its Tags what it is, grants and requires, and its Components add features with hooks into its life; its WorkerGroup, OnHit and Modifiers hold on its entity while the entity knows it.

AbilityActivationHandle

One activation of an ability as it runs, which its slot, an AbilitySpecContainer, keeps from its claim to its end: its Data, the compiled Proxy that runs it, the tokens that cancel its targeting and its stages, whether it's targeting or running its stages, and whether it has let its claim go. HandleId numbers it among its entity's activations, from 0.

AbilityCharges

An ability's charges on its caster: several uses, each spending a charge that comes back as the ability's Recharge says, by default on a cooldown of its own. An ability of 1 charge, with a recharge of PlayForge's own, is an ordinary cooldown. Its Charges component keeps them with the ability as its caster holds it, and is the ability's cooldown model through them.

AbilityDataPacket

Data packet for ability execution. Extends SequenceDataPacket so that abilities run through the TaskSequence system natively — the same packet flows through targeting, execution stages, callbacks, and ProcessControl.

IMPORTANT: Ability tasks must be stateless. All per-activation data flows through this packet, NOT as fields on the task itself.

AbilityDefinition

AbilityEffectUsage

AbilityInspector

BuildEffectSeries reads one effect across a range of levels, against a caster and a target where it's given them, and EvaluateAtLevel one number at a level; a number that reads what the read wasn't given, as the target, is marked dynamic.

AbilityLevelValidation

AbilityProxy

Bridge between ability activation and sequence-based execution.

AbilityProxy compiles the AbilityBehaviour into reusable TaskSequences at construction time — one for targeting and one for execution stages. Both phases register with ProcessControl for full visibility.

Since AbilityDataPacket extends SequenceDataPacket, the same packet flows through targeting, execution, callbacks, and ProcessControl.

Injection, callbacks, and usage effects are handled transparently:

  • Injections go to the compiled sequence, its callback heard either way
  • AbilitySystemCallbacks fire from sequence stage hooks
  • Usage effects apply at configured stage boundaries
AbilityReader

AbilitySave

AbilitySequenceCompiler

Compiles an AbilityBehaviour into a reusable TaskSequence.

Each AbilityTaskBehaviourStage becomes a SequenceStage with:

  • Ability tasks wrapped via AbilityTaskAdapter
  • Stage policy mapped from IAbilityProxyStagePolicy to ISequenceStagePolicy
  • Usage effects wired into stage OnTerminate callbacks
  • AbilitySystemCallbacks wired into stage event hooks

The compiled TaskSequence is reusable: each Run() creates a fresh TaskSequenceRuntime, so the immutable definition is safe to share.

IMPORTANT: Ability tasks must be stateless. All per-activation data flows through the AbilityDataPacket, not as fields on the task.

AbilitySpec

An ability as one entity knows it, made as the entity learns it: the Ability (Base) and its caster (Source), with its level kept on the caster under the ability's asset tag (GetLevel). It's the origin of the effects the ability applies, so they take its level and context tags, and an activation's data holds it as its EffectOrigin. It applies the ability's cost and cooldown (ApplyUsageEffects, PayCosts, SpendCooldown), reads the ability's numbers at its level (Read), finds its components (Find<T>), and keeps what they hold for this entity (ComponentStates) and its cooldown model (CooldownModel), which a HUD reads.

AbilitySpecContainer

One slot of an entity's ability system: the AbilitySpec it holds (Spec), its slot (Index), and the ability's activations as they run. It says whether the ability runs (IsActive), holds its claim (IsClaiming), is choosing its target (IsTargeting), is cooling down (IsCooldown) and which stage it's in (ActiveStageIndex); Cancel interrupts its runs, but one in a critical stage, and Inject sends an injection into each. AbilitySystemComponent.TryGetAbilityContainer finds one by slot or by ability.

AbilityStageCondition

AbilityTags

Names says whether a list of tags names an ability, its asset tag or a context tag being one of them or under one, as Cancelled By and Blocks name abilities.

AbilityTaskAdapter

Adapts an AbstractAbilityTask to the ISequenceTask interface.

Since AbilityDataPacket extends SequenceDataPacket directly, the adapter simply casts the incoming data to AbilityDataPacket and delegates to the underlying ability task.

IMPORTANT: Ability tasks must be stateless — all per-activation state must flow through the AbilityDataPacket, not as fields on the task. Shared [SerializeReference] instances are reused across activations.

AbilityTaskBehaviourStage

One of an ability's stages, which run one after another: the Tasks it starts together, and its Completion (StagePolicy), which ends it as its last task ends (All) or its first (Any), cancelling the rest. ApplyUsageEffects applies the ability's cost and cooldown in it, as it starts or, with ApplyUsageAtStageStart off, as it ends well, once an activation; its Condition skips it unless a tag is there, or missing; and its own CancelledBy and Blocks, where it names any, hold while it runs in place of the ability's. A stage that holds a critical task is critical (IsCritical): it commits the ability, so Cancelled By doesn't cancel it.

AbilityTrigger

What activates an ability on its own: a gameplay event sent to its entity, or a tag its entity gains. An ability activated only when code asked for it.

The core sends events of its own to an entity where an ability listens for them: IMPACT_DEALT and IMPACT_TAKEN as an impact lands between two entities, KILL to the killer and DIED to the one who died, and REVIVED. Their data hold the instigator as SOURCE, the entity it was done to as EVENT_SUBJECT, and an impact's ImpactData as EVENT_IMPACT. A trigger can take only some impacts, roll a chance, and target the instigator or the subject.

AbstractMonoProcess

The base of every MonoBehaviour process: a component, on a prefab or a scene object, that ProcessControl registers, steps and ends, as a GAS hub or a projectile. AbstractScaler

Base class for all magnitude scalers. Scalers determine how a value changes based on level (relative level = (level-1)/(maxLevel-1)).

CachedAttributeValue

Per-attribute runtime state under the two-kind impact model:

  • Modifiers (transient, derived) — registered via RegisterModifier(IAttributeImpactCarrier, IReadOnlyList<IAttributeImpacter>, IReadOnlyDictionary<IAttribute, CachedAttributeValue>) while their carrier lives, re-evaluated on every Recompute(IReadOnlyDictionary<IAttribute, CachedAttributeValue>), reversed exactly by UnregisterModifier(IAttributeImpactCarrier, IReadOnlyDictionary<IAttribute, CachedAttributeValue>).
  • Commits (permanent) — applied via CommitImpact(IAttributeImpactCarrier, IReadOnlyList<IAttributeImpacter>, IReadOnlyDictionary<IAttribute, CachedAttributeValue>); the post-bounds delta is recorded per retention group in FarEmerald.PlayForge.CachedAttributeValue.committedByGroup and NEVER reversed.

The formula (EFFECT_PIPELINE.md §2.1): ActiveValue = ConstrainValue( Override( FlatBonus( Multiply( Add( root ) ) ) + Σ committedByGroup ) ). Committed history lands where it was measured, after the % modifiers, so they don't scale it: 50 base × 1.5 aura + 10 tome = 85, and a 30 hit under the aura takes 30.

Reproducibility invariant: ActiveValue is always exactly what Recompute(IReadOnlyDictionary<IAttribute, CachedAttributeValue>) produces from current state — every mutation path ends in a recompute. There is no second bookkeeping system to drift.

[Serializable]
[NumberKind("Distance to Target", Reads = NumberReads.Caster | NumberReads.Target)]
[ForgeMenu("Space", Description = "How far the target is from the caster")]
public class DistanceToTarget : NumberKind
{
    [Tooltip("Along the ground, leaving height out")]
    public bool Flat = true;

    protected override float Evaluate(in NumberContext context)
    {
        var from = context.Caster.GetTargetingPacket().position;
        var to = context.Target.GetTargetingPacket().position;
        if (Flat) from.y = to.y = 0f;
        return Vector3.Distance(from, to);
    }

    public override string Describe() => "the distance to the target";
}
