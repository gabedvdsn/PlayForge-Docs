Which retention groups' parts an attribute read takes. An attribute's value is the sum of its groups' parts: the base its set gives, what items add in their group, what a buff adds in its. × Attribute, an Attribute condition, a Math worker's Relative To and a Threshold worker read the whole value unless they say otherwise: only these groups, as a bonus of the items' strength reads their group; or all but these, as a bonus of a hero's own damage leaves its items' out. A group names the groups under it too. Group Impact reads one group's part, as it did.
