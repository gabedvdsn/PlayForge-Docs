Callbacks fired by ProcessControl for high-level observers (debuggers, recorders, GAS-aware observers, etc.).

These are in addition to the per-process ProcessWatcher system — watchers subscribe to a specific ProcessRelay, whereas callbacks fire for every process managed by ProcessControl.
