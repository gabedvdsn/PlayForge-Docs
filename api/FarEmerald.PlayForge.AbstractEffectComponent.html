Lend(EffectComponentContext)

As a lasting effect starts to last on the one it's on, and lends what it gives while it lasts, as an aura: as it lands, before its Land; as it takes the place of another effect under its tag; and as a load or a move puts it back, where it doesn't land again. Each is matched by a TakeBack(EffectComponentContext). An instant effect lends nothing.
