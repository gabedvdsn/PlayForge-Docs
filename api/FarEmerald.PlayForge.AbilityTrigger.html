What activates an ability on its own: a gameplay event sent to its entity, or a tag its entity gains. An ability activated only when code asked for it.

The core sends events of its own to an entity where an ability listens for them: IMPACT_DEALT and IMPACT_TAKEN as an impact lands between two entities, KILL to the killer and DIED to the one who died, and REVIVED. Their data hold the instigator as SOURCE, the entity it was done to as EVENT_SUBJECT, and an impact's ImpactData as EVENT_IMPACT. A trigger can take only some impacts, roll a chance, and target the instigator or the subject.
