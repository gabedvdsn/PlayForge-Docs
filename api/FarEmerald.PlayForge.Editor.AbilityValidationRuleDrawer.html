An ability's validation rule: its kind, as its [ForgeMenu] names it or named without "ValidationRule" or "Validation", then the kind's own fields. A rule is always of some kind, so the picker offers no None. Each list is offered the kinds of its side, the Caster Checks a caster's and the Target Checks a target's, with those of either; a check a component stands behind, as Check Range, is offered to neither, as it comes with its component.
