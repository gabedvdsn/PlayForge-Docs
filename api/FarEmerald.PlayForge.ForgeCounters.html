PlayForge's counters in Unity's Profiler, in its PlayForge category, which the PlayForge Processes and PlayForge GAS modules chart. A frame's counts start from 0 each frame, and count every world's work, a headless run's within the frame among them. A level holds until it changes: the scene's ProcessControl gives the process levels as its LateUpdate loop ends, and the others follow what starts and ends. Unity compiles them out of release builds, so they count in the editor and development builds. A ProfilerRecorder reads one by its name in the category, as "Effects Applied". The markers time PlayForge's work; these say how much of it there is.
