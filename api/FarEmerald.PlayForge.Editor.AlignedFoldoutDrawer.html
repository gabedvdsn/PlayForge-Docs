Each class PlayForge serializes in a field with no drawer of its own, drawn as a foldout of its fields, its arrow in line with the rows around it, as a list's is: an attribute read's retention groups, as an × Attribute scaler's Groups, a turn to face's settings, an asset's definition and tags, a list's entries. Unity hangs an inspector foldout's arrow 12 px into the margin, to the left of a list's arrow and, in a scaler's block, out to the block's edge; this one keeps the lists' column, as pf-foldout--aligned sets in PlayForge.uss. A class opts in with a CustomPropertyDrawer of its type here. A field marked ForgeShowWhenAttribute shows only while the field it names says so, as a read's Groups shows only for the reads that name groups. An inspector drawn with IMGUI gets the same foldout.
