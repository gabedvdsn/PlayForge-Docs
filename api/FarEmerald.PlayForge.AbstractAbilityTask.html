The base of a task in an ability's stage, as Apply Effects, Delay or Run Sequence: a stage starts its tasks together and ends as its Completion says, as its last task's Activate ends or its first's. A task of your own derives from it and overrides Activate, the work, and IsCriticalSection, with Prepare and Clean for what it does as its stage starts and ends; [ForgeMenu] files it in the stage's task picker. What it keeps for one activation goes in data.GetTaskState<T>(this), as one task serves every activation of its ability.
