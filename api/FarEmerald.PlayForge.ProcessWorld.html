The processes of one world and how they're stepped: every process's block, its step groups, the watches, the groups, which processes each GAS owns, the control state and its transitions, and the callbacks. A scene's ProcessControl wraps one and steps it from Unity's loop, and code can own one and step it itself, as a headless run does. It was ProcessControl itself, so no process ran without a ProcessControl in a scene.

Step()

Steps every process the world holds once, as a frame does: Update, then LateUpdate. A world of its own steps by Unity's frame time; a scene's, by the clock read now, as its ProcessControl steps it from Unity's loop.

public void Step()

Step(float)

Steps a world of its own by a time its owner gives: its clock reads that time, its tick goes on, so what waits on it goes on, and every process steps once, Update and then LateUpdate. A loop of steps runs the world through its time within one frame, as a headless run does; its waits were Unity's next frame. A scene's world steps with Unity's loop, so this steps it once by the clock read now.
