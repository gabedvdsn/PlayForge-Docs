Which value stands where several claim one result: the highest (UseMaximum), the lowest, their average, the first or the last, none of them (Ignore), or their sum (Combine). A Group's policy picks among its overriding members, Ignore setting them aside so the rest join as they would; an attribute set's collision settings pick among the elements that give one attribute, Ignore keeping the first.
