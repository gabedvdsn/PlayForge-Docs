An ability's Tags: its AssetTag, made from its Name, which its entity carries while it knows the ability and keeps the ability's level under; its ContextTags, which describe it and which the changes its effects make carry as context; its Passive Tags (PassiveGrantedTags), on its entity while it knows the ability, and its Active Tags (ActiveGrantedTags), while an activation runs; and its TagRequirements of its caster, to activate and while it runs, and of its target. Names says whether a list of tags names an ability, its asset tag or a context tag being one of them or under one, as Cancelled By and Blocks name abilities.
