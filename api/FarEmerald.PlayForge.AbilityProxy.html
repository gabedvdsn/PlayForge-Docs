Bridge between ability activation and sequence-based execution.

AbilityProxy compiles the AbilityBehaviour into reusable TaskSequences at construction time — one for targeting and one for execution stages. Both phases register with ProcessControl for full visibility.

Since AbilityDataPacket extends SequenceDataPacket, the same packet flows through targeting, execution, callbacks, and ProcessControl.

Injection, callbacks, and usage effects are handled transparently:

  • Injections go to the compiled sequence, its callback heard either way
  • AbilitySystemCallbacks fire from sequence stage hooks
  • Usage effects apply at configured stage boundaries
