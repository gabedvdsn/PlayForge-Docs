Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.Editor

FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.Editor.dll

The library in the editor, by kind: the active Forge Project's assets, as The Forge's pickers offer them, or every asset of the project's own folders while it has no Forge Project; it switches the active project, as The Forge does, and follows as The Forge switches it. Given folders, it plays those folders' assets alone. Its prefabs are those of the project's own folders with a GameplayAbilitySystem whose Entity Data is set: of the active project's, those in its folders, and those in no project's whose entity is the active project's or no project's. It keeps the lab's scenarios as assets: in the active project's Lab Scenarios folder, or its first folder's.
