The library in the editor, by kind: the active Forge Project's assets, as The Forge's pickers offer them, or every asset of the project's own folders while it has no Forge Project; it switches the active project, as The Forge does, and follows as The Forge switches it. Given folders, it plays those folders' assets alone.
