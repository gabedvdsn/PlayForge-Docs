FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab

The Creator Lab: a space where an author puts units down, gives them their project's abilities, effects and items, casts, and watches the numbers move. It builds what it needs as it wakes: PlayForge, where the scene has none; the ground, a light and its camera; a world of its own, which it steps by its own time; its own caster, a neutral source for what the author drops; and its panels.

Window → PlayForge → Creator Lab opens it in a new scene. A test makes one with Create(ILabLibrary, LabRules, bool) and drives it through the same calls the panels make.
