An item's Tags: its AssetTag, made from its Name, which its holder carries while the item is in its inventory, worn or not, and keeps the item's level under; its ContextTags, which the changes its effects make carry as context; and its Granted Tags (PassiveGrantedTags), on its holder while it's equipped.
