Class ProcessControl

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The scene's scheduler of processes: every process, a GAS, a projectile, an ability's sequence, registers with it, and it steps each from Unity's player loop, in Update, LateUpdate or FixedUpdate as the process's timing asks, moves it between its states as its lifecycle says, and ends it. A Bootstrapper brings it up as the one Instance, and it then registers the scene's own mono processes, as its Collect On Awake says.

ProcessControl.Register(process).HandledBy(handler).With(data).Run() registers a process and starts it, and its result holds the process's relay, which pauses, resumes and ends it; RegisterGroup and RegisterWithGroup hold runtime processes in a group by a tag. Its control state (SetControlState) pauses, closes or ends every process at once; its Fixed Tick Rate, above 0, steps every process at fixed steps of game time, so a run comes out the same at any frame rate; and its Callbacks hear every process register, change state and go. Its bookkeeping is its World, a ProcessWorld, which code can also own and step with no scene.
