Class StageBuilder

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

Builds one stage of a sequence, handed to the action given to a builder's Stage, a stage's SubStage, or a branch's If and Default: its tasks (Task, Do, SyncTask, the delays and waits) and sub-stages, its policy (WhenAll, WhenAny, WhenN), its repeats, time limits and conditions, the injections it allows, whether it's critical, its name, and its handlers, OnInit, OnRepeat, OnTimeout, OnTerminate and the rest.

WithRepeat(int)

Runs this stage the given number of times in all, then moves on: 3 runs it three times, repeating it twice. A break, a skip or an interrupt still ends it sooner, and its OnRepeat hears each repeat, not the last run. With WithRepeatAndDelay(float?), the count holds as the policy repeats it.
