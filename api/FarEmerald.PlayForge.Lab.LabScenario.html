Class LabScenario

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab

FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.dll

A setup of the Creator Lab, saved to test again: each unit put down with what it was given, the lab's mode and battle, its camera and time, its stops and watches, and a line saying what it's for. The lab saves one as an asset in the active Forge Project's Lab Scenarios folder, lists them in its Scenarios section, and loads one there, over the space as it is.
