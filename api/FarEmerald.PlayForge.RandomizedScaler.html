Adds random variance to level-based scaling. Useful for damage ranges, proc chances, etc. Its variance is a class: by a share of its value, by an amount, between two multipliers, or a way of your own, as dice. A variance's numbers are of any kind, read with the variance as their owner, so a share can widen by level and a Max multiplier can be 3 × its Min.
