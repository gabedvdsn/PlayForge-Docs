What an effect can land on: an entity, with its sides, its tags and a definition to show. It makes, applies and removes effects (GenerateEffectSpec, ApplyGameplayEffect, RemoveGameplayEffect), reads and changes its attributes, reads its levels, finds its subsystems, and says whether it's dead. Every GAS hub is one, and so is a stand-in, as the placeholder a disjointed shot is aimed at.
