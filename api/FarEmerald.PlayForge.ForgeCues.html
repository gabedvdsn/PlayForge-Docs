Where every cue plays, whatever raised it: an effect's, an ability's, an entity's, an item's, or a game's own code, as sparks at a ray's hit. A handler that throws is reported and the rest play on: what something shows mustn't stop what it does. And what PlayForge's cues reach in the game: the camera shaker the game gives.
