An effect's impact section: its first impact, the attribute it changes, the operation and the value it changes, its magnitude, pipeline and impact types, whose delivery the duration gives it; its Additional Impacts, each with a delivery of its own; its affiliation check, its chance to apply and its contained effects. On every GameplayEffect.Generate, RebuildEntries derives the first impact's entry, which is never saved, and appends the additional ones after it: Entries is what an application commits and registers.

ReverseImpactOnRemoval

Drives both the retention rule and the per-tick rollback path:

  • For Current-only impacters, set true to be retained as a sustained modifier (auto-reverses on removal via Recompute).
  • For periodic-tick contributions committed past the impacter list, set true to enqueue a synthetic rollback carrier on container removal that subtracts TrackedImpact from the attribute's one-shot history.
  • Redundant for Base-touching impacters (Base contributions are always retained). The drawer flags this with a warning.
  • Meaningless for instant-policy effects (no removal phase). Drawer hides.
