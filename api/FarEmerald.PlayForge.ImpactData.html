A change as it landed on an attribute: the Target it landed on, the Attribute, the SourcedModifier that made it, whose carrier names its source and its effect, the RealImpact, what the attribute's Current and Base changed by after workers, bounds and rounding, and the OldValue before it. Attribute callbacks, impact workers, an effect's Impact hook and the frame summary are each handed one; Ends marks a lasting modifier coming off, a change but no hit.
