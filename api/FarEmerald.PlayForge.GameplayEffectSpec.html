One application of a Gameplay Effect: the effect, its origin, its source and its target, as the target's GenerateEffectSpec makes it and ApplyGameplayEffect lands it. It keeps what belongs to that application alone: magnitudes set by its caller, where it hit (WithHit), what modifiers changed of it, its workers' and components' state, what it has dealt, and, while it lasts, the container that holds it on the shelf. Its impacters, built once from its effect's entries, are what the attribute system evaluates for each delivery.
