Guide: Abilities

public enum EActivationStatus

Fields

Refused = 0

It was refused. Refusal says why, as OnAbilityRefused tells it.

Started = 1

It started: its claim, its targeting and its first stage ran inside the call until one of its tasks waited, so an ability whose tasks all finish at once has ended by now.

Buffered = 2

It waits in the input buffer, as its Queue policy lets it, until the claim or the block that holds it lifts. It starts then, or is let go as its window runs out.

NextFrame = 3

It was asked for while its own activation call still ran, from its own callbacks or from another ability's that it started, so it starts in the next frame instead. If it's refused then, OnAbilityRefused tells it.
