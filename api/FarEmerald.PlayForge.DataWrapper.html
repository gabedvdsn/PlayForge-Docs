One entry of an asset's or an entity's Local Data: a value under a key. Its value is a class, a kind of its own: a number that can scale, a whole number, a string, a bool, a tag, an attribute, an effect, an ability, an entity, an object, a MonoBehaviour or a ScriptableObject; or a kind of your own, as a point or a colour, a [Serializable] class made from LocalDataValue<T> or LocalDataValue, which is offered beside them. Each entry saves its own value alone.
