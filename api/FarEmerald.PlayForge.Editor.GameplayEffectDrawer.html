FarEmerald.PlayForge.Editor.dll

A reference to an effect: Unity's object field, with Open or New beside it, and under it a line that says how the effect takes its levels, with Link to make it take them from the asset that holds the field. Its right-click menu holds the field's template, a pick of the effects nothing links, and New Linked Effect. Setting the field asked in a dialog whether to link the effect, and the template, the link filter and a create button sat beside the field as tinted buttons.
