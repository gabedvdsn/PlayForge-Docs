Namespace

A request to activate the ability in one slot of an entity's ability system, which CreateActivationRequest makes and TryActivateAbility takes: its slot (Index); the When Busy it's asked under (Policy), as its ability says unless the request names another; the Data it hands the activation, as a target an AI picked or set-by-caller magnitudes; how long it may wait in the input buffer (BufferWindow), below 0 for its ability's Buffer Window; and whether an ability still choosing its target gives way to it (CancelsTargeting), on as a player's press is, and off for a trigger's request and Activate At Start's.
