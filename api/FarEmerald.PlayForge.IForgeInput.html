Interface IForgeInput

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

What click targeting reads of the player's input: whether a pointer's button went down this frame, and the ray the pointer aims along. The defaults read a mouse, a pen or a touch through a camera; a game with a gamepad's reticle, a controller's ray or input of its own puts its own in with Use(IForgeInput). The Creator Lab and the task sequence libraries' waits read the keys and the pointer through it too.
