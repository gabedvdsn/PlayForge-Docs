Class AttributeChangeEffectWorker

Namespace

Answers how far an attribute has moved since its effect began, for a number of the Worker kind to read: how much health the target has lost since the curse landed, from every source. It notes the attribute as each application of its effect begins, and answers under its Key: the amount lost, gained or changed, or the attribute as it was.

With EveryTicks above 0, it answers only on each Nth tick of its effect, and 0 on the others, so an impact that reads it lands every Nth tick: a curse dealing 5 a second, and every fourth second a quarter of what its target has lost since it began, is one periodic effect with a second impact of −0.25 × this worker's answer. Its tick is counted before the tick's impacts are worked out, so on its Nth tick the amount lost is the amount before that tick lands.
