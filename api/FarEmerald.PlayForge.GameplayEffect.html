A Gameplay Effect asset: a change to an entity, as damage, a heal, a timed buff, a poison, a cost or a cooldown. Abilities, items and code apply it to a target: Generate, through the target's GenerateEffectSpec, makes a GameplayEffectSpec for one application, which ApplyGameplayEffect lands.

Its duration's Policy decides how long it lives: an Instant effect makes its change once, and a Durational or Infinite one goes on the target's effect shelf until its time runs out or something removes it. Its impacts change attributes, each a commit that stays or a modifier reversed as the effect ends; it can also grant tags and abilities, apply contained effects, run workers and carry components, and its requirements and conditions say whether it lands, goes on and comes off. It has no level of its own: it takes its origin's.
