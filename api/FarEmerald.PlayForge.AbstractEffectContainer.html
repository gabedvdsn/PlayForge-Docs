ReverseImpact()

Reverse this container's spec contribution: unregister its Continuous entries through the attribute pipeline (workers, callbacks and the regulation refresh all observe the removal). Called by OnRemove() and by the ReplaceOldContainer re-application path.

Commits (OnApply / Periodic entries) are permanent by design — there is no rollback path in the two-kind impact model.

TickIsDue(double, float, double)

Whether a tick falls due this frame. A duration's last tick falls due as the duration ends, and the two count down apart, so rounding could end the duration a hair before the tick, which then never landed. A tick that near due on the duration's last frame is that tick.
