Respawn: revives an entity that stays as it dies a while after, in its own time, at its home, where it died, a point, or a place of your own, as a hero comes back at its fountain. Its Death's On Revive effects land as it does, as any revive's do; a revive before its time, as a buyback, ends the wait. The wait is its entity's RespawnTimer, a service a HUD can read. An entity that ends as it dies has nothing to revive, which the Problems view says. An entity holds one at most.
