Class AbstractRuntimeProcess

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The base of every runtime process: a plain C# class, with no GameObject, that ProcessControl registers, steps and ends, as a runtime GAS, a task sequence's process or a group's member. Its name, step priority, step timing and lifecycle go to its base constructor; the one with none gives its type's name, the first priority, no step timing and SelfTerminating. Only a runtime process joins a group.

Derive from it, or from LazyRuntimeProcess for a run that lasts until it's paused or ended, and override RunProcess, the run ProcessControl awaits, and the hooks you need: WhenInitialize; WhenUpdate, WhenLateUpdate and WhenFixedUpdate at its timing; WhenWait and WhenTerminate; and TryHandlePause and TryHandleResume to keep a run through a pause. An override of WhenInitialize or WhenTerminate calls the base's: the process is IsInitialized from registration until it ends, so it can be registered again once ended, and what it handles ends with it.
