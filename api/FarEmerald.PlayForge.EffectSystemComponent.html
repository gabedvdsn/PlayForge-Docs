The effect shelf — apply / tick / re-apply / remove for gameplay effects, shared by BOTH RuntimeGameplayAbilitySystem and the MonoBehaviour GameplayAbilitySystem (the twin-drift class of bugs ends here; the GAS classes keep only thin delegating wrappers).

Lifecycle under the two-kind impact model:

Apply (durational):  shelve container
                     → RegisterModifier per Continuous entry
                     → app workers
                     → commit OnApply entries; TickOnApplication? then Periodic ones once
Tick:                commit Periodic entries per execute tick
Remove:              container.OnRemove() → UnregisterModifier per Continuous entry.
                     Commits are permanent — there is no rollback path.
Instant:             commit OnApply entries — no shelf, no removal phase.

MoveTo(GameplayEffect, IGameplayAbilitySystem)

Moves each of an effect's containers on this shelf onto another entity's, with its stacks and its time left, as a possession hands a body's effects over. A move is neither a removal nor an application: nothing of either runs, its workers, its commits or its contained effects, and neither is told. Its lasting modifiers leave this entity and hold on the other, its lasting cues end here and begin there, the abilities it lends go with it, and both entities' tags are compiled again. Each keeps what applied it. Taking an effect off and applying it to the other started its time again and ran its application twice. Returns how many moved.
