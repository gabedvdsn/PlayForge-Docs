Class AbstractMonoProcess

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The base of every MonoBehaviour process: a component, on a prefab or a scene object, that ProcessControl registers, steps and ends, as a GAS hub or a projectile. Its lifecycle, step timing and step priority are fields of its inspector, Synchronous in Update and LateUpdate at the first priority by default, and as it initializes it takes the parent, position and rotation its packet gives. ProcessControl registers those already in a scene as it starts.

Derive from it, or from LazyMonoProcess for a run that lasts until it's paused or ended, and override RunProcess, the run ProcessControl awaits, and the hooks you need: WhenInitialize; WhenUpdate, WhenLateUpdate and WhenFixedUpdate at its timing; WhenWait, WhenTerminate and WhenDestroy; and TryHandlePause and TryHandleResume to keep a run through a pause. An override of WhenInitialize or WhenTerminate calls the base's, and WhenDestroy stands in for OnDestroy, which a class of its own would hide.

Its object comes from its registration's instantiator, its handler's or its own Instantiator, or is cloned from its prefab, and is given back or destroyed as the process ends. Processes on its child objects pause and end with it, as ProcessControl cascades by default.
