A component of a PlayForge asset: a feature it carries, added in its Components section, a class with hooks into its asset's life, each a no-op a kind may override. An effect's, an ability's, an entity's and an item's components each hear their own asset's moments, beside its workers. A component is its asset's, shared by every entity the asset reaches, so what it keeps for one of them it keeps through its context's state, never on itself.
