Class FloatingTextCue

Namespace

FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

Floats a text where its placement says, above the one the cue is on by default: a hit's damage from an effect's On Hit, the gold a kill gave, a status's name as it lands. It says its Text, where {value} is its number, written as its Value says, {name} the name of what raised it, as an effect's, and {tag} its cue tag; it moves, fades and scales as its Motion says, looks as its Look says, and merges, staggers and caps as its Stacking says. Its texts come from ForgeCuePool and go back to it.
