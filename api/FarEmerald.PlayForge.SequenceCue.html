Namespace

Runs a task sequence on a data packet loaded for the cue (CuePackets), its Data list last, so the sequence reads the cue's entity, its asset and its numbers: a burst's runs its course, and one in a list of lasting cues runs until its cue ends, as a shield's flicker that stops as the shield goes. It runs in the world of the one it plays on, by the clock its Clock picks, else the sequence's own, else its owner's.
