TryActivateAbility(AbilityActivationRequest)

Activates the ability the request names, if it may run. The ability runs inside this call until one of its tasks waits: its claim, its targeting and its first stage. So an instant ability has ended, and its callbacks have fired, by the time this returns. An ability asked for again while its own activation call still runs, from its own callbacks or from another ability's that it started, starts next frame instead, so a loop of instant activations goes round once a frame.

public ActivationResult TryActivateAbility(AbilitySystemComponent.AbilityActivationRequest req)

req AbilitySystemComponent.AbilityActivationRequest

ActivationResult

What came of it: it started, waits in the input buffer, starts in the next frame, or was refused, and why. It reads as true where it wasn't refused.
