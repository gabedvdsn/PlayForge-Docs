What works out a number for a carrier, an effect's spec or another: Initialize as the carrier is made, which can capture what the number needs, then Evaluate each time it's read. Every AbstractScaler is one, and a carrier keeps what a scaler captures under the scaler. Generate makes a CustomScaler from two delegates, or, given none, one that reads how far up its range the origin's level is, from 0 to 1.
