Interface IForgeTurner

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

A game's own turner, on an entity's object: it turns its character itself, by its animation or its controller, as each step of a turn PlayForge orders asks, in place of PlayForge, which sets the entity's facing straight. PlayForge reads how far is left to turn from the entity's facing, its object's rotation, so that's what the turner turns.
