A number's read, explained: a tree of its parts, each with what it read and what it gave. A charged hit reads −180 because its Group adds its caster's Attack Damage, 100, to a Conditional that gives 80, as the caster's Combo Charges, 4, are above 0. A part whose context lacks what it reads says what it needs, "the target", and gives its fallback, so a read in the editor with no entities says where it would change in play.

Each part reads as the number does, through the same context, so the parts and the read agree. A read that rolls, as a Randomized scaler's, rolls again for its parts. A trace allocates, and is for the editor, tools and tests.

A kind or a condition of a project's own explains its parts by overriding Explain(in NumberContext, NumberTrace) or Explain(in NumberContext, NumberTrace) and adding them: trace.Add(Min, context, this, "Min").
