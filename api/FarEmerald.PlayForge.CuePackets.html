The data a cue's process or sequence runs with, loaded for what raised it. An ability's cue starts from its activation's packet: its targets, its targeting data and the numbers its caller set. On it go the cue itself (CUE), its asset (ASSET) and its spec (SPEC), its source, the one it plays on as its target where the packet names none, where it plays, and its owner's clock; then the asset's Local Data, its numbers read for the cue, as at its level; then the cue's own Data list. Each step's keys take the place of an earlier step's, so the cue's own data has the last word.
