A process's registration with ProcessControl, built a step at a time: what handles the process, the packet it carries, the ProcessControl that takes it, and, for a MonoBehaviour process, what makes its object. Register(AbstractRuntimeProcess) and its siblings start one, and Run() registers the process and starts it, as registering always has:

var result = ProcessControl.Register(projectile).HandledBy(gas).With(data).Run();
if (!result) Debug.Log(result.Refusal);

What isn't given takes its default. The handler follows the packet: one with a source, as an ability's is, hands the process to its source (Source), and any other to ProcessControl's default handler, the GameRoot where there is one, or to none in a world of its own, which reaches for nothing of the scene's. ProcessControl had 28 static Register overloads, and the argument's static type picked the handler, so an ability's packet passed as a SequenceDataPacket filed its sequence under GameRoot, and the sequence didn't end with its entity.
