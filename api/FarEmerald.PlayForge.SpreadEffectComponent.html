Spread: spreads its effect, or another it names, from the one it's on to those near them, at the moments it names: as it lands, each tick, as it ends, or as the one it's on dies, as a plague jumps from its victim to the enemies near it. Each it takes gets the effect from the same origin, its caster's, so its modifiers and its chance to land are its own. A landing by spread spreads on only so far as its Hops allow.
