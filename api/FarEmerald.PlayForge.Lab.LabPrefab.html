FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab

A prefab as the lab's library lists it, beside the project's PlayForge assets: one with a GameplayAbilitySystem whose Entity Data is set, as a game's character is. Dropped on the ground, put down, or seated in a battle, it stands as a unit of the lab's: an instance of the prefab, its own body, its GAS registered in the lab's world on the side given. Every lab feature works on it as on the lab's own units: it's selected and dragged, takes orders and casts, changes side, takes what's dropped on it, shows its reach, and keeps its place in a scenario.
