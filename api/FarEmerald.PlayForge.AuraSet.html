Class AuraSet

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

An entity's running auras: each checks who's near at its interval, by its holder's time, holds its effect on each it takes, from an origin of its own, and takes it off as they leave, die or end, as its holder dies, or as the aura goes. Two of one aura, as two of an item, are two runs, each holding its own. It's a service of its entity, gas.GetService<AuraSet>(), which the entity steps by its own time and ends as the entity ends.
