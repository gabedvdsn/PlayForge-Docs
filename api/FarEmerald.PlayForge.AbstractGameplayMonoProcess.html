Class AbstractGameplayMonoProcess

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

A mono process that takes part in gameplay, as a projectile an ability spawns. It knows its origin, the ability or entity that spawned it, and it carries attributes of its own: a projectile's speed is the projectile's, set on its prefab, by the level of what spawned it, or from the caster, and its code can change it as it flies. A role, as ProjectileSpeed, names the attribute a process reads for a purpose.

Guide: Processes

Named in: Getting Started: Quickstart: a damage ability in ~10 minutes: Running a process or a sequence

GiveMagnitudes(GameplayEffectSpec)

Gives an effect spec the process applies the set-by-caller magnitudes it should read: those of the data that spawned it, then each of its own attributes under its role, so an effect scales by what the process carries, as a fire's burn by its heat. Its own win, as the nearer. Only its caller's reached its effects, so what it carried stopped at the process.
