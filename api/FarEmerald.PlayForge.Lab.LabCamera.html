Class LabCamera

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab
Assembly
FarEmerald.PlayForge.Lab.dll

The lab's camera: it looks down at an angle at a point on the ground, from the distance and tilt it's set to. Scroll pulls it back, a middle drag pans it, and ResetView() puts it back as it started. It follows a unit when given one, as the Demo's follows its hero, and shakes as a cue asks, as the game's camera shaker PlayForge's cues reach through ForgeCues.

Guide: The Creator Lab

[RequireComponent(typeof(Camera))]
