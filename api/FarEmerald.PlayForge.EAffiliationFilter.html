Enum EAffiliationFilter

Namespace

Whom a search or a check takes, by how each stands to its caster, or to an aura's holder: an enemy shares none of its sides, an ally shares one, and the caster itself is an ally of itself and no one's enemy. The one choice an Area Effect, an effect's area, a spread, an aura, the auto-target tasks and an Affiliation check make. A caster with no sides takes every other one as its ally and its enemy alike.
