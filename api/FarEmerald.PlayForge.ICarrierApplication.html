Interface ICarrierApplication

Namespace
FarEmerald.PlayForge

FarEmerald.PlayForge.dll

The "applicable" facet of a carrier — duration, container generation, contained effects, worker phases, validation. ITarget exposes its application surface against this interface (not against a concrete GameplayEffectSpec) so any future carrier kind that participates in the apply-shelf pipeline can plug in without subclassing the spec.

Plain IAttributeImpactCarriers — synthetic deltas, leveler snapshots, nullified markers — intentionally do NOT implement this interface. They are identity + magnitude only; forcing them to expose duration policy or worker hooks would be lying about their lifecycle.
